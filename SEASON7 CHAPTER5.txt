# VOID WALKER — SEASON 7

## CHAPTER 5: THE VOICE CALLED "BUDDY" (PART 1)

Darkness surrounded Harris.

Not the darkness of fear.

Not the darkness of the Void.

A calm darkness.

A peaceful darkness.

The kind of darkness that existed before a new beginning.

For a few moments, Harris simply stood there.

Silent.

The warmth he had felt inside his chest continued growing.

Slowly.

Steadily.

Like a heartbeat that had been asleep for years.

Then—

The voice spoke again.

"Harris."

The moment he heard it, his eyes widened.

Unlike the Creator.

Unlike the Third Voice.

This voice felt different.

Warmer.

Closer.

Almost like it had been beside him his entire life.

A smile immediately appeared on Harris's face.

For the first time in many days—

He genuinely looked happy.

"So you finally talked."

The voice remained silent for a second.

Then replied.

"You are surprisingly excited."

"Of course."

"Why?"

"Because everyone keeps speaking in riddles."

The voice laughed softly.

The sound echoed through the darkness.

It was the first time Harris had heard it laugh.

And strangely...

It sounded familiar.

Almost like hearing himself laugh.

Harris crossed his arms.

"Okay."

"Okay?"

"How should I call you?"

The voice seemed surprised.

"You want to give me a name?"

"Yes."

"Why?"

"Because calling you 'The Divine Suit Entity Living Inside My Soul' sounds too long."

Silence.

Then laughter.

Actual laughter.

The voice laughed harder than before.

For a moment even Harris started laughing.

The atmosphere became unexpectedly relaxed.

Finally the voice replied.

"You can call me anything."

Harris immediately answered.

"Buddy."

Silence.

The voice paused.

Almost like it wasn't expecting that answer.

"Buddy?"

"Yeah."

"Why Buddy?"

"Because you're helping me."

The darkness became quiet.

The voice didn't answer immediately.

And for some reason—

That silence felt emotional.

Finally the voice replied.

"Okay."

A huge smile appeared on Harris's face.

"Then Buddy it is."

The voice sighed.

"You really are strange."

"Thank you."

"That wasn't a compliment."

"I know."

The voice laughed again.

And for the first time—

Harris felt like he had gained another companion.

Not a weapon.

Not power.

A friend.

A buddy.

Someone he could talk to.

Someone who wasn't hiding behind mysterious clues every five minutes.

At least...

That was what he hoped.

Then Harris suddenly pointed forward dramatically.

"Buddy."

"Yes?"

"Are you ready?"

The voice became confused.

"For what?"

Harris's eyes sparkled.

"Training."

Silence.

Absolute silence.

Then—

"Training?"

"Yes."

"You just met me."

"Yes."

"You're severely injured."

"Yes."

"You nearly died."

"Yes."

"Your body is barely functioning."

"Yes."

"You are physically damaged."

"Yes."

"You are mentally damaged."

"Yes."

"You are emotionally unstable."

"Maybe."

"HARRIS."

"Okay yes."

The voice sounded exhausted already.

Like it had just inherited the world's biggest problem.

Finally Buddy spoke seriously.

"Harris."

"What?"

"You are willing to sacrifice yourself far too easily."

The smile on Harris's face weakened.

Only slightly.

But Buddy noticed.

The voice became softer.

"You don't value your own life enough."

For the first time—

Harris didn't joke.

Didn't laugh.

Didn't argue.

Because deep down—

He knew it was true.

After the war.

After losing his powers.

After seeing the future threat.

After hearing about Omega Void Emperor.

Something inside him had changed.

He wasn't afraid of dying anymore.

Not because he was brave.

Because he believed protecting everyone mattered more.

Buddy sighed.

Then finally spoke.

"We'll help you."

Harris immediately looked up.

"Really?"

"Yes."

A huge smile appeared.

Then Buddy continued.

"But under one condition."

The smile disappeared.

Immediately.

Because conditions always caused problems.

Harris folded his arms.

"What condition?"

"You must not leave your friends behind."

Silence.

The words echoed throughout the darkness.

Harris blinked.

Then frowned.

Then blinked again.

"What?"

"You heard me."

"That's your condition?"

"Yes."

The answer came immediately.

Without hesitation.

Without doubt.

Harris stared.

Completely confused.

"Why?"

Buddy remained silent.

Several moments passed.

Then Harris asked again.

"Why?"

Still silence.

The warmth in his chest continued beating.

But no answer came.

Harris's expression slowly changed.

From confusion.

To frustration.

Then anger.

"Tell me the reason."

"I can't."

"Why?"

"I simply can't."

The silence returned.

And that silence only made Harris angrier.

Because he already knew what he was planning.

Leave alone.

Find the books.

Face Omega Void Emperor.

Protect everyone from a distance.

If nobody followed him—

Nobody would get hurt.

At least that was what he believed.

Then Buddy spoke again.

"If you accept this condition, I'll help you."

Harris clenched his fists.

The darkness around him trembled slightly.

His voice became cold.

"No."

Buddy froze.

"What?"

"No."

The answer was immediate.

Firm.

Determined.

Harris looked directly into the darkness.

"If gaining this power means pulling my friends into danger..."

His eyes sharpened.

"...then I don't need this power."

The words echoed endlessly.

Even Buddy became silent.

For several long moments.

Nothing moved.

Nothing spoke.

Nothing existed except those words.

Then—

The darkness slowly disappeared.

Harris's consciousness returned.

His eyes slowly opened.

Night still surrounded the campsite.

The stars remained overhead.

The campfire had long since gone out.

Everything was quiet.

Everything was peaceful.

Yet Harris felt completely restless.

Then he noticed something.

Harlina.

She was still hugging him.

Tightly.

Like she was afraid to let go.

Her face rested against his shoulder.

Even while sleeping—

Tears remained at the corners of her eyes.

And then—

She whispered again.

"D-Don't leave me alone..."

The words struck harder than any attack.

Harder than any arrow.

Harder than any wound.

Harris froze.

His chest hurt.

Not physically.

Emotionally.

His eyes slowly widened.

Then—

For the first time since learning about Omega Void Emperor—

Tears appeared.

Real tears.

Silent tears.

He carefully touched Harlina's hair.

Then gently kissed her forehead.

"I'm sorry..."

His voice barely escaped.

"I'm sorry..."

Another tear fell.

Because for the first time—

He was beginning to understand something.

Everyone was afraid.

Not only him.

Not only the Creator.

Not only Buddy.

Everyone.

And that realization hurt.

A lot.

Yet despite that—

His determination remained unchanged.

The books.

The threat.

The war.

He still needed answers.

No matter what.

Slowly he closed his eyes again.

Then called out.

"Creator."

Silence.

"Creator."

Nothing.

"Third Voice."

No answer.

His expression darkened.

The frustration returned.

The fear returned.

The helplessness returned.

"Answer me."

Nothing.

He clenched his fists.

"Tell me where the three books are."

No reply.

The darkness inside his heart began growing.

Not because of anger.

Because of desperation.

He needed answers.

But nobody would give them.

And as the night continued—

Harris sat there silently.

Unable to sleep.

Unable to rest.

Unable to stop thinking.

While somewhere far beyond the stars...

An unseen enemy continued watching Universe 4.

And for the first time—

The loneliness inside Harris's heart began returning.

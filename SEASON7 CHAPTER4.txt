# VOID WALKER — SEASON 7

## CHAPTER 4: THE EMOTION INSIDE THE HEART (PART 1)

The night remained silent.

The camp that had been filled with arguments, tears, laughter, and promises only a few hours earlier had finally fallen asleep.

The mountains stood beneath the moonlight.

The forest moved gently with the cold wind.

The fountain continued flowing peacefully.

Everyone slept.

Everyone except Harris.

He sat alone near the fountain.

His knees pulled close to his chest.

His head lowered.

The smile he always showed others was gone.

The confidence was gone.

The cheerful attitude was gone.

Only Harris remained.

The real Harris.

The boy who was terrified of losing everyone again.

The boy who had already seen countless deaths.

The boy who was preparing to walk away from the people he loved most.

Tears slowly rolled down his face.

He didn't stop them.

For once...

He allowed himself to cry.

The image of Harlina's face appeared in his mind.

Her tears.

Her voice.

Her fear.

*"Don't leave me."*

The memory stabbed his heart.

He closed his eyes tightly.

"I'm sorry..."

The words escaped quietly.

The wind carried them away.

A few more tears followed.

"I don't want to leave either."

Silence.

Only the fountain answered.

His hands trembled.

Because deep down...

He wanted nothing more than to stay.

Stay beside Harlina.

Stay beside Koji.

Stay beside Lunar.

Stay beside everyone.

Live peacefully.

Grow older.

Laugh.

Travel.

Enjoy life.

Like a normal person.

But every time he imagined that future—

Omega Void Emperor appeared.

Entire universes burning.

Countless lives disappearing.

Friends dying.

Families disappearing.

Existence collapsing.

The peaceful future shattered.

And Harris knew.

Someone had to stop it.

Even if it meant becoming the villain in his friends' eyes.

Even if it meant walking away.

Even if it meant suffering alone.

He wiped his eyes.

Then closed them.

Slowly.

Carefully.

And called out.

"Creator."

Silence.

"Third Voice."

For a few moments nothing happened.

Then the world faded.

The stars appeared.

Darkness surrounded him.

The endless cosmic realm welcomed him once again.

The Creator stood nearby.

The Third Voice stood beside him.

Both immediately noticed Harris's expression.

The usual smile wasn't there.

Neither was his confidence.

Only determination.

The Third Voice crossed his arms.

"You're crying again."

Harris glared at him.

"That's your first sentence?"

"It was an observation."

"I hate your observations."

The Third Voice laughed.

The Creator shook his head.

Even after everything...

Those two still found ways to annoy him.

Harris took a deep breath.

Then immediately got to the point.

"Train me."

The Third Voice blinked.

The Creator blinked.

Several seconds passed.

Then both started laughing.

Harris frowned.

"What's funny?"

The Third Voice pointed at him.

"That's your problem."

Harris froze.

"What?"

"Your problem."

"Explain."

The Third Voice stepped closer.

Then tapped Harris's chest.

Right above his heart.

The moment he touched it—

Harris felt something.

A presence.

Small.

Faint.

But real.

His eyes widened.

The Creator smiled.

"Did you feel it?"

Harris remained silent.

The warmth continued.

Almost like something sleeping.

Something alive.

Something waiting.

Then slowly...

A smile appeared on his face.

"I understand."

The Third Voice raised an eyebrow.

"That was faster than expected."

Harris looked toward them.

"The answer is emotion."

The Creator nodded.

"Correct."

The stars around them glowed brighter.

The Third Voice smiled.

"The power you sealed never disappeared."

"The suit never disappeared."

"The Void never disappeared."

"They are sleeping."

Harris listened carefully.

The Creator continued.

"And emotions are the key."

Silence.

The warmth inside his chest became stronger.

Like a heartbeat.

Like a distant voice.

Waiting.

Watching.

The realization excited him.

If emotions were the key—

Then there was a path forward.

A way to become stronger.

A way to stop Omega Void Emperor.

A way to protect everyone.

The Third Voice suddenly became serious.

"But there is a problem."

Harris immediately frowned.

Of course there was a problem.

There was always a problem.

"What now?"

The Third Voice pointed directly at him.

"You."

"...Me?"

"Yes."

The Creator sighed.

"Your emotions are dangerous."

Harris looked confused.

The Third Voice continued.

"You care too much."

The answer surprised him.

"What kind of problem is that?"

"A very big one."

The Creator stepped forward.

"If you awaken your power through grief..."

The stars shifted.

A terrifying vision appeared.

A version of Harris standing alone.

Darkness covering his body.

Eyes filled with despair.

Everything around him destroyed.

The image felt familiar.

Too familiar.

Harris immediately understood.

Omega Void Emperor.

The Creator nodded.

"Exactly."

Silence.

The message was clear.

Pain could awaken power.

But pain could also corrupt it.

The Third Voice crossed his arms.

"We're allowing only two paths."

"Happy emotions."

"Anger."

"No despair."

"No hopelessness."

"No self-destruction."

Harris stared.

Then slowly smiled.

The smile looked harmless.

Too harmless.

The Creator immediately narrowed his eyes.

The Third Voice narrowed his eyes too.

They knew that smile.

It was the smile Harris used whenever he was planning something stupid.

"You are absolutely going to ignore us."

Harris placed a hand on his chest dramatically.

"I'm offended."

"You should be."

The Creator sighed.

The Third Voice sighed.

Both of them already knew.

Harris had understood their warning.

And he had probably already started planning around it.

Still...

Neither said anything else.

Because some lessons had to be learned naturally.

Eventually Harris nodded.

"Fine."

"I understand."

The Third Voice looked unconvinced.

The Creator looked unconvinced.

Harris looked innocent.

Nobody believed him.

Not even Harris.

The conversation continued.

Then suddenly Harris remembered something important.

The arrow.

Immediately his expression changed.

The joking atmosphere disappeared.

"One more thing."

The Creator looked at him.

"The arrow."

Instantly both cosmic beings became serious.

The stars around them darkened.

The Third Voice stepped forward.

"So they attacked you."

Harris nodded.

"Yes."

Silence followed.

Then Harris asked the question.

"Who was it?"

The Creator and Third Voice exchanged glances.

For the first time...

Neither answered immediately.

That alone made Harris uneasy.

Finally the Creator spoke.

"A version of you."

Silence.

Complete silence.

Harris blinked.

Once.

Twice.

Then a smile slowly appeared.

A calm smile.

A dangerous smile.

A smile neither cosmic being expected.

The Third Voice frowned.

"That's your reaction?"

Harris shrugged.

"What reaction did you expect?"

"Shock."

"Fear."

"Confusion."

Harris laughed.

Instead of fear...

Excitement appeared in his eyes.

"So it begins."

The Third Voice looked confused.

The Creator looked confused.

Harris folded his arms.

"If Omega Void Emperor is already sending versions of me..."

His smile widened.

"...then that means I'm already affecting his plans."

Neither cosmic being could read his thoughts.

Which was unusual.

Very unusual.

The Third Voice narrowed his eyes.

"What are you planning?"

Harris smiled.

"Let's wait and watch."

Even the Creator couldn't understand his idea.

The atmosphere became strangely silent.

Then the warmth inside Harris's chest appeared again.

Stronger this time.

A familiar warmth.

Comfortable.

Gentle.

Safe.

His vision slowly faded.

The cosmic realm disappeared.

The stars vanished.

The darkness dissolved.

And Harris returned to reality.

---

When he opened his eyes...

Everything was quiet.

The moon still shined above.

The fountain continued flowing.

The cold wind brushed across his face.

Then he noticed something.

Warmth.

Very close warmth.

He slowly looked down.

And froze.

Harlina was sleeping beside him.

No.

Not beside him.

Practically attached to him.

Her arms were wrapped tightly around him.

Her head rested against his chest.

Her face was slightly red even while sleeping.

She looked completely peaceful.

Completely safe.

Completely unaware of the war approaching.

A small smile appeared on Harris's face.

The warmth he felt earlier finally made sense.

It was her.

The same warmth.

The same comfort.

The same feeling.

His fingers gently moved through her hair.

For several moments he simply watched her sleep.

Then suddenly—

She spoke.

Softly.

Half asleep.

"D-don't leave me alone..."

Harris froze.

His heart stopped.

The words struck harder than any attack.

Her voice sounded frightened.

Like a child afraid of losing someone important.

A tear formed in Harris's eye.

Then another.

Before he realized it—

He was crying again.

A weak laugh escaped.

"You're making this difficult."

His voice shook.

He gently leaned forward.

Then kissed her forehead.

A long gentle kiss.

Filled with everything he couldn't say.

Everything he couldn't explain.

Everything he couldn't promise.

"I'm sorry."

Another tear fell.

"I really am."

The night remained silent.

Only the fountain listened.

Only the stars witnessed the moment.

And for the first time since learning about Omega Void Emperor...

Harris made a decision.

If emotions truly awakened power...

Then he would awaken it.

No matter how long it took.

No matter how painful it became.

No matter what he had to sacrifice.

The next morning would mark the beginning of that path.

And somewhere deep inside his heart...

Something sleeping had already begun to awaken.

# VOID WALKER — SEASON 7

## CHAPTER 3: THE CHOICE OF PAIN (PART 1)

The night remained silent.

The camp that had been filled with laughter only a few hours ago was now asleep beneath the stars. The forest was calm. The mountains stood motionless in the distance. The fountain reflected the moonlight like a mirror of silver.

Everyone was sleeping.

Everyone except Harris.

He sat alone at the edge of the fountain, staring into the flowing water.

His eyes never left the surface.

But his mind was somewhere else.

Far beyond the camp.

Far beyond the mountains.

Far beyond his universe.

The Third Voice's words repeated endlessly inside his head.

*"Another Harris is destroying universes."*

*"He sees you as a threat."*

*"Find the three books."*

*"Your powers are not gone."*

The more he thought about it, the heavier his chest felt.

The war against Omega Crimson Eclipse had finally ended.

Everyone deserved peace.

Koji deserved peace.

Lunar deserved peace.

Reku, Roku, Lina, and Mika deserved peace.

Most of all...

Harlina deserved peace.

Yet somehow another war was already waiting.

A bigger one.

A war beyond universes.

A war that could destroy everything.

Harris clenched his fists.

"No..."

The words escaped quietly.

"I'm not letting that happen."

The wind moved through the trees.

No answer came.

Harris closed his eyes.

For several minutes he tried to contact the Creator.

Nothing.

He tried again.

Nothing.

Then he attempted to call the Third Voice.

Still nothing.

The silence frustrated him.

"Come on..."

Nothing.

The fountain continued flowing peacefully.

Harris lowered his head.

Then suddenly—

An idea appeared.

A terrible idea.

A dangerous idea.

An idea that only Harris would think was reasonable.

His eyes slowly widened.

The last two times he had spoken with them...

He had lost consciousness.

His mind immediately connected the pattern.

"If unconsciousness opens the path..."

He stood up slowly.

"...then I need to become unconscious."

Immediately another voice inside him objected.

His mind.

His logic.

His reason.

*"That's stupid."*

*"Very stupid."*

*"Extremely stupid."*

But another voice spoke.

His heart.

*"If pain now saves everyone later..."*

*"Then do it."*

Silence followed.

A long silence.

Then Harris smiled.

The kind of smile that worried people.

"Yep."

He looked toward the sleeping camp.

"Definitely stupid."

Five seconds later he was already doing it.

A few moments later Harris stood beneath a large tree near the fountain.

The moonlight shone through the branches.

He stared upward.

Then sighed.

"Sorry, future me."

And before common sense could stop him—

THUD.

A sharp impact.

Pain shot through his head.

Not enough to seriously hurt him.

Just enough.

Then another.

Then another.

Several scratches appeared on his hands.

Small wounds.

Nothing life-threatening.

Just enough.

His vision slowly blurred.

The world tilted.

The fountain disappeared.

The stars stretched into streaks of light.

And then—

Darkness.

Complete darkness.

---

When Harris opened his eyes again, he immediately knew where he was.

The endless cosmic realm.

Countless stars.

Infinite darkness.

Galaxies drifting through existence itself.

And standing nearby...

The Creator.

The Third Voice.

Both of them looked completely shocked.

The Third Voice blinked.

The Creator blinked.

Harris smiled proudly.

The Third Voice pointed at him.

"Did you just knock yourself unconscious?"

"...Maybe."

The Third Voice stared.

The Creator stared.

Several seconds passed.

Then both simultaneously facepalmed.

"You cannot be serious."

"I am serious."

The Third Voice looked genuinely disappointed.

"This is the worst method anyone has ever used."

"It worked."

"That's not the point!"

"It still worked."

The Creator sighed deeply.

Even cosmic beings looked tired sometimes.

Finally Harris crossed his arms.

"I need answers."

Immediately both of them became serious.

The joking atmosphere vanished.

The stars around them seemed to grow quieter.

Harris stepped forward.

"Where are the three books?"

The Third Voice laughed.

The Creator remained silent.

Harris didn't laugh.

He didn't smile.

He didn't move.

He simply repeated the question.

"Where are the three books?"

The Third Voice stopped laughing.

Because Harris wasn't joking.

Not this time.

The determination in his eyes was different.

Stronger.

Sharper.

The Creator studied him carefully.

Then finally spoke.

"You are not ready."

Harris frowned.

"Not ready?"

"Yes."

"I fought Omega Crimson Eclipse."

"That doesn't mean you're ready."

"I saved my universe."

"That doesn't mean you're ready."

Harris became annoyed.

"Then what does?"

Silence.

The Creator didn't answer.

The Third Voice looked away.

The response only irritated Harris more.

"I'm asking where they are."

Still silence.

Harris stepped forward again.

His eyes were completely serious now.

The Third Voice noticed immediately.

The playful attitude vanished from his face.

For the first time...

He realized Harris had already made his decision.

He wasn't asking because he was curious.

He wasn't asking because he wanted information.

He was asking because he had already chosen his path.

The Third Voice sighed.

"You really plan on finding them."

"Yes."

"You understand what that means?"

"Yes."

"You'll become a target."

"I know."

"You might die."

"I know."

"You have no powers right now."

"I know."

The Third Voice stared directly into his eyes.

"And you'll still do it?"

Harris smiled.

A calm smile.

A fearless smile.

The smile of someone who had already accepted the cost.

"Of course."

The Third Voice became silent.

Even the Creator looked surprised.

Then Harris spoke again.

"If pain can protect them..."

His voice softened.

"...then I'll accept it."

For a brief moment nobody spoke.

Because they knew he meant every word.

The Creator finally looked away.

The Third Voice sighed.

Then slowly nodded.

"Fine."

The stars around them shifted.

The atmosphere darkened.

The conversation became serious.

"The threat's name is Omega Void Emperor."

The words echoed across the cosmic realm.

Immediately Harris focused.

The Third Voice continued.

"He wasn't always a monster."

Silence.

"He was once a hero."

Harris froze.

A hero?

The Third Voice nodded.

"He was a Void Walker."

The stars around them began forming images.

Harris watched.

A different universe appeared before him.

Another world.

Another sky.

Another version of himself.

The other Harris.

Standing proudly beside friends.

Standing beside a girl who looked similar to Harlina.

Standing beside people he loved.

Then—

Everything burned.

The sky shattered.

The Crimson Eclipse consumed the universe.

The people around him died.

One by one.

Friends.

Family.

Companions.

Everyone.

The images continued.

The other Harris survived.

Alone.

Broken.

Destroyed from the inside.

His eyes lost their light.

His smile disappeared.

His heart shattered.

Harris watched silently.

Because for the first time...

He understood.

The Third Voice spoke quietly.

"He lost everything."

The Creator continued.

"And after that..."

The image changed.

The broken Harris stood among the ruins of existence.

His face emotionless.

Cold.

Empty.

"He decided the Void Walker was the problem."

Harris frowned.

"What?"

The Creator nodded.

"He believed every universe would eventually suffer the same fate."

Silence.

"So he chose to end them first."

The image darkened.

Universes began collapsing.

Worlds began disappearing.

Countless versions of Harris were defeated.

Controlled.

Changed.

Transformed into soldiers.

Entire realities vanished.

Harris watched everything.

His fists clenched tightly.

"He calls it mercy."

The Third Voice's voice grew colder.

"He believes he's saving everyone from future pain."

Silence filled the cosmic realm.

The destruction continued.

Universe after universe.

Life after life.

Everything disappearing.

Finally the image vanished.

Only darkness remained.

Harris slowly lowered his head.

For the first time...

He didn't hate Omega Void Emperor.

He pitied him.

Because somewhere deep inside...

He understood exactly how much pain it would take to become something like that.

The Creator noticed immediately.

"You understand him."

Harris nodded slowly.

"...Yes."

The Creator sighed.

"That makes this even more dangerous."

The Third Voice stepped forward.

"Because now he knows you exist."

Harris looked up.

The Third Voice pointed directly at him.

"And you're the one person he fears."

Harris blinked.

"...Me?"

The Creator nodded.

"Because you don't know your true power yet."

Harris frowned.

"My true power?"

The Creator smiled.

"The power you sealed."

The Third Voice smiled as well.

"The power even Omega Void Emperor never discovered."

Harris froze.

The stars around them grew brighter.

And for the first time since arriving...

He realized this conversation was about to reveal something far bigger than the multiverse itself.

## CHAPTER 3: THE CHOICE OF PAIN (PART 2)

The endless cosmic realm remained silent.

Not yet.

"...the answer is simple."

"Talk."

"...What?"

"To who?"

"The suit."

Complete silence.

Again.

The suit.

Inside him.

Still.

After all this time.

The battles.

The transformations.

The power.

The armor.

Everything.

Still there.

Waiting.

"The suit recognizes only one owner."

"You."

"The suit never abandoned you."

"You abandoned it."

Harris stared in disbelief.

The Third Voice laughed again.

"You really thought it disappeared?"

"...Yes."

"That's adorable."

"Stop saying that."

The Creator almost looked amused.

For a moment the atmosphere became lighter.

Then Harris suddenly remembered something.

"The books."

The Third Voice nodded.

"The books are important."

"No."

Silence returned.

Annoying Harris seemed to be one of his favorite activities.

Different universes.

Different worlds.

Different stories.

Different versions of himself.

Each carrying different scars.

Different losses.

Different hopes.

Countless possibilities.

Countless lives.

Countless versions of himself.

The Creator spoke again.

"Some still fight."

The Third Voice continued.

"Some still believe."

The Creator nodded.

"And some are waiting."

Silence.

Then Harris smiled.

A genuine smile.

Because for the first time since learning about Omega Void Emperor...

He wasn't alone.

Somewhere out there...

Other versions of himself were still fighting.

Still protecting.

Still refusing to surrender.

The realization strengthened his resolve.

A very important thought.

"What now?"

Silence.

Hard.

Very hard.

"I'm serious."

The Third Voice laughed even harder.

"This is why we don't tell you things."

"Tell me."

The Third Voice finally calmed down.

Then answered.

"Just call us."

"...What?"

"Just call us."

"That's it?"

"Yes."

Harris blinked.

Several times.

"That's the answer?"

"Yes."

The Third Voice shrugged.

"If you truly believe we'll hear you."

"We'll hear you."

Silence.

Harris stared.

Then stared some more.

Then finally pointed at them.

"You could've told me that earlier."

"We tried."

"No you didn't."

"We definitely did."

"No."

"Yes."

The argument continued.

Eventually the Creator interrupted.

"If you use that method..."

He pointed toward Harris.

"...everyone will think you're talking to yourself."

Silence.

Harris imagined it.

Walking around.

Having conversations with invisible cosmic beings.

Everyone staring.

Koji judging.

Lunar questioning.

Harlina worried.

The image was horrifying.

"There has to be another way."

The Creator smiled.

"There is."

Harris immediately stepped forward.

"What is it?"

The Third Voice smiled as well.

"Unlock your power."

Silence.

"Then you'll know."

Harris groaned.

Of course that was the answer.

Everything always returned to unlocking his power.

Before he could complain—

The entire cosmic realm suddenly trembled.

The stars distorted.

The darkness cracked.

The connection was ending.

The Creator looked toward him one last time.

"Harris."

The seriousness in his voice immediately caught attention.

"The next time an arrow appears..."

"The arrow?"

The Third Voice nodded.

"Do not ignore it."

The atmosphere darkened.

Instantly.

The Creator continued.

"Someone is already searching for you."

Before Harris could ask who—

Reality shattered.

The stars disappeared.

The darkness vanished.

The cosmic realm collapsed.

And suddenly—

SPLASH!

Ice-cold water exploded around him.

Harris's eyes snapped open.

Completely soaked.

Completely awake.

Birds were beginning to sing.

The camp remained asleep.

Then slowly sat up.

Water dripped from his hair.

His clothes.

His face.

Everything.

A smile appeared.

Then another.

Then suddenly—

Raised both fists toward the sky.

And shouted at maximum volume.

"LET'S BEGIN, OMEGA VOID EMPEROR!"

And somewhere inside the camp—

A loud laugh.

A genuine laugh.

The first true laugh since the war ended.

Because now he finally had direction.

The threat had a name.

The enemy had a face.

The path existed.

And he was ready to walk it.

No matter the cost.

No matter the pain.

No matter the sacrifice.

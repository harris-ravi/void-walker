## **VOID WALKER – SEASON 6**

# **CHAPTER 8 – The Shadow Knight's Revelation**

## **PART 1 – The First Day of the Shadow Knight**

The morning sun slowly rose above the city.

Golden rays illuminated the massive training ground.

Hundreds of soldiers stood in perfectly organized rows.

Young recruits.

Academy students.

City guards.

Army soldiers.

Experienced warriors.

Everyone had gathered before sunrise.

They stood silently.

Waiting.

Waiting for the mysterious instructor.

The one who had defeated everyone yesterday.

The one who stopped a massive blast with a single finger.

The one who called himself...

**The Shadow Knight.**

---

At the front stood...

Koji.

Lunar.

The City President.

Several Generals.

Commanders.

Reku.

Roku.

Jekuna.

Izuku.

Beside them...

Harlina.

Lina.

And Mika.

Although Harlina's face looked calm...

Her mind wasn't.

She quietly thought,

*"Who are you...?"*

*"Why does your fighting style feel strangely familiar...?"*

*"Why did your words sound exactly like Harris...?"*

She shook her head.

*"No..."*

*"It can't be..."*

---

Just then...

A cool breeze swept across the training ground.

**WHOOSH!**

Everyone looked toward the sky.

A black figure descended gracefully.

Without making unnecessary movements...

He landed silently before everyone.

The long dark coat gently fluttered in the wind.

The black mask completely hid his face.

His gloves covered both hands.

Not even a small part of his identity was visible.

The soldiers immediately stood straighter.

The President smiled respectfully.

"Good morning, Shadow Knight."

The Shadow Knight gave a small nod.

"Good morning."

---

Koji stepped forward.

"Everyone."

"Today's training officially begins."

"You are standing before the instructor who accepted the responsibility of helping every one of you become true protectors."

"No one will question his identity."

"No one will interfere."

"From this moment..."

"Treat every order as if your lives depend on it."

The entire army shouted together,

**"YES, MASTER KOJI!"**

---

The Shadow Knight slowly walked to the center.

He calmly looked at every single soldier.

Some were nervous.

Some were excited.

Some admired him.

Some feared him.

He spoke in a calm voice.

"Before learning how to fight..."

"You must first understand..."

"...why you fight."

Silence filled the ground.

---

He continued,

"A sword..."

"...is not made to show strength."

"It exists..."

"...to protect."

"A blast..."

"...is not created to destroy everything."

"It exists..."

"...to stop destruction."

"If your reason for becoming stronger..."

"...is pride..."

"You'll lose."

"If your reason..."

"...is protecting someone..."

"You'll continue standing..."

"...even after falling."

Every soldier listened carefully.

Even the experienced generals remained silent.

---

The Shadow Knight slowly picked up a simple wooden practice sword.

Not a legendary weapon.

Not an enchanted weapon.

Just...

A normal practice sword.

He raised it.

"This..."

"...is enough."

Several recruits looked confused.

One quietly whispered,

"Can someone really become strong with only a practice sword?"

Before anyone answered...

The Shadow Knight suddenly disappeared.

**WHOOSH!**

Everyone gasped.

The next moment...

He reappeared behind the young recruit.

The recruit jumped.

"W-When—?!"

The Shadow Knight smiled beneath the mask.

"The weapon..."

"...doesn't make the warrior."

"The warrior..."

"...gives meaning to the weapon."

The recruit immediately lowered his head.

"I'm sorry."

"No."

The Shadow Knight replied calmly.

"You asked the right question."

"Never stop asking."

"But always search for the answer yourself."

---

He returned to the front.

"Today..."

"We begin with the foundation."

"If your foundation is weak..."

"Everything above it..."

"...will collapse."

---

He planted the practice sword into the ground.

"First lesson."

"Balance."

He demonstrated the stance.

Both feet firmly planted.

Body relaxed.

Center of gravity perfectly aligned.

No unnecessary movements.

"Copy this stance."

Instantly...

Hundreds of soldiers copied him.

Some stood correctly.

Many didn't.

The Shadow Knight quietly walked between every row.

He adjusted shoulders.

Moved feet.

Corrected posture.

Raised elbows.

Lowered knees.

Every correction was precise.

He never shouted.

Never insulted anyone.

He simply explained patiently.

---

He stopped before one young soldier.

"Your feet."

"They're too close."

The soldier immediately corrected them.

"Better."

He moved to another.

"Relax your shoulders."

"You're wasting energy."

Another recruit.

"Grip the sword firmly."

"But don't strangle it."

"If your hand becomes stiff..."

"So will your movements."

The soldiers quickly realized...

Every small mistake mattered.

---

Nearby...

Koji quietly smiled.

"He notices everything."

Lunar nodded.

"Even mistakes I almost missed."

The President folded his arms.

"No wonder..."

"He defeated everyone yesterday."

---

After nearly an hour...

Every soldier finally understood the basic stance.

The Shadow Knight nodded.

"Good."

"Now..."

"Footwork."

He demonstrated.

One step.

Two steps.

Forward.

Backward.

Sideways.

Diagonal.

Every movement flowed naturally.

Like flowing water.

The soldiers copied him.

Again...

He corrected every mistake.

Again...

He patiently explained.

Again...

No anger.

Only discipline.

---

Hours passed.

The training gradually became harder.

Now...

The Shadow Knight introduced sword movements.

"No unnecessary swings."

"No wasted strength."

"Every movement..."

"...must have purpose."

He demonstrated a simple slash.

It looked ordinary.

Yet...

The air itself split with a faint whistle.

Everyone stared.

Koji quietly whispered,

"Such perfect control..."

---

The soldiers practiced again and again.

Some stumbled.

Some improved quickly.

Whenever someone fell...

The Shadow Knight personally helped them stand.

He never mocked weakness.

Instead...

He said,

"Falling..."

"...is part of learning."

"But..."

"Standing up..."

"...must become your habit."

Those words deeply inspired everyone.

---

After sword practice...

He moved to energy control.

He drew a small circle on the ground.

"Stand inside."

One recruit stepped forward.

The Shadow Knight instructed,

"Gather your energy."

"But don't release it."

The recruit tried.

His aura immediately became unstable.

**BOOM!**

A small explosion occurred.

The recruit panicked.

"I-I'm sorry!"

The Shadow Knight calmly waved away the smoke.

"No."

"This..."

"...is why we train."

He patiently explained breathing techniques.

How to calm the mind.

How to stabilize energy.

How to prevent unnecessary explosions.

The recruit tried again.

This time...

A perfectly stable sphere of energy formed above his palm.

The entire army applauded.

The recruit smiled brightly.

"I did it!"

The Shadow Knight nodded.

"Good."

"Remember this feeling."

---

One by one...

Every soldier practiced.

Every mistake became a lesson.

Every improvement brought confidence.

---

Nearby...

Harlina quietly watched everything.

She softly spoke to Lina.

"...He's strict."

Lina nodded.

"But..."

"...he never humiliates anyone."

Mika smiled.

"He teaches exactly like..."

She suddenly stopped.

The three girls looked at each other.

For a brief second...

The same thought crossed all of their minds.

*"Just like Harris..."*

They quickly dismissed the thought.

Harlina quietly looked at the masked warrior again.

*"Who are you...?"*

---

The morning slowly passed.

The soldiers had already learned more in a single morning than many had learned in weeks.

The President smiled proudly.

Koji crossed his arms.

Lunar nodded with satisfaction.

The entire army had begun changing.

And at the center of it all...

The mysterious Shadow Knight silently continued teaching.

Behind the mask...

Harris quietly watched the soldiers improve.

A small smile appeared beneath the mask.

*"Become stronger..."*

*"One day..."*

*"Protect this city..."*

*"...so that no one has to carry everything alone again."*

The sound of the training ground echoed across the city as the morning slowly gave way to noon...

---

Another family.

The Lion cubs.

"I appreciate your kindness."

"I must refuse."

"May I ask why?"

The Shadow Knight calmly replied,

"I have somewhere I need to be."

"I understand."

"You don't have to explain."

The Shadow Knight gave a small nod.

"Thank you."

Without another word...

**WHOOSH!!**

He vanished.

The soldiers gasped.

"So fast..."

"I couldn't even see him move."

Koji smiled faintly.

"He already left."

### **Deep Inside the Ancient Forest**

Sunlight barely reached its entrance.

Inside...

The Lion King rested near the entrance.

"He came back!"

"I told you."

"He keeps his promises."

A gentle breeze entered the cave.

The next moment...

Still wearing the Shadow Knight outfit.

"Big Brother!"

One cub jumped onto his leg.

Two more rubbed their tiny heads against his waist.

"I just left this morning."

"You already missed me?"

The cubs nodded repeatedly.

"We always miss you!"

"You promised to come back!"

"You came back!"

Harris smiled.

"I always keep my promises."

---

He removed his gloves.

Then gently scratched behind one cub's ears.

The little lion happily purred.

Another cub rolled onto its back.

"Me too!"

Harris laughed.

"So everyone wants attention?"

The cave filled with soft laughter.

Even the Queen Lion smiled.

---

The Lion King slowly walked toward Harris.

"You've returned safely."

Harris nodded.

"Training finished for now."

The Lion King looked carefully at him.

"You should eat."

"You've been using too much energy."

Harris smiled.

"I know."

---

Without wasting time...

He walked deeper into the forest.

The cubs followed him.

"Wait!"

"We're coming too!"

Harris laughed.

"You'll only slow me down."

The cubs puffed their cheeks.

"We're fast!"

The Lion King chuckled.

"Go."

"They won't listen anyway."

---

The small group walked through the forest.

The birds sang above them.

Fresh wind passed through the trees.

Soon...

They reached several enormous fruit trees.

Harris immediately climbed one.

Despite his injured shoulder...

He carefully picked dozens of fruits.

Then another tree.

And another.

The cubs below cheered loudly.

"Big Brother!"

"That one!"

"The red fruit!"

Harris laughed.

"I saw it."

---

Nearly thirty minutes passed.

By the time he finished...

There was a huge pile of fruits.

Far more than the family could eat today.

The Lion King looked surprised.

"...That's enough food for days."

Harris smiled.

"Exactly."

He carried every fruit back into the cave.

Then carefully stacked them.

Large pile after large pile.

Until one corner of the cave was almost completely filled.

The Queen Lion looked amazed.

The cubs' eyes sparkled.

"So many!"

"So many fruits!"

---

The Lion King quietly asked,

"Why are you gathering this much?"

Harris looked toward the cave entrance.

Then answered honestly.

"I don't know..."

"...when I'll be able to come again."

"If something happens..."

"I don't want any of you to go hungry."

Silence filled the cave.

The Lion King understood.

Harris was preparing...

Just in case.

---

The cubs suddenly ran toward Harris again.

They rubbed their tiny heads against his legs.

One hugged his foot.

Another climbed into his lap.

Harris gently patted every one of them.

The smallest cub looked up at him.

"When will you come back?"

Harris smiled.

"This evening."

The cub's face immediately brightened.

"Promise?"

Harris extended one finger.

"I promise."

The tiny cub gently bumped its paw against his finger.

"Promise."

The Lion King quietly watched.

"You care about us..."

"...as if we're your family."

Harris softly smiled.

"You are."

The Lion family fell silent.

Those simple words carried enormous warmth.

---

The Queen Lion gently said,

"Our cave..."

"...will always welcome you."

"No matter when."

"No matter how tired you become."

Harris lowered his head respectfully.

"Thank you."

---

He slowly stood.

Looked one last time at the smiling cubs.

The Lion King.

The Queen Lion.

Then said,

"I'll see you all this evening."

The cubs shouted together,

"Come back safely!"

Harris smiled.

"I will."

---

A gentle wind surrounded him.

**WHOOSH!!**

He disappeared from the cave.

The cubs continued waving toward the empty space.

"He'll come back."

"He promised."

The Lion King looked toward the distant city.

"He carries everyone's burdens..."

"But..."

"For a few moments...

He smiled here."

---

# VOID WALKER — SEASON 9 CHAPTER 9

## PART 1 — The Weight He Carried

Silence filled Void Haven.

The nightmare was gone.

Omega’s fear had vanished.

But the aftermath remained.

---

The battlefield still carried the scars of what had happened.

Cracked earth.

Broken barriers.

Fading divine energy.

And at the center—

The one who carried everyone’s fear.

---

Harris.

---

His breathing was weak.

Slow.

Heavy.

---

Though Buddy had healed the visible injuries…

The internal exhaustion remained.

Far worse than physical wounds.

---

Harris had carried the fear, pain, despair, and trauma of ninety-six universes.

That burden was not something healing alone could erase.

---

He was beyond exhausted.

---

Yet even now—

His first concern wasn’t himself.

---

It was everyone else.

---

His tired purple eyes slowly moved.

Looking at everyone.

---

Harlina.

Koji.

Lunar.

Reku.

Roku.

Lina.

Mika.

All versions.

All allies.

All family.

---

He saw them smiling through tears.

Alive.

Safe.

Free from fear.

---

That alone made him smile.

A weak but genuine smile.

---

> “Good…”

---

His voice was barely above a whisper.

---

> “Everyone’s okay…”

---

Harlina’s eyes instantly filled with tears again.

---

Even now.

Even after everything.

---

He was still worrying about them.

---

She slowly sat beside him.

Then carefully lifted him.

Supporting his weak body.

---

Harris didn’t resist.

He didn’t have the strength.

---

The moment he felt her warmth—

His exhausted body relaxed.

---

His head slowly fell onto her shoulder.

---

Everyone went silent.

---

Because this version of Harris…

Was rare.

---

The fearless warrior.

The Void Walker.

The man who stood against Omega.

---

Now looked like an exhausted child.

Completely drained.

---

Harlina gently held him.

One hand supporting his back.

The other softly brushing through his hair.

---

Her voice shook.

---

> “You idiot…”

---

Tears fell again.

Onto his hair.

Onto his shoulder.

---

> “Why do you always push yourself this far…”

---

Harris slowly opened his eyes.

Just a little.

---

Then weakly lifted one arm.

---

And wrapped it around her.

---

Hugging her.

Weakly.

But tightly enough to show intention.

---

Harlina froze.

---

So did everyone else.

---

Because Harris—

Even in this condition—

Instinctively chose her.

---

He buried his face into her shoulder.

---

His breathing slowed further.

---

Warm.

Safe.

Peaceful.

---

Then he whispered.

So softly only Harlina heard.

---

> “Because… I can’t lose you…”

---

Harlina’s heart stopped.

---

Tears poured instantly.

---

She bit her lip.

Trying not to cry loudly.

---

But failed.

---

She hugged him tighter.

Protectively.

Lovingly.

---

> “I’m here…”

---

Her voice cracked.

---

> “I’m right here…”

---

Harris smiled faintly.

---

The tension in his body disappeared.

---

For the first time since the nightmare attack—

He felt peace.

Real peace.

---

Everyone watched silently.

---

No one spoke.

---

Because the scene in front of them was too emotional.

Too precious.

---

Even Koji quietly looked away.

---

Lunar crossed his arms silently.

---

Reku and Roku stayed unusually quiet.

---

Lina and Mika wiped tears.

---

Even the versions felt emotional.

---

Because all of them understood something.

---

This was Harris’s true weakness.

---

Not power.

Not fear.

Not Omega.

---

Love.

---

The people he wanted to protect.

---

Harlina continued gently stroking his hair.

Like calming a child after a nightmare.

---

Minutes passed.

---

Silently.

Peacefully.

---

Then everyone noticed.

---

Harris wasn’t speaking anymore.

---

His breathing had become steady.

Deep.

Calm.

---

Harlina slowly looked down.

---

And froze.

---

Harris had fallen asleep.

---

Still hugging her.

---

Completely asleep.

---

Everyone’s hearts melted.

---

Because he looked so peaceful.

So vulnerable.

---

So human.

---

Not as the Void Walker.

Not as a warrior.

---

Just Harris.

---

A tired boy.

Finally resting.

---

Harlina smiled through tears.

---

Then whispered softly.

---

> “Sleep.”

---

She kissed his forehead.

Gently.

Lovingly.

---

> “You’ve done enough.”

---

The golden sunlight slowly covered them.

Warm and peaceful.

---

And for the first time in a long while—

Harris slept without pain.

Without nightmares.

Without burden.

---

Only in Harlina’s arms.

---

Safe.

---

# VOID WALKER — SEASON 9 CHAPTER 9

## PART 2 — Don’t Leave Me

Very rare.

Deeply.

Loneliness.

Fear.

Sacrifice.

Lovingly.

Peace.

Still deeply asleep.

Watching.

Quietly.

Stronger.

More dangerous.

Silence.

Then at everyone.

---

Only Harlina and Harris remained inside the house.

Silence filled the room.

Warm sunlight entered through the windows.

The peaceful atmosphere felt gentle.

Comforting.

Minutes passed.

Harlina quietly watched Harris sleeping.

---

His breathing was steady.

Calm.

---

She smiled softly.

---

Then—

She slowly stood.

---

She planned to join training for a while.

Just until he woke up.

Trying not to make any sound.

One step.

---

Two steps.

---

Then—

Suddenly—

A hand grabbed her sleeve.

Harlina froze.

Completely.

---

Her heart skipped.

---

Slowly—

She turned back.

And what she saw shattered her heart.

Harris was still asleep.

---

But his hand…

Was tightly holding her sleeve.

Weak.

Yet desperate.

---

Like someone afraid of being abandoned.

Harlina’s eyes widened.

---

Then—

Harris moved slightly in sleep.

His brows tightened.

---

His expression changed.

---

Pain.

Fear.

Loneliness.

The peaceful face vanished.

Lost.

Alone.

Very softly.

Weakly.

Broken.

> “Don’t…”

Silence.

---

His grip tightened.

---

> “Don’t leave me…”

Harlina froze.

---

Her entire body trembled.

---

Because his voice…

Was not the voice of the fearless Void Walker.

---

The abandoned child.

The boy who lost everything.

The boy who feared being left alone.

More weakly.

More painfully.

---

> “Please…”

A pause.

---

> “Don’t leave…”

Harlina’s heart broke.

Completely.

Tears instantly fell.

---

Because she finally understood something terrifying.

Even after becoming stronger.

Even after gaining everyone.

Even after being loved—

Deep inside Harris…

The fear of being left alone still existed.

---

That old wound had never fully healed.

---

Tears streamed down her face.

---

She quickly rushed back to him.

---

> “Harris…”

---

Her voice broke instantly.

---

She sat beside him again.

Grabbed his trembling hand.

Held it with both of hers.

---

Warmly.

Tightly.

---

> “I’m here.”

---

But he still looked afraid.

Something filled with pure love.

Then placed it gently onto her lap.

---

His body relaxed slightly.

But his hand still held her sleeve.

---

Tightly.

---

Desperately.

---

Harlina cried softly.

But smiled through tears.

---

Then she gently touched his face.

Brushed his hair.

And whispered near his ear.

With warmth.

With love.

With absolute certainty.

---

> “I’m not leaving.”

---

A tear fell onto his cheek.

---

She smiled.

Softly.

Lovingly.

---

> “Never.”

---

Harris’s expression slowly softened.

---

The fear disappeared.

The tension faded.

---

His breathing became calm again.

---

Then—

Still asleep—

Slowly.

Instinctively.

---

Toward her.

---

Hugging her tightly.

---

Like she was his entire world.

---

Harlina froze.

Then cried harder.

---

Because even asleep—

His heart knew where safety was.

---

Inside her arms.

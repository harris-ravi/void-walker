VOID WALKER — SEASON 7
CHAPTER 2: THE VOICE BEYOND PEACE (PART 1)

The morning sunlight stretched across the streets as Harris ran through the city at a speed that made several people stop and stare.

On one shoulder was a large travel bag.

In one hand was another luggage case.

And on his other shoulder...

Was Harlina.

"HARRIS!"

"What?"

"PUT ME DOWN!"

"We are late."

"We are not late enough for this!"

"We are."

"We are not!"

"We are!"

Harlina covered her burning face while several people turned toward them.

One elderly man looked completely confused.

A child pointed toward Harris and shouted,

"Look! A superhero is carrying his wife!"

Harlina's face somehow became even redder.

"We are not married!"

The child simply laughed.

Harris smiled.

"See? Even he knows."

"HARRIS!"

He laughed loudly and continued running.

For the first time in months...

He felt normal.

Not like the Void Walker.

Not like a hero.

Not like a survivor.

Just Harris.

Eventually the massive railway station appeared ahead.

The train whistle echoed through the air.

People rushed around carrying bags and tickets.

Harris immediately spotted a familiar group near Platform Three.

His eyes widened.

Then he shouted at maximum volume.

"KOJIIIIIIIIIIII!"

The entire station turned.

Koji nearly dropped his backpack.

Lunar almost choked on his drink.

Reku looked around in panic.

Roku covered his ears.

Lina jumped.

Mika nearly fell off the bench.

A few seconds later they all spotted Harris.

Still carrying Harlina.

Silence.

Absolute silence.

Harris slowly lowered Harlina onto the platform.

Her face was completely red.

Nobody spoke.

Nobody moved.

Then Koji pointed.

Then Lunar pointed.

Then everyone burst into laughter.

"HARRIS!"

"What?"

"You carried her all the way here?"

"We were late."

"That doesn't answer anything!"

"It does."

"No it doesn't!"

Even Lunar was laughing now.

Harlina wanted the ground to open and swallow her whole.

Meanwhile Harris looked completely proud of himself.

Eventually the train arrived.

Its massive steel body slowly entered the station.

The doors opened.

Everyone boarded.

Their trip had finally begun.

The group quickly occupied several seats together.

Koji immediately started talking.

Lunar joined.

Soon everyone was laughing and discussing the trip.

The atmosphere felt completely different from the battlefield.

No weapons.

No enemies.

No death.

Only life.

Only friends.

Only peace.

Harris sat beside the window.

At first Harlina sat next to him.

But eventually Lina and Mika pulled her into their conversation.

Soon she was talking with the other girls.

Laughing.

Smiling.

Enjoying herself.

Harris quietly watched her.

Nothing else.

Just her.

The sunlight coming through the train window reflected across her face.

The way she smiled.

The way she laughed.

The way she looked genuinely happy.

For a moment Harris forgot everything.

The war.

The Void.

The pain.

Everything.

His chest felt strangely warm.

Then another thought entered his mind.

Nobody was paying attention to him.

Nobody was asking about his injuries.

Nobody was worrying.

Nobody was watching him every second.

Normally that would feel lonely.

But today...

It felt nice.

Because everyone was finally happy.

And that happiness mattered more than anything.

A small smile appeared on his face.

Then suddenly...

Everything changed.

The smile vanished.

A pressure entered his mind.

A familiar pressure.

One he immediately recognized.

His heartbeat stopped.

No...

Not again.

The Third Voice.

The presence became stronger.

Much stronger.

Then another presence appeared.

The Creator.

Before Harris could react—

Darkness swallowed everything.

The train disappeared.

The passengers disappeared.

The world disappeared.

Silence.

Infinite silence.

Harris slowly opened his eyes.

He was standing in a place made entirely of darkness and stars.

Countless galaxies floated around him.

Endless streams of light moved through the void.

It felt larger than existence itself.

Then two figures appeared.

The Third Voice.

And the Creator.

Harris immediately crossed his arms.

"You two again?"

The Third Voice laughed.

The Creator smiled.

Neither seemed surprised.

Harris sighed.

"I thought all of this was finished."

The Third Voice grinned.

"You always think that."

"Because it should be."

The Creator stepped forward.

"It never ends."

Harris rolled his eyes.

"Wonderful."

The Third Voice laughed again.

Harris pointed toward them.

"Tell me one thing."

"What?"

"Why are you here?"

The Creator remained silent.

The Third Voice simply smiled.

Harris frowned.

"The war ended."

Silence.

"My powers are gone."

Silence.

"The threats are gone."

Silence.

"So why are you here?"

The Third Voice suddenly burst into laughter.

The Creator followed.

Harris stared at them.

"...What is funny?"

The Creator finally spoke.

"You still think your powers are gone."

Harris froze.

"What?"

"You never lost them."

The words echoed throughout the cosmic darkness.

Harris couldn't believe what he heard.

"What do you mean?"

The Creator raised his hand.

A glowing image appeared.

Inside the image...

Harris could see his own body.

And deep within it...

Massive amounts of power.

Sleeping.

Sealed.

Waiting.

His eyes widened.

"No..."

The Creator nodded.

"Your power never disappeared."

"You sealed it."

Harris stepped backward.

His mind raced through countless memories.

The Void Throne.

The final battle.

The collapse of the Void.

Everything.

He had truly believed his powers were gone.

Yet now...

Everything was still inside him.

Waiting.

Sleeping.

Hidden.

The realization left him speechless.

Then confusion replaced shock.

"Then why are you here?"

The Creator's smile disappeared.

The Third Voice became serious.

Immediately Harris knew.

Something was wrong.

Very wrong.

The atmosphere changed completely.

The stars around them dimmed.

The cosmic space itself felt heavier.

The Creator slowly spoke.

"The threat to your universe is over."

Harris nodded.

"I know."

"But the threat beyond your universe has begun."

The words hit him harder than expected.

His expression changed.

"What threat?"

The Creator looked directly into his eyes.

Then answered.

"You."

Silence.

Absolute silence.

Harris stared.

"...What?"

"You."

His heartbeat accelerated.

"What do you mean me?"

The Third Voice suddenly laughed.

"Not you."

Harris looked even more confused.

"What?"

The Third Voice walked forward.

"There are countless universes."

"Countless realities."

"Countless worlds."

"And countless Harrises."

Every sentence increased Harris's confusion.

The Third Voice continued.

"You are the protector of Universe Four."

"Universe Four?"

"Yes."

Harris blinked.

"Wait..."

"There are more universes?"

The Third Voice smiled.

"Far more than you can imagine."

Harris remained silent.

His entire understanding of reality was changing.

Then the Third Voice spoke again.

"And another Harris..."

The atmosphere darkened.

"...is destroying them."

Harris froze.

The words felt impossible.

Another Harris?

Destroying universes?

How?

Why?

The questions flooded his mind.

The Creator answered before he could ask.

"Because he sees you as a threat."

Harris stared.

"A threat?"

"Yes."

"Why?"

The Creator looked directly into his eyes.

"Because no Void Walker reached the Third Book."

Silence.

"Because no Void Walker completed the Divine Suit."

Silence.

"Because no Void Walker reached the truth beyond the Third Gate."

The words struck Harris one after another.

The Third Gate.

The Divine Suit.

The Third Book.

Every mystery connected together.

The Third Voice continued.

"You are currently the strongest known Void Walker."

For the first time since the conversation began...

Fear appeared in Harris's eyes.

Not fear for himself.

Fear for everyone else.

If another version of him existed...

And if that version was destroying universes...

How powerful was he?

The question remained unanswered.

Then something happened.

The Third Voice stepped closer.

His appearance began changing.

The glowing aura around him shifted.

His face slowly became visible.

Harris watched.

Confused.

Then shocked.

Then completely frozen.

Because the face revealed beneath the light...

Was identical to his own.

His heartbeat stopped.

The universe around him seemed to disappear.

He couldn't move.

Couldn't speak.

Couldn't breathe.

The face staring back at him...

Was Harris.

His own face.

His own eyes.

His own appearance.

The Third Voice smiled.

And Harris finally managed to whisper:

"...Why do you look like me?"

# VOID WALKER — SEASON 7

## CHAPTER 2: THE VOICE BEYOND PEACE (PART 2)

"...Why do you look like me?"

The question escaped Harris's mouth almost like a whisper.

The cosmic darkness around them remained silent.

The countless stars floating through the endless void seemed frozen in place.

The Third Voice continued smiling.

But this time...

There was something different in that smile.

Something familiar.

Something painful.

Something that made Harris feel like he was looking at a forgotten piece of himself.

The Creator remained silent.

Watching.

Waiting.

For a few moments nobody spoke.

Harris couldn't stop staring.

The face.

The eyes.

Even the expression.

Everything looked like him.

It wasn't similar.

It wasn't close.

It was exactly him.

"Answer me."

The Third Voice looked away.

"Not yet."

Harris immediately became annoyed.

"Not yet?"

"Yes."

"You're showing me my own face and saying 'not yet'?"

The Third Voice laughed.

The Creator almost smiled.

Harris crossed his arms.

"You two are impossible."

The Third Voice stepped forward.

"There are truths you are not ready to hear."

"I've literally survived the Void."

"Exactly."

Harris wanted to argue.

But before he could speak—

The Creator finally raised his hand.

Three glowing books appeared before them.

Floating.

Ancient.

Covered in symbols.

Harris immediately recognized two of them.

"The first book..."

The book that started everything.

The book that revealed the existence of the Void Walker.

The book that changed his life forever.

Then his eyes moved toward the second book.

"The Eclipse Book..."

The book connected to Harlina.

The book connected to the Void Queen.

The book that revealed countless secrets during the Eclipse War.

Then his eyes moved toward the third.

And his heartbeat stopped.

Because he recognized it too.

The Third Book.

The book connected to the Divine Suit.

The book connected to the mysterious arrow.

The book he lost during the final battle.

The book that disappeared when the Gate was sealed.

The Creator pointed toward them.

"You must find them."

Harris frowned.

"The first two already exist."

"They are incomplete."

"What?"

"The truths inside them are incomplete."

Harris stared.

The Creator continued.

"And the third book has been lost."

Silence.

The Third Voice finally spoke.

"You need all three."

"Why?"

The Creator answered.

"Because the future depends on them."

The atmosphere became heavier.

Harris suddenly remembered something.

The dream.

The nightmare.

The version of himself destroying everything.

The dead universe.

The blood.

The destruction.

The Creator looked directly into his eyes.

As if reading his thoughts.

Then quietly said:

"You already know."

Harris froze.

The dream immediately returned to his mind.

His breathing stopped.

"How..."

The Creator smiled.

"You have already seen part of the future."

Harris's eyes widened.

No.

Impossible.

It was just a dream.

Wasn't it?

Before he could ask another question—

Everything shattered.

The stars disappeared.

The books vanished.

The darkness collapsed.

And suddenly—

SPLASH!

Cold water hit his face.

Harris's eyes opened instantly.

The train was gone.

The cosmic realm was gone.

The Third Voice was gone.

The Creator was gone.

Only reality remained.

He was sitting on a station bench.

Koji standing nearby.

Lunar standing nearby.

Harlina holding a water bottle.

Everyone looking worried.

Before Harris could even process reality—

His mouth moved automatically.

"How do you know about the dream?!"

Silence.

Everyone froze.

Koji blinked.

"What dream?"

Reku looked confused.

Roku looked confused.

Lina looked confused.

Mika looked confused.

Harlina looked confused.

Only Lunar narrowed his eyes.

Harris immediately realized his mistake.

His brain moved quickly.

Too quickly.

"Harlina disappeared in my dream."

Silence.

Then—

Everyone burst into laughter.

Harlina's face instantly turned red.

"HARRIS!"

Even Koji almost fell off the bench laughing.

"You were dreaming about that?!"

"I thought something serious happened!"

"HARRIS IS AFRAID OF LOSING HARLINA!"

The teasing continued.

Meanwhile Harris forced a smile.

A fake one.

A practiced one.

The kind he always used when hiding pain.

But Lunar wasn't laughing.

Because something wasn't right.

Harris never slept during travel.

Never.

Not even once.

And the look in his eyes...

That wasn't the look of someone waking from a normal dream.

That was the look of someone who had seen something terrifying.

Hours later the group finally reached the campsite.

The place was beautiful.

Large mountains surrounded the area.

A crystal-clear fountain stood near the center.

The air felt fresh.

Birds could be heard singing.

Sunlight reflected across the water.

Everyone immediately became excited.

Tents were unpacked.

Supplies were arranged.

Games were planned.

The entire group became busy.

Everyone except Harris.

He quietly walked toward the fountain.

Then sat beside it.

Watching the water flow.

Listening to its sound.

Thinking.

Always thinking.

The Third Voice.

The Creator.

The books.

The dream.

The other Harris.

The universes.

Everything kept repeating inside his mind.

Eventually footsteps approached.

Lunar.

He sat beside Harris without saying anything.

A few moments passed.

Then Lunar finally spoke.

"Why did you lie?"

Harris smiled.

"So you noticed."

"I always notice."

The smile slowly disappeared.

Silence followed.

Then Harris quietly spoke.

"This isn't over."

Lunar immediately understood.

The peace.

The war.

The future.

Everything.

Harris slowly explained everything.

The Third Voice.

The Creator.

The countless universes.

The mysterious threat.

The other Harris.

The books.

The dream.

As he spoke...

His voice became weaker.

More emotional.

Because for the first time since the war ended...

He admitted the truth.

He was afraid.

Not of dying.

Not of pain.

But of losing everyone again.

Lunar listened quietly.

Then looked toward the sky.

And smiled.

"So the war ended."

Harris laughed weakly.

"Looks like it."

Lunar continued.

"And peace decided to start another one."

Both laughed.

A sad laugh.

The kind shared between survivors.

Before they could continue—

Harlina appeared.

"What are you two doing?"

Both immediately stood up.

Nothing more was said.

Not today.

The rest of the afternoon passed peacefully.

The girls wanted to explore part of the forest.

So Harris and Lunar accompanied them.

Maintaining distance.

Watching carefully.

Protecting everyone without making it obvious.

Eventually the girls noticed.

"Why are you two so far away?"

Lina shouted.

"Come here!"

Mika waved.

Reku laughed.

Harlina crossed her arms.

"You two look suspicious."

Harris and Lunar exchanged glances.

Then both ran toward the group.

Everyone laughed.

For a brief moment...

The future didn't matter.

Only the present.

Only friendship.

Only life.

By afternoon everyone began preparing food.

Cooking together.

Laughing together.

Arguing over recipes.

The campsite felt alive.

When everything was ready everyone gathered together to eat.

Then Harlina suddenly looked around.

"Where's Harris?"

Silence.

Everyone looked.

No Harris.

Lunar immediately knew.

"The fountain."

He stood up.

"I'll bring him."

Nobody questioned it.

Lunar walked toward the fountain.

Then stopped.

Because Harris was sitting there.

Alone.

Crying.

Quietly.

No one else could see it.

But Lunar could.

Years of friendship made some things impossible to hide.

The tears weren't dramatic.

They weren't loud.

They were silent.

The tears of someone carrying too much.

The tears of someone who finally realized peace might not last.

Lunar didn't interrupt immediately.

He simply stood there.

Waiting.

Giving Harris a few moments.

Eventually Harris wiped his eyes.

Forced a smile.

And stood up.

"Let's go."

Neither mentioned the tears.

Neither needed to.

When they returned—

Harlina immediately noticed something was wrong.

She always did.

"Why were you sitting alone?"

Harris smiled.

"I remembered something."

"What memory?"

"A lot of memories."

It wasn't exactly a lie.

And somehow that made it worse.

Evening arrived slowly.

The sunset painted the campsite gold.

The fountain reflected the sky beautifully.

Everyone was busy.

Playing.

Laughing.

Living.

Harlina quietly approached Harris carrying two cups of coffee.

She handed one to him.

Then sat beside him.

For a while neither spoke.

Just enjoying the silence.

Then Harris suddenly looked at her.

"Sit here."

She blinked.

"Where?"

He pointed toward his lap.

Immediately her face turned red.

"HARRIS!"

He laughed.

After several moments of embarrassment...

She sat down.

Very carefully.

Very shyly.

Harris smiled.

Then looked toward the sunset.

"Do you remember your birthday?"

Harlina froze.

The question surprised her.

"Which birthday?"

"The night everything changed."

Her expression softened.

Of course she remembered.

Some memories never disappear.

The two sat quietly.

Remembering.

Feeling.

Existing together.

Then suddenly—

"HARLINA!"

Her friends shouted.

"VOLLEYBALL!"

She looked back.

Then looked toward Harris.

"You too."

He shook his head.

"No."

"Come on."

"You go."

"Are you sure?"

"Yeah."

She smiled.

Then ran toward the others.

Harris watched her leave.

The smile remained.

But his eyes looked distant.

Night eventually arrived.

The games ended.

Dinner ended.

The laughter slowly faded.

One by one everyone entered their tents.

The campsite became silent.

The stars filled the sky.

The moon reflected across the fountain.

Everyone slept.

Except Harris.

Because after years of sleeping beside someone...

Sleeping alone felt strange.

Lonely.

Uncomfortable.

Impossible.

Eventually he gave up.

He quietly left the tent.

Walked through the silent camp.

And returned to the fountain.

The same fountain.

The same water.

The same thoughts.

He sat alone.

Looking toward the stars.

Thinking about the dream.

Thinking about the Third Voice.

Thinking about the books.

Thinking about the other Harris.

The one destroying universes.

The one who somehow feared him.

The wind moved gently through the trees.

The water continued flowing.

And deep inside his mind...

One question refused to leave.

Who exactly was the Third Voice?

And why did he have Harris's face?

Far beyond the stars...

Far beyond his universe...

Something watched.

Waiting.

Preparing.

And the peaceful night continued...

Unaware of the storm approaching existence itself.

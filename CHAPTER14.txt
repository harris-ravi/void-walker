SEASON 6 — CHAPTER 14
PART 1 — THE MORNING OF CARE

The morning arrived quietly over Ravynm.

For once, the city did not shake beneath the pressure of a battle. No alarms echoed through the streets. No enemies appeared in the sky. No powerful aura tore through the atmosphere.

There was only the soft light of dawn passing through the bedroom window.

Inside the room, Harris was still asleep.

His black hair rested messily against the pillow, and his dark violet aura had settled into a faint glow around his body. The same aura that had once terrified armies now moved gently, almost like a silent shield protecting the room.

Beside him, Harlina slept peacefully.

Her silver-white hair spread across the pillow and over part of the blanket. Her breathing was calm, but her injured hand and leg remained carefully supported exactly as Harris had arranged them before they went to sleep.

Even while sleeping, Harris had kept one arm around her.

He had made sure she would not accidentally turn onto her injured hand. He had positioned the pillows so her leg would remain comfortable. He had checked her bandages several times during the night, not because she had asked him to, but because he could not completely relax while knowing she was still recovering.

The room was warm.

The blanket covered them both.

Harlina’s uninjured hand rested against Harris’s chest, and her body leaned slightly toward him. Her face was peaceful, with a small, relaxed smile still visible.

For several minutes, neither of them moved.

Then Harris slowly opened his eyes.

At first, he did not remember the time.

He only noticed the warmth beside him.

His gaze shifted toward Harlina.

The moment he saw her sleeping face, his expression softened.

A small smile appeared on his face.

She looked peaceful.

After everything that had happened—the injuries, the fear, the hospital, the misunderstandings, and the countless moments when he had been terrified that something worse might happen—seeing her rest safely beside him brought a quiet happiness into his chest.

Harris carefully lowered his eyes toward her face.

“She’s finally sleeping peacefully,” he thought.

He did not want to wake her immediately.

He simply remained still, watching her breathe.

Then his eyes moved toward her injured hand.

The swelling had not disappeared completely.

The bandage was still wrapped around it, and even though the condition was improving, it reminded him of the pain she had experienced.

His smile slowly faded.

His gaze moved toward her injured leg.

The support beneath it was still in place.

He remembered carrying her home, helping her sit down, taking her to the hospital, and hearing the doctor explain that the injuries were healing but still required careful treatment.

Harris lowered his head slightly.

The happiness he had felt only moments earlier became mixed with sadness.

He hated seeing her hurt.

He hated knowing that even after he had healed her, some pain had remained. He hated remembering how frightened she had looked when she had fallen, and how helpless he had felt for those brief moments before he could reach her.

His hand gently tightened around the blanket.

He had the power to fight cosmic enemies.

He could summon legions.

He could create weapons, barriers, and techniques that could stop attacks capable of destroying entire cities.

But when Harlina was injured, none of that mattered to him.

All he wanted was for her to stop hurting.

Harris looked at her again.

“She’s getting better,” he reminded himself quietly. “The doctor said she’s getting better.”

He took a slow breath.

He did not allow his fear to control him.

Instead, he carefully moved his hand toward the pillow supporting her injured arm. He checked that it was still in the correct position. Then he looked at her leg and made sure the blanket was not pressing against it.

Everything was safe.

Only after confirming that did he begin to wake her.

“Harlina,” he said softly.

She did not respond.

Her eyes remained closed.

Harris waited a moment before speaking again.

“Harlina… morning has started.”

She moved slightly.

The moment she began to shift, Harris immediately placed his hand near her shoulder—not forcing her, but gently guiding her so she would not move her injured hand or leg.

“Careful,” he whispered. “Don’t move your injured hand or leg suddenly.”

Harlina slowly opened her eyes.

For a moment, she looked confused by the morning light.

Then she saw Harris beside her.

Her expression changed into a small smile.

“Good morning, Harris,” she said quietly.

Harris smiled back, though the concern in his eyes remained.

“Good morning.”

Harlina noticed that he was looking at her injured hand.

She glanced down at it.

Then she looked at her leg.

“You’re checking them again,” she said.

“I’m making sure you’re comfortable.”

“You checked them before we slept.”

“I know.”

“And during the night.”

“I know.”

“And now again.”

Harris paused.

Harlina’s smile became slightly wider.

“You didn’t sleep properly, did you?”

“I slept.”

“You were watching over me.”

“I was resting.”

“Harris.”

He looked away for a moment.

Harlina gave a quiet laugh, but the laugh quickly softened when she noticed the sadness in his expression.

“You’re still worried.”

Harris did not answer immediately.

He carefully adjusted the pillow beneath her injured hand.

“I’m worried because you’re still hurt.”

“I’m getting better.”

“I know.”

“The doctor said I’m getting better.”

“I know.”

“Then why do you look like that?”

Harris lowered his eyes.

“Because I don’t like seeing you in pain.”

Harlina’s smile faded into something gentler.

She looked at him for a long moment.

Then, without moving her injured hand or leg, she leaned closer using her uninjured side and carefully placed her arm around him.

Harris immediately supported her back.

“Slowly,” he reminded her.

“I’m moving slowly.”

“Don’t put pressure on your hand.”

“I won’t.”

“Your leg—”

“I know, Harris.”

She rested her head against him.

Harris became still.

He could feel her breathing against his chest.

The room remained quiet, but the silence no longer felt empty. It felt safe.

Harlina’s voice was soft.

“You don’t have to look sad every time you see my bandages.”

Harris looked down at her.

“I can’t help it.”

“You can.”

“I’m trying.”

“I know.”

She gently held onto him.

“You’ve been taking care of me since the accident.”

“I should take care of you.”

“You already are.”

“I want to make sure you recover completely.”

“You will.”

Harris looked at her injured hand again.

Harlina noticed.

“You’re doing it again.”

He blinked.

“Doing what?”

“Looking at my hand like you’re about to fight the bandage.”

Harris gave a small, reluctant smile.

“I’m not going to fight the bandage.”

“You look like you might.”

“I’m just checking.”

“You’re always checking.”

“Because you’re important.”

The words came out quietly, without hesitation.

Harlina’s expression softened.

She did not tease him this time.

Instead, she leaned slightly closer and rested her forehead against his shoulder.

“I know,” she whispered.

Harris gently placed his hand over her back.

He did not move her injured areas.

He simply held her carefully, making sure she remained comfortable.

For a while, they stayed like that.

The morning light grew brighter.

Outside, Ravynm slowly began waking. Distant footsteps could be heard in the streets. Somewhere beyond the house, a vehicle passed along the road. The city was beginning another ordinary day.

But inside the room, Harris and Harlina remained wrapped in a quiet moment that neither of them wanted to rush.

Harlina finally lifted her head.

“Harris?”

“Hmm?”

“Are you happy?”

He looked at her.

“Yes.”

“Even though I’m still injured?”

“I’m happy because you’re here.”

Harlina smiled.

“And sad because I’m hurt.”

“Yes.”

“Then you’re both happy and sad.”

“That’s what happens when someone you care about is recovering.”

She studied his face.

“You really were scared when I fell.”

Harris’s expression became serious.

“I was.”

“I remember.”

“I thought you were hurt worse than you were.”

“I know.”

“I didn’t like hearing you cry out.”

Harlina lowered her gaze.

“I didn’t mean to frighten you.”

“You didn’t do anything wrong.”

“I still feel bad.”

“You don’t have to.”

“I fell twice.”

“That doesn’t mean you did something wrong.”

“I made you worry.”

“You’re allowed to need help.”

Harlina looked at him again.

Harris continued, his voice calm but emotional.

“You don’t have to pretend you’re fine just because you think I’ll worry. If something hurts, tell me. If you need to rest, rest. If you need help moving, ask. I’d rather know than have you hide it.”

Harlina’s eyes softened.

“You really mean that?”

“Yes.”

“Even if I ask you to carry me again?”

“Especially then.”

She gave a small laugh.

“You’re going to carry me everywhere now?”

“Until you recover.”

“Harris, I can walk when the doctor says it’s safe.”

“I know.”

“And I can do things myself.”

“I know.”

“But you’ll still try to carry me.”

“Yes.”

Harlina smiled.

“Then I’ll have to remind you not to overdo it.”

“You can remind me.”

“I will.”

She carefully hugged him again.

This time, Harris did not speak.

He simply held her.

The sadness in his expression had not disappeared completely, but it had become calmer. Harlina’s presence reminded him that she was not helpless. She was recovering, and she trusted him enough to let him help her.

That trust meant more to him than any reward he had received from the presidents.

Harlina remained close to him for several minutes.

Then she whispered, “I’m glad you’re here.”

Harris looked down at her.

“I’m glad you’re here too.”

“Even when I’m injured?”

“Especially when you need someone.”

She smiled.

“You always say things like that.”

“Because they’re true.”

Harlina rested against him again.

Harris carefully checked the position of her injured hand one more time.

She noticed immediately.

“Harris.”

“What?”

“You’re checking again.”

“I’m making sure.”

“You just checked.”

“I know.”

She laughed softly.

He finally smiled.

The moment felt almost ordinary.

No battle.

No enemy.

No transformation.

No cosmic threat.

Just two people sitting together in the morning, trying to make sure one of them could recover safely.

After a few more minutes, Harris gently spoke.

“Harlina.”

“Hmm?”

“Morning has already begun.”

“I know.”

“We should wake up.”

She did not move immediately.

Instead, she held onto him a little longer.

“Can we stay like this for another minute?”

Harris looked at her.

“Yes.”

“One more minute.”

“One more minute.”

They remained together quietly.

Harris kept his arm around her while supporting her injured areas. Harlina rested against him, her eyes half-closed, listening to the steady rhythm of his breathing.

Then another minute passed.

Harris spoke again.

“Harlina.”

She opened one eye.

“What?”

“We really should wake up now.”

She smiled.

“Okay.”

But before moving, she carefully looked at him.

“Good morning again.”

Harris smiled.

“Good morning again.”

Harlina gently hugged him once more.

Harris returned the hug carefully, making sure she did not put pressure on her injured hand or leg.

Then, slowly and safely, they began preparing to leave the bed.

Harris moved first, checking the floor and arranging the pillows. He made sure Harlina would have support before she changed position.

“Don’t rush,” he said.

“I won’t.”

“Tell me if anything hurts.”

“I will.”

“And don’t try to stand before I’m ready.”

Harlina looked at him with an amused expression.

“You sound like the doctor.”

“The doctor was right.”

“He also said I should rest.”

“You should.”

“And you’re going to remind me of that all day.”

“Yes.”

Harlina laughed softly.

“Then I suppose I’m not escaping your care.”

Harris looked at her with a gentle smile.

“No. Not until you recover.”

She nodded.

“Okay, Harris.”

The morning continued.

And for the first time in a long while, Harris did not feel the need to prepare for war.

He only needed to make sure Harlina could begin the day safely.

That was enough.

PART 2 — HARLINA WANTS TO REFRESH

Harris carefully lifted Harlina from the bed.

One arm supported her back while the other supported her beneath the knees, making sure her injured leg remained steady. He moved slowly, watching her expression for any sign of pain.

“Harlina, don’t move your injured hand or leg,” he reminded her.

Harlina rested her uninjured hand against his shoulder.

“Harris, I want to refresh.”

Harris immediately became concerned.

“We can clean you with a wet cloth first. That will be safer until your injuries recover.”

Harlina shook her head gently.

“No. I want to refresh properly.”

“Harlina—”

“If you don’t help me, I’ll go and refresh myself now.”

Harris stopped walking.

His eyes widened.

“You can’t do that.”

“Why not?”

“Because you still can’t move one hand and one leg properly. You could lose your balance or hurt yourself again.”

Harlina looked at him with a small smile.

“I’ll be careful.”

“You already said that before you fell.”

Her smile softened.

Harris held her a little more securely, but he did not squeeze her injured areas.

“Please listen to me, Harlina. First recover. You don’t have to prove that you can do everything alone.”

Harlina looked down at her bandaged hand.

For a moment, she seemed to understand how frightened he was.

But she still wanted to refresh.

“I want to refresh now,” she said firmly.

Harris sighed, his worry growing.

“But how will you do it? You can’t properly move one leg or one hand.”

Harlina’s cheeks slowly turned pink.

Then she smiled.

“Then you also refresh with me and help me.”

Harris’s face immediately turned red.

“H-Huh? When did you start asking like this?”

Harlina raised her eyebrows playfully.

“First answer me. Will you help me refresh, or will I refresh myself?”

Harris froze.

His mind immediately imagined her trying to stand without support, and his expression became even more worried.

“Harlina, please…”

She watched him for a few seconds.

Then she carefully shifted her weight as if she were going to stand.

Harris instantly tightened his support.

“Please don’t move!”

Harlina stopped and looked at him.

“Then refresh with me.”

Harris remained silent.

He knew she was not trying to make fun of him. She simply wanted to feel normal again. She wanted to begin the morning without feeling completely helpless.

Finally, Harris lowered his head and sighed.

“Okay. I’ll help you. But you must follow everything I say.”

Harlina smiled.

“Okay.”

“And you won’t move your injured hand or leg suddenly.”

“Okay.”

“And if anything hurts, you tell me immediately.”

“I promise.”

“And you won’t try to stand by yourself.”

Harlina gave him a gentle look.

“Okay, Harris.”

Only then did Harris begin walking toward the bathroom.

He carried her carefully through the bedroom and into the bathroom. Before doing anything else, he checked the floor to make sure it was dry and safe. He brought a sturdy chair, placed it in a comfortable position, and arranged clean towels and her fresh clothes nearby.

Harlina watched him quietly.

He was treating the simple act of refreshing as carefully as he would prepare for a dangerous battle.

“Harris,” she said softly.

“Hmm?”

“You’re being very serious.”

“Because I don’t want you to fall again.”

“You’re checking everything.”

“I have to.”

“You even checked the floor three times.”

Harris glanced down.

“It could be slippery.”

Harlina smiled.

“You really are too caring.”

Harris’s ears turned red.

“I’m just being careful.”

“That’s what makes you caring.”

He looked away, embarrassed.

Harlina carefully changed into a comfortable towel wrap with his help while he kept his attention on supporting her safely and preserving her privacy. There was no rush, no teasing that could make her uncomfortable, and no movement that might strain her injuries.

Once she was ready, Harris helped her sit on the chair.

“Are you comfortable?”

“Yes.”

“Is your leg supported?”

“Yes.”

“Is your hand resting safely?”

“Yes.”

“Does anything hurt?”

“Only a little.”

Harris immediately became serious.

“Where?”

Harlina gently pointed toward the injured areas.

Harris checked the bandages without disturbing them.

“It may still be sore because the swelling hasn’t completely disappeared,” he said. “We’ll be careful.”

Harlina looked at him with a warm smile.

“You’re acting like my personal protector.”

“I am your protector.”

“You don’t have to say it like that.”

“I mean it.”

Her expression softened.

Harris helped her refresh safely, keeping the injured hand and leg supported throughout. He avoided putting pressure on the bandages and made sure she could remain seated comfortably. The process took longer than usual, but Harlina did not complain.

She simply watched him.

Every few moments, Harris asked whether she was comfortable.

Every time, Harlina answered him calmly.

Finally, when they were finished, Harris helped her dry herself and get dressed again. He carried her back toward the bedroom instead of allowing her to walk.

Harlina rested against him.

“You know,” she said, “you didn’t have to look so frightened.”

“I was frightened.”

“I noticed.”

“You tried to stand while injured.”

“I only moved a little.”

“That was enough.”

Harlina smiled.

“You really don’t want me to get hurt again.”

“No.”

“Even if I ask you to help me with everything?”

“Yes.”

“Even if I ask you to carry me downstairs?”

“Yes.”

“Even if I ask you to stay beside me all day?”

Harris looked at her.

“I was already planning to.”

Harlina’s smile became softer.

They reached the bedroom, and Harris carefully placed her on the bed. He arranged the pillows beneath her injured leg and supported her hand with another pillow.

Harlina looked toward her clothes.

“I want to choose my dress.”

“Of course.”

Harris opened the wardrobe and waited while she selected a comfortable dress. She chose one that was easy to wear and would not press against her bandages.

Harris helped her change safely, then stepped back so she could adjust the dress herself where possible.

When she finished, she sat on the edge of the bed.

Harris changed into his own clothes nearby.

Harlina watched him with a small smile.

“What?”

“Nothing.”

“You’re smiling.”

“I’m happy.”

“Why?”

“Because you’re taking care of me.”

Harris looked at her.

“You don’t have to thank me.”

“I know.”

She carefully moved closer without disturbing her injured leg.

“Harris?”

“Hmm?”

“Can I have a kiss?”

Harris smiled gently.

He leaned toward her and gave her a brief, caring kiss on the forehead.

Harlina’s cheeks turned pink.

“That was sweet.”

“You’re still recovering.”

“I know.”

“And I don’t want you moving too much.”

“I know.”

She rested her head lightly against him.

“Then carry me downstairs.”

Harris immediately nodded.

“Okay.”

He carefully lifted her again, supporting her back and injured leg. Harlina placed her uninjured arm around his shoulder while keeping her injured hand safely positioned.

As they left the bedroom, Harris reminded her once more:

“Don’t move your injured hand or leg.”

Harlina smiled against his shoulder.

“I won’t.”

He carried her slowly down the stairs, checking every step before moving.

The house was beginning to fill with morning light.

And although Harlina still needed care, she felt safer than she had since the accident—not because she was unable to do anything, but because Harris never made her feel alone while she recovered.

PART 3 — SAFE MORNING REFRESH

Harris carefully carried Harlina down the stairs.

One arm supported her back while the other kept her injured leg steady. Harlina’s uninjured hand rested around his shoulder, while her injured hand remained safely supported against her body.

“Don’t move your injured hand or leg,” Harris reminded her as he reached the living room.

“I won’t,” Harlina replied with a gentle smile.

He slowly lowered her onto the sofa and arranged the cushions around her. He made sure her injured leg was resting comfortably and that her hand was not pressed against anything.

“Are you comfortable?”

“Yes, Harris.”

“Does your leg hurt?”

“Only a little.”

“Your hand?”

“It’s better than yesterday.”

Harris still looked worried.

Harlina noticed his expression and smiled.

“I’m okay. You can prepare breakfast now.”

“Stay here. Don’t try to stand.”

“I won’t.”

“And call me if you need anything.”

“I will.”

Harris gave her one final careful look before walking into the kitchen.

He began preparing breakfast.

The kitchen soon filled with the sounds of plates, utensils, and food being prepared. Harris moved quickly but carefully, making sure everything was ready while still keeping one ear toward the living room.

Harlina sat quietly on the sofa, watching him from a distance.

She could see how seriously he was taking care of her. Even while preparing breakfast, he occasionally turned around to check whether she was comfortable.

Harlina smiled to herself.

Then suddenly—

*Knock! Knock!*

The sound came from the front door.

Harlina turned toward the entrance.

“Harris!”

“I’m coming!” he called from the kitchen.

He quickly placed the utensil down and walked toward the door.

When he opened it, Lina and Mika were standing outside with a large basket filled with fresh fruits.

Lina raised the basket with a cheerful smile.

“Good morning!”

Mika smiled beside her.

“We brought fruits for Harlina.”

Harris’s expression softened.

“Come inside.”

Lina and Mika entered the house.

Harris closed the door and pointed toward the living room.

“She’s on the sofa. Please be careful around her injured leg and hand.”

“We know,” Mika said.

They walked toward Harlina.

“Harlina!” Lina said warmly. “How are you feeling today?”

Harlina smiled.

“I’m feeling better.”

Mika placed the fruit basket on the table.

“How is the swelling now?”

Harlina looked down at her bandaged hand.

“The swelling has reduced a little. My leg is also better.”

“That’s good,” Lina said with relief.

Mika carefully sat beside her.

“Are you still having pain?”

“Only a little. Harris keeps checking everything every few minutes.”

From the kitchen, Harris called out, “Because you’re injured!”

Lina and Mika looked toward the kitchen.

Then both of them began laughing.

Harlina smiled and shook her head.

“He has been saying that since morning.”

Mika grinned.

“He probably said it before you even woke up.”

“He did,” Harlina replied.

Lina leaned slightly toward her.

“Did he let you walk downstairs?”

“No. He carried me.”

Mika looked toward the kitchen again.

“Harris, you’re becoming a full-time protector!”

Harris answered while preparing the food.

“I’m only making sure she recovers safely.”

Lina smiled.

“That’s exactly what a full-time protector would say.”

Harlina laughed softly.

The sound reached Harris in the kitchen.

He paused for a moment.

Hearing Harlina laugh again made him happy.

For the past few days, he had been surrounded by worry. Every time she moved, he became frightened. Every time she felt pain, he blamed himself. But now, hearing her laugh with Lina and Mika reminded him that she was slowly returning to her normal self.

He continued preparing breakfast with a small smile.

In the living room, Lina opened the fruit basket and showed Harlina the different fruits.

“We brought apples, oranges, bananas, grapes, and some mangoes.”

Mika added, “And we brought enough for everyone.”

Harlina looked at the basket.

“You didn’t have to bring so much.”

“We wanted to,” Lina said.

“You’re recovering, so you need good food,” Mika added.

Harlina smiled.

“Thank you.”

Lina gently touched Harlina’s shoulder.

“You don’t have to thank us. We’re your friends.”

Mika nodded.

“And we wanted to see you smiling.”

Harlina looked at both of them.

“I’m happy you came.”

Their conversation continued, and soon the living room was filled with laughter again.

Harris finished preparing breakfast.

He placed every dish carefully on the dining table. He arranged the plates, glasses, and utensils so nobody would need to reach too far. He also placed a comfortable chair nearby in case Harlina needed additional support.

Then he walked back toward the sofa.

“Harlina, breakfast is ready.”

She looked at him.

“Okay.”

Harris bent down carefully.

“I’m going to lift you.”

Harlina placed her uninjured hand around his shoulder.

“Be careful.”

“I will.”

He lifted her slowly and securely, making sure her injured leg did not swing and her injured hand remained supported.

Lina and Mika stood up immediately.

“Do you need help?” Lina asked.

“I’m okay,” Harris replied. “Just keep the way clear.”

They moved the cushions aside while Harris carried Harlina toward the dining table.

He pulled out the chair and sat down, placing Harlina carefully on his lap instead of making her sit unsupported.

Harlina looked slightly embarrassed but smiled.

“Harris, I can sit on the chair.”

“I know. But I want to make sure you don’t move your injured leg or hand.”

Lina gave Mika a playful look.

“He’s not going to let her do anything today.”

Mika smiled.

“Not even hold a spoon.”

Harris ignored the teasing and picked up a small portion of food.

“Harlina, open your mouth.”

Harlina looked at him.

“I can eat.”

“You can, but your hand is still injured.”

She smiled and opened her mouth.

Harris fed her carefully.

He waited until she finished chewing before offering another bite. Between each bite, he checked that her injured hand was resting safely and that her leg had not shifted.

Harlina looked happy while he fed her.

She had eaten with everyone many times before, but today felt different. She could feel how much care Harris was putting into every small action.

Lina and Mika sat beside them and began eating as well.

Lina looked at Harlina.

“You look very happy.”

Harlina’s cheeks became slightly pink.

“I’m just enjoying breakfast.”

Mika grinned.

“Because Harris is feeding you?”

Harlina looked away with a shy smile.

“Maybe.”

Lina laughed.

“Maybe? You’re smiling like you received the greatest treasure in Ravynm.”

Harris continued feeding Harlina.

“She needs to eat properly.”

Mika leaned toward Lina.

“Look at him. He’s pretending this is only about breakfast.”

Lina nodded seriously.

“Yes. Completely professional.”

Harris’s face turned slightly red.

“Both of you, eat your breakfast.”

Harlina laughed again.

Harris looked at her and smiled.

As long as she was laughing, he felt a little less afraid.

He continued feeding her slowly, making sure she did not move her injured leg or hand. Whenever she tried to adjust herself, he immediately supported her.

“Careful.”

“I’m only moving a little.”

“Still be careful.”

“You’re very strict.”

“Only until you recover.”

Harlina smiled.

“Okay.”

The four of them continued eating together.

Lina and Mika talked about the city, the market, and the fruits they had brought. Harlina joined the conversation whenever she could, and Harris occasionally answered while making sure she was comfortable.

The morning became warm and peaceful.

After breakfast, Harris helped Harlina finish her medicine.

“Here,” he said gently. “Take this.”

Harlina accepted it with her uninjured hand.

She swallowed the medicine with water, then handed the glass back to him.

Harris carefully helped her move back to the sofa. Once she was seated, he brought the medical supplies.

“Harlina, I’m going to change your bandages.”

“Okay.”

Lina and Mika remained nearby, giving him space while still watching with concern.

Harris carefully removed the old bandage from her injured hand. He checked the swelling and examined the area without pressing it.

Harlina watched his face.

“You’re worried again.”

“I’m checking.”

“You always say that.”

“Because I need to make sure it’s healing.”

He applied the prescribed cream carefully, avoiding unnecessary pressure. Then he wrapped a clean bandage around her hand, making sure it was secure but not too tight.

“Does that feel comfortable?”

“Yes.”

“Any pain?”

“No. It feels better.”

Harris then checked her injured leg and changed its bandage as well. He supported it throughout the process and made sure she did not need to move it.

When he finished, he placed the supplies away.

Harlina looked at him.

“Thank you.”

Harris smiled.

“You don’t have to thank me.”

Lina folded her arms.

“He says that every time.”

Mika nodded.

“And then he checks the bandages again.”

Harris looked embarrassed.

“I’m only being careful.”

Harlina smiled at him.

“I know.”

Harris then turned serious.

“We’re going for another check-up.”

Harlina nodded.

“Okay.”

“I want the doctor to check whether the swelling is improving.”

“Okay.”

“And you’ll stay close to me.”

“I will.”

Lina stood up.

“We’ll come with you.”

Mika also rose from the sofa.

“Of course. We want to know what the doctor says.”

Harris nodded.

“Alright.”

He carefully lifted Harlina again.

Before leaving, he checked her injured hand and leg one more time.

“Don’t move them suddenly.”

“I won’t.”

They left the house together.

Harris carried Harlina through the streets of Ravynm. Lina and Mika walked beside him, carrying the fruit basket and a small bag containing the medicine.

The city was already busy.

## **"A Brother's Peace"**

The night passed quietly.

Outside the ship...

The endless sea of stars slowly drifted through the silent universe.

Inside...

Not a single alarm sounded.

Not a single battle remained.

For the first time after countless hardships...

The entire ship rested peacefully.

---

The main hall was illuminated by the soft golden rays of the rising sun.

The gentle morning light entered through the large observation window.

It fell upon a peaceful scene.

One that no one had ever imagined they would witness.

---

Harris was still asleep.

His breathing was calm.

His face, usually carrying the burden of an entire universe...

Now looked completely innocent.

Like a little boy.

His head rested comfortably on **Kira's lap**.

One of Kira's hands rested gently on his shoulder.

The other remained softly in his black hair.

Even in her sleep...

She had never stopped protecting her little brother.

---

Several minutes later...

Kira's eyes slowly opened.

She blinked once.

Then twice.

For a moment, she simply looked around the quiet hall.

Then...

She felt a slight weight on her lap.

She lowered her eyes.

There...

Sleeping peacefully...

Was Harris.

---

A warm smile appeared on her face.

"So peaceful..."

she whispered.

"...You finally stopped carrying the whole universe for one night."

---

She slowly brushed a few strands of hair away from his forehead.

His peaceful expression never changed.

His breathing remained slow and steady.

---

Kira gently smiled.

"So this is what my little brother looks like when he's truly asleep..."

---

She carefully adjusted the blanket covering him so the morning breeze wouldn't disturb him.

She made sure not to wake him.

Not today.

He deserved this rest more than anyone.

---

For several quiet minutes...

She simply watched him sleep.

There was no fear.

No pain.

No burden on his face.

Only peace.

---

Kira softly whispered,

"You've protected everyone..."

"Now..."

"...let your big sister protect you."

---

Outside...

The sun climbed higher into the sky.

Golden light spread across the stars.

The ship continued its journey peacefully toward its next destination.

---

A bedroom door slowly opened.

Koji stepped into the hallway after refreshing himself.

He stretched his arms.

"Good morning..."

---

He walked toward the hall.

Then suddenly stopped.

His eyes widened slightly.

A gentle smile appeared immediately.

---

Before him...

Harris was still sleeping peacefully on Kira's lap.

Kira looked up.

She placed one finger over her lips.

"Shh..."

---

Koji chuckled softly and nodded.

He walked closer with careful steps.

"So..."

he whispered,

"Our guardian finally found someone who could make him rest."

---

Kira smiled warmly.

"He cried himself to sleep."

---

Koji's smile faded into quiet understanding.

"I figured..."

---

"He has carried too much alone."

---

Kira looked down at Harris again.

Her fingers continued gently brushing his hair.

---

"He isn't just the strongest..."

---

"He's also..."

---

"...someone who has needed a family for a very long time."

---

Koji nodded.

"He finally has one."

---

The two quietly watched Harris sleep.

Neither of them spoke again.

They didn't want to disturb the peaceful silence.

For the first time...

The Void Walker...

The guardian of countless worlds...

Was simply sleeping...

Like a younger brother...

Safe beneath the care of his elder sister.

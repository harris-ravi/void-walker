## PART 1 — “THE TRAINING THAT FELT LIKE PEACE”

The evening had settled over Town Ravynm.

For the first time since Harris had awakened to the strange truth surrounding the Void, everything felt almost peaceful.

Almost.

Outside Harris's home, the training ground was filled with the sounds of movement, footsteps, energy bursts, and excited voices.

Harris stood at the center.

His eyes were closed.

His body remained completely still.

He was meditating.

Seirus stood several meters away, watching him carefully.

The white-purple aura surrounding Seirus remained calm, but his presence alone carried the authority of Harris's newly awakened Void Instinct form.

Around them, Harris's friends and sisters were training according to their own abilities.

Harlina practiced with her light-based power.

Zero focused on her golden energy and precision.

Ravian stood beneath the open sky, concentrating on his ability.

Chrona practiced her time-based techniques.

Gaia trained her gravity manipulation.

Aether worked on her own powerful abilities.

Lina and Mika trained together.

Reku and Roku practiced their techniques.

And Harris's sisters—Harina, Ziro, Rina, and Izury—continued their own training while Kira also participated.

Nobody was being left behind.

That was exactly what Harris wanted.

He had promised his friends that they would become stronger together.

He wasn't going to abandon that promise simply because his own power was growing at an impossible speed.

Seirus watched everyone for several moments.

Then his eyes moved toward Ravian.

"Ravian."

Ravian stopped.

"Yes, Seirus?"

Seirus calmly studied him.

"You are training the wrong way."

Everyone nearby turned.

Ravian blinked.

"Wrong way?"

Seirus nodded.

"Your ability is not what you believe it is."

Ravian became confused.

"What do you mean?"

Seirus walked closer.

"Your true technique is **Sky**."

Everyone froze.

Ravian stared at him.

"Sky?"

Seirus nodded.

"Yes. Sky is your true ability."

Ravian looked at his hands.

"But I thought my technique was Infinite Shield."

Seirus's expression remained serious.

"That is only a part of it."

The entire group became silent.

Even Harris opened his eyes.

Seirus continued.

"Your Infinite Shield is not your complete ability. It is merely one manifestation of your Sky technique."

Ravian looked shocked.

"So... I've been using only a part of my power?"

"Correct."

Seirus then raised one hand.

"But do not misunderstand what I am saying."

His voice became more serious.

"Sky does not mean that you possess an absolute defense."

Ravian listened carefully.

"Your Infinite Shield can protect you from an enormous number of attacks, but it has a weakness."

Ravian's expression changed.

"What weakness?"

Seirus looked directly toward Harris.

"**The Void.**"

Everyone became silent.

Harris didn't move.

Seirus continued.

"The Void can bypass your Infinite Shield."

Ravian's eyes widened.

"So even my strongest defense..."

"Can be bypassed by Void power."

Harris remained quiet.

Seirus looked at him.

"This is why you must understand your technique instead of depending on one defensive ability."

Ravian slowly nodded.

"Then... I need to learn Sky properly."

Seirus smiled slightly.

"Exactly."

Harris finally spoke.

"Ravian, don't worry."

Ravian looked toward him.

Harris smiled.

"We'll learn together."

Ravian smiled back.

"Yeah."

The atmosphere became lighter again.

Seirus turned toward the entire group.

"Everyone listen."

They gathered around him.

"Every ability has more depth than what you initially understand."

Harris remembered the mysterious voice from his earlier experiences.

The voice had told him that the abilities people believed they understood were only a small part of something much greater.

And Harris still remembered the words concerning the **12 Gen Eyes**.

The voice had told him that every ability could possess a maximum state through the Gen Eyes.

Only one of those twelve had been properly revealed to him so far:

**Voidgen.**

The supreme eye connected to the Void.

The remaining eyes were still unknown.

Harris knew there was much more waiting for him.

And somewhere beyond all of that were the **Void Balls**, the mysterious twelve balls capable of summoning four dragons and granting wishes.

He didn't yet understand everything about them.

But he remembered.

He had to grow step by step.

Power without discipline and heart could become destruction.

Seirus suddenly looked toward Harris.

"And you."

Harris opened his eyes.

"Me?"

"Your meditation."

Harris nodded.

"I'm trying to awaken Void Instinct."

Seirus smiled.

"Then continue."

Harris closed his eyes again.

His breathing became slower.

The training ground gradually became quiet around him.

His friends continued their individual training.

His sisters watched him occasionally.

And inside the house, Harisa and the others were preparing dinner for everyone.

For a few minutes, everything seemed completely normal.

Harris remained seated beneath the evening sky.

His heart was calm.

His mind was focused.

And somewhere deep inside him...

Something was waiting.

Something connected to the Void.

Something connected to the mysterious voice.

Something connected to the future forms and armies that Harris had not yet unlocked.

But it did not awaken.

Not yet.

Seirus watched silently.

"Step by step," he whispered.

Harris continued meditating.

And the peaceful evening continued...

For now.

"That's Inferogen and Hydrogen!"

"Stay calm."

"So I can't control them whenever I want?"

"You have awakened their power, but awakening something does not mean mastering it."

Harris nodded slowly.

He understood.

His **Inferogen** had appeared before.

His **Hydrogen** had appeared afterward.

But neither was permanently active.

Neither was something he could freely maintain at maximum power.

They could appear temporarily when his emotions, instincts, or circumstances triggered them.

And now he was attempting something even more difficult.

Void Instinct.

Harris closed his eyes again.

"I'll keep trying."

Seirus nodded.

"Good."

Harris continued meditating.

His friends returned to their training.

Ravian looked toward Seirus.

"So Harris can't control those maximum abilities yet?"

Seirus answered calmly.

"Correct."

Ravian nodded.

"Then he's still learning too."

Harris heard that.

He smiled slightly.

"Of course."

He didn't want everyone thinking he had already mastered everything.

There was still a long way to go.

Suddenly—

Harris felt something.

His eyes opened.

For an instant, his pupils changed.

A strange sensation passed through his body.

The Void responded.

His aura flickered again.

This time—

**VIOLET.**

A faint Void aura appeared around him.

Seirus immediately looked toward him.

Harris's friends stopped training.

"Harris?"

Harris stood slowly.

His expression was confused.

"I felt it..."

Seirus remained completely focused.

"Do not force it."

Harris nodded.

The Void aura became slightly stronger.

His hair moved in the energy.

His eyes briefly carried a strange Void-like glow.

Then—

**FWOOSH!**

The aura disappeared.

Harris returned to normal.

He stared at his hands.

"I couldn't hold it."

Seirus nodded.

"Because Void Instinct has not awakened permanently."

Harris looked disappointed.

"So that wasn't the transformation?"

"No."

Seirus's answer was firm.

"It was only a response."

Harris understood.

He had not unlocked Void Instinct yet.

He had only touched the edge of it.

Seirus continued:

"Do not chase the transformation itself. Strengthen your body, mind, discipline, and control. When the time comes, the form will awaken."

Harris smiled.

"Then I'll keep training."

He sat down again.

His friends smiled.

Harlina quietly watched him.

Zero smiled too.

Nobody knew how long Harris would need.

Nobody knew what would happen when Void Instinct finally awakened.

But Harris wasn't going to rush it.

He remembered the lesson he had already learned:

**Power without discipline and heart becomes destruction.**

So he closed his eyes once again.

He began meditating.

The Inferogen energy remained dormant.

The Hydrogen energy remained dormant.

Neither was permanently awakened.

And Void Instinct remained locked deep inside him.

Waiting.

Seirus stood beside his lord.

Not as a coach.

As his General.

Ready to assist whenever Harris needed him.

The peaceful training continued.

But then—

Far away from the training ground...

The sky suddenly began to change.

A strange pressure appeared over the town.

Seirus slowly raised his head.

Harris opened his eyes.

Everyone stopped.

A single burst of energy suddenly erupted near the center of Town Ravynm.

**BOOOOOOM!**

A brilliant light pierced the night sky.

Harris froze.

His eyes widened.

And every person in the training ground felt it.

Something had arrived.

Something powerful.

Something completely unknown.

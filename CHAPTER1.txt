Earth looked almost exactly like the world people knew.

**Powers existed.**

Some could create wind.

Others had abilities that were not connected to elements at all.

Some could transform.

Some could create weapons from their own power.

Some could enhance their bodies.

Some could heal.

Some could manipulate energy.

And some people possessed abilities that were passed down through their families.

Generation after generation, powers continued to evolve.

A child's ability could sometimes be completely different from their parents' abilities.

A family's power could become stronger over generations.

And humanity slowly became accustomed to a world where extraordinary abilities were simply part of everyday life.

But among all those powers...

There was one power that had never appeared.

A power mentioned only in the oldest records.

A power whose name had been whispered for thousands of years.

**The Void.**

Fire.

Water.

Lightning.

Wind.

Earth.

Light.

Darkness.

Transformation.

Energy.

Weapons.

Everything.

But nobody had ever seen it.

Nobody had ever used it.

Nobody had ever proven that it actually existed.

Because whenever historians searched for evidence...

There was nothing.

No living person possessed it.

No ancient warrior had ever demonstrated it.

No recorded battle had ever confirmed it.

No bloodline had ever awakened it.

Nothing.

Eventually, people began calling it what they believed it truly was.

A **myth**.

Years passed.

Then decades.

Then centuries.

Generations were born, lived their lives, and disappeared into history.

The world continued changing.

Powers became more advanced.

New abilities appeared.

Old abilities evolved.

Some warriors became unbelievably powerful.

Some people became heroes who protected their cities.

Others had powers but never wanted to fight.

They simply used their abilities in ordinary life and lived peacefully with their families.

Humanity learned how to live alongside supernatural abilities.

But the Void never appeared.

The ancient myth slowly became nothing more than a story told by historians.

A forgotten legend.

A mysterious name written inside ancient books.

Children were sometimes told stories about it.

Adults laughed about it.

Scientists rejected it.

Warriors dismissed it.

Even powerful people believed there was nothing behind the legend.

Thousands of years passed...

And still...

**No Void appeared.**

The world continued moving forward.

Civilizations grew.

Countries changed.

Cities expanded.

Power systems developed.

New generations inherited abilities from their ancestors and created abilities of their own.

But somewhere within the oldest surviving records, one sentence remained unchanged.

> **“When the Void finally appears, the world will know that the myth was never a myth.”**

Nobody knew who had written those words.

Nobody knew why they had been written.

Nobody knew what the Void truly was.

And nobody could have imagined that one day...

A child would be born who would completely change the meaning of those ancient words.

A child who had not yet entered the world.

A child whose name would eventually become known across Earth.

But for now...

His story had not begun.

The world was still waiting.

And the forgotten power called...

**VOID...**

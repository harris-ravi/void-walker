## **PART 1 – A Peaceful Family Dinner**

The warm lights inside the Void Ship glowed softly.

Outside...

Countless stars stretched across the endless universe.

Inside...

Laughter filled every corner of the ship.

For one peaceful night...

There was no battle.

No destruction.

No fear.

Only...

A family enjoying dinner together.

---

The large dining table was filled with delicious food.

Every dish had been prepared with care.

The aroma spread throughout the ship.

Koji smiled.

"I think today's dinner smells even better than yesterday."

---

Lina laughed proudly.

"Of course."

"We all cooked together."

---

Mika nodded happily.

"Everyone helped."

---

Roku immediately grabbed a piece of food.

"I'm starving!"

---

Before he could eat—

**Tap!**

Reku lightly hit his forehead.

"Wait."

"We haven't started yet."

---

Everyone laughed.

---

Harris looked around the table.

Every single person...

Was smiling.

Seeing them like this...

Made his own heart feel lighter.

---

He slowly joined his hands.

"Thank you for the meal."

---

Everyone followed.

"Thank you for the meal."

---

The peaceful dinner began.

---

Koji immediately started talking.

"So..."

"Our fearless leader."

"When are we reaching the next planet?"

---

Harris smiled.

"Soon."

"But tonight..."

"No missions."

"No training."

"Just relax."

---

Everyone nodded happily.

---

Meanwhile...

Zero quietly picked up a spoon.

She looked toward Harris.

"Harris."

---

He turned.

"Hm?"

---

Without saying another word...

She gently held the spoon toward him.

"Open."

---

Harris laughed softly.

"You started already?"

---

Zero smiled.

"Yes."

"Now open."

---

Harris obediently opened his mouth.

She carefully fed him.

---

Zero smiled brightly.

"Good."

---

Before Harris could continue eating—

Harlina gently cleared her throat.

"My turn."

---

She also held a spoon toward him.

"Harris."

---

He looked at both girls.

Then sighed dramatically.

"I have no choice, do I?"

---

Zero smiled proudly.

"No."

---

Harlina giggled.

"None."

---

Everyone around the table laughed.

---

Harris accepted Harlina's bite.

He smiled.

"Thank you."

---

Watching the scene...

Izury couldn't help smiling.

She quietly stood up.

She picked up a spoon.

Walked toward Harris.

And smiled warmly.

"Buddy."

---

Harris looked at her.

"Yes?"

---

She gently offered him another bite.

"You accepted me as your little sister."

"So let your sister take care of you too."

---

Harris smiled gently.

"Thank you, Izury."

He accepted the food.

---

Ziro immediately stood up.

"My turn, Buddy!"

---

Harina laughed.

"And mine after that!"

---

The two sisters happily fed Harris one after another.

---

Harris laughed helplessly.

"I think I'm eating more because of all of you."

---

Harina smiled innocently.

"That's the plan."

---

Everyone burst into laughter.

---

Koji leaned back in his chair.

"I've never seen anyone get fed by five girls at once."

---

Lunar covered his mouth to hide his laughter.

"He's completely surrounded."

---

Roid smiled quietly.

"Our captain really is loved."

---

Harris scratched his cheek.

"You all are making this embarrassing."

---

Zero looked at him with a gentle smile.

"You deserve it."

---

Harlina nodded.

"You always take care of everyone."

"So today..."

"We're taking care of you."

---

Harris became silent.

Those simple words...

Touched his heart.

---

A warm smile slowly appeared on his face.

"...Thank you."

---

He then picked up his own spoon.

He first turned toward Izury.

"Little sister."

---

Izury blinked.

"Yes, Buddy?"

---

He gently fed her.

"Your turn."

---

She smiled brightly.

"Thank you, Buddy!"

---

Next...

He fed Ziro.

---

"Thank you, Buddy!"

---

Then...

Harina.

---

She smiled happily.

"Brother always keeps his promises."

---

Finally...

Harris turned toward Zero.

---

She smiled softly.

"Harris?"

---

He gently fed her.

"Thank you..."

"For always worrying about me."

---

Zero's cheeks became slightly pink.

She accepted the food.

"...You're welcome."

---

Then Harris turned toward Harlina.

She was already smiling.

"I knew you wouldn't forget me."

---

Harris laughed.

"Never."

He gently fed her.

---

Harlina smiled brightly.

"Thank you, Harris."

---

The entire table watched the scene.

No one teased them.

Instead...

Everyone smiled warmly.

Because they knew...

This wasn't just love.

This was trust.

Care.

And a promise...

To always protect one another.

---

Dinner continued peacefully.

Everyone talked.

Everyone laughed.

For a little while...

The coming battles...

The Fragments...

Alpha...

Everything disappeared from their minds.

Only happiness remained.

---

After nearly an hour...

Everyone finished eating.

The plates were empty.

The conversations slowly became quieter.

A peaceful silence settled over the ship.

---

Harris stood up with a smile.

He looked at every member of his family.

"I'm glad..."

"We could all eat together like this."

"No matter what happens tomorrow..."

"Let's keep protecting these moments."

---

Everyone smiled.

Together...

They answered with one voice.

**"We will."**

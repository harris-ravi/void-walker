The words of the **First User** were still inside his memory.

The **12 Void Balls**.

The guardians.

The Gen Eyes.

Narkar.

Kaisel.

And now—

**Darkray.**

Harris slowly looked toward the windows.

For a moment, his expression became serious.

He could still remember the warning he had received.

There were twelve special Void Balls scattered across different countries.

Each one was protected by an extremely powerful guardian.

Only someone possessing the proper **Gen Eyes** could defeat those guardians.

And somewhere in the world, **Narkar, the Monster King**, and **Kaisel, the Darkborn King**, were also searching.

Their sons were being prepared.

**Alpha.**

**Beta.**

**Gamma.**

**Omega.**

And the sons connected to Narkar's side.

The threat wasn't gone.

It was only waiting.

Midoriya looked at Harris.

“Are you thinking about something again?”

Harris looked at him and smiled.

“Yeah.”

Midoriya waited.

Harris simply said,

“The Void Balls.”

Midoriya froze slightly.

Neprus also became quiet.

Harris continued walking.

“I don't know where they are yet. But I know someone else is searching for them too.”

Neprus understood immediately.

“You mean Narkar and Kaisel.”

Harris nodded.

“And their sons.”

Midoriya looked worried.

“But you're still only five…”

Harris smiled.

“I know.”

He looked at his own hand.

“I still have a lot to learn.”

Then he remembered the First User's words.

**Master the basic Gen Eyes first.**

Harris closed his hand.

“I'll get stronger.”

Neprus watched him carefully.

She knew Harris wasn't simply talking about becoming stronger.

He wanted to understand his powers.

He wanted to control them.

And most importantly, he wanted to protect everyone around him.

They finally reached the classroom.

Harris entered first.

His classmates immediately looked toward him.

The atmosphere became lively.

Some students were talking about the morning chase.

Others were discussing Harris' rapidly growing abilities.

Harris simply walked to his desk.

He placed his books down.

Midoriya sat nearby.

“Everyone, settle down.”

The class slowly became quiet.

“The examinations are approaching.”

“Both theory and practical examinations will be conducted. You must prepare for both.”

Harris immediately opened his notebook.

He began writing.

His pen moved quickly across the page.

The lesson continued.

Harris listened carefully.

Logical questions.

Detailed questions.

Questions that made the entire classroom pause.

Neprus answered what she could.

Harris didn't ask questions to show off.

He genuinely wanted to understand.

He wanted to know how abilities worked.

How Gen Eyes affected abilities.

How summoning worked.

How power control worked.

And how someone could grow without losing control.

His notebook slowly filled.

One page.

Then another.

Then another.

Harris continued writing.

Outside the classroom, however, something completely different was happening.

Far away from Ravynm…

Inside the headquarters of **Narkar and Kaisel**…

The atmosphere was dark.

Narkar and Kaisel stood before an ancient chamber.

Their expressions were serious.

Behind them stood their sons.

**Alpha.**

**Beta.**

**Gamma.**

**Omega.**

The others stood nearby as well.

A deep presence suddenly awakened.

The temperature of the entire chamber seemed to change.

Dark energy spread across the walls.

A pair of ancient eyes opened.

**Darkray had awakened.**

The ancient Evil God slowly rose.

His aura filled the headquarters.

“Welcome back, Lord Darkray.”

Darkray looked toward them.

“So…”

His voice was calm.

“The Void has appeared again.”

Narkar lowered his head.

“Yes, Lord.”

Kaisel continued.

“The child named Harris.”

Darkray remained silent for several seconds.

Then he smiled.

“Harris…”

The name echoed through the chamber.

“The second Void User.”

Narkar and Kaisel looked at each other.

Darkray slowly raised his hand.

Dark energy gathered around him.

“I have remembered something.”

Narkar asked,

“What, Lord?”

“There were ancient warriors who fought against the Void long ago.”

The room became silent.

“Ancient Evil Gods.”

“Ancient Emperors.”

“Warriors who possessed powers that disappeared from history.”

Kaisel immediately understood.

“You want us to find their remains?”

Darkray smiled.

“Yes.”

“But what about the twelve Void Balls?”

“Search for them too.”

Narkar hesitated.

“The guardians are extremely powerful. Only Gen Eyes users can defeat them.”

“Then find warriors who possessed Gen Eyes.”

Kaisel's eyes widened.

“Search every country.”

“Search every ancient battlefield.”

“Search every forgotten era.”

“Find the bodies of the old Evil Gods and Emperors.”

“We will use them.”

“Yes, Lord Darkray.”

“There is one problem.”

“The First Void User may have sealed those ancient bodies.”

“Then what should we do?”

“We find the bodies first.”

“We will find a way to remove the seal.”

“And perhaps…”

Back at school…

A distant presence.

Something ancient.

Something dark.

“Dark…”

“Harris?”

“Nothing.”

The world was beginning to move around him.

The **Void Balls** were waiting.

The **Gen Eyes** were awakening.

The ancient enemies were returning.

And somewhere beyond Ravynm…

**Darkray had opened his eyes.**

The next lesson began.

**Mirrorgen.**

completely unaware that his own attack had opened the door to an ancient enemy.

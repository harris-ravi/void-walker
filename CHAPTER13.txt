# VOID WALKER — SEASON 11

## CHAPTER 13 — PART 1: **“PEACE AFTER THE WAR”**

The night over Ravynm was quiet.

For the first time in many hours, there were no explosions in the distance.

No dark portals.

No enemy army.

No terrifying voices threatening to conquer the world.

Only the soft sound of wind moving through the trees outside Harris’s home.

Inside the house, the dining room was filled with warmth, laughter, and the smell of dinner.

Harris sat between his mother, Harisa, and Neprus. Lumi sat close beside him, while Zevian sat across the table with the other adults. Kira, the singers, aunts, uncles, Harlina, Zero, Harixa, Zeru, Midoriya, Hariya, and Haruka were all gathered around the large table.

Everyone was eating.

Everyone was safe.

And for a few precious minutes, nobody wanted to talk about war.

Harris lifted another spoonful of food and began eating happily.

Harisa watched him carefully.

She had been watching him ever since he returned from the battlefield.

Even though Harris was sitting in front of her, smiling and eating like an ordinary child, she still remembered the moment when he had exchanged places with his family.

She remembered the enemy attacks moving toward Harlina, Zero, Haruka, Hariya, Zeru, Midoriya, Ravian, Chrona, Aether, and Gaia.

She remembered Harris appearing in their place.

She remembered him taking the attacks meant for everyone else.

And she remembered the terrible silence that followed.

Harisa’s hand slowly tightened around her spoon.

Harris noticed.

“Mom?” he asked.

Harisa immediately looked at him.

“What happened?”

“You stopped eating.”

Harisa tried to smile.

“Nothing, Harris.”

Harris looked at her for a moment.

Then he gently pushed his plate a little closer to her.

“Eat, Mom. You were also fighting.”

Harisa’s expression softened.

She picked up her spoon again, but her eyes became wet.

Harris noticed that too.

“Mom… why are you crying?”

“I’m not crying.”

“You are.”

“No, I’m not.”

“You are.”

Harisa looked away, trying to hide her face.

Neprus noticed and placed a hand gently over Harisa’s shoulder.

“He always does this,” Neprus said quietly. “He notices everyone else’s pain before noticing his own.”

Harris blinked.

“What did I do?”

Lumi leaned closer and lightly touched his hair.

“You scared everyone.”

Harris looked confused.

“But I came back.”

“That is not the point,” Lumi replied.

Kira crossed her arms.

“You disappeared after taking every attack meant for us.”

The singers nodded immediately.

Laira looked at Harris with a serious expression.

“You made all of us believe we had lost you.”

Diana added, “And then you returned with a terrifying new form and laughed like a king.”

Vira leaned forward.

“Not just a new form. You created another legion.”

Harris slowly looked around the table.

“Genfera Legion?”

“Exactly,” Vira said.

“Twenty-second Legion,” Diana added.

“And General Genfera,” Laira finished.

Harris smiled.

“He was helpful.”

Everyone stared at him.

Haruka nearly dropped her spoon.

“Helpful?”

Harris nodded.

“He came when I needed him.”

Haruka pointed at him.

“You created an entire legion during the most terrifying moment of the war, and you are calling it helpful?”

Harris thought for a second.

“Very helpful?”

The entire table burst into laughter.

Even Harisa laughed through her tears.

Harris looked pleased.

He had not intended to make everyone laugh.

He had only answered honestly.

Across the table, Harlina and Zero looked at each other.

Their smiles were softer than everyone else’s.

They were still remembering what had happened earlier.

Harris had accepted their love.

He had told them that he loved them too.

And now, for the first time, they were not hiding their happiness.

Harlina gently placed her hand near Harris’s.

Zero noticed and smiled.

Harris looked between them.

“Why are you both smiling like that?”

Harlina immediately looked away.

“Nothing.”

Zero also looked away.

“Nothing.”

Harris narrowed his eyes.

“You both said that at the same time.”

Haruka grinned.

“That is because they are thinking about you.”

Harlina’s face became warm.

“Haruka!”

Zero covered her face with one hand.

“Please stop.”

Haruka laughed.

“But it is true.”

Hariya leaned toward Haruka.

“You should not tease them too much.”

Haruka looked at her.

“Why?”

“Because Harris will become embarrassed.”

Harris immediately shook his head.

“I am not embarrassed.”

Kira raised an eyebrow.

“You are.”

“I’m not.”

“You are eating your food without looking at anyone.”

Harris looked down.

He had been staring at his plate.

The singers began laughing again.

Harlina gently leaned closer to him.

“Are you embarrassed, Harris?”

“No.”

Zero smiled.

“Then look at us.”

Harris slowly raised his eyes.

Harlina and Zero were both looking at him with bright, happy expressions.

Harris froze for a moment.

The entire table became quiet.

Not because anyone was afraid.

This time, everyone was waiting to see what he would do.

Harris looked at Harlina.

Then he looked at Zero.

Then he smiled.

“I’m just not used to this yet.”

Harlina’s expression softened.

“Used to what?”

Harris looked down again.

“Being loved like this.”

The laughter disappeared.

The room became completely silent.

Harisa stopped eating.

Zevian looked at his son.

Neprus’s expression became gentle.

Harris continued quietly.

“I know everyone here loves me. Mom, Dad, my sisters, my friends… everyone. But this feels different.”

Harlina listened carefully.

Zero also became serious.

Harris held his spoon loosely in his hand.

“I spent so much time thinking that I had to protect everyone alone. I thought if I became stronger, nobody would get hurt. I thought I had to stand in front of every enemy by myself.”

His voice became quieter.

“But when I saw all of you in danger, I didn’t think about myself. I only thought about moving you away.”

Harisa’s eyes filled with tears again.

Harris looked at her.

“I know I scared you.”

Harisa immediately reached across the table and held his hand.

“You did more than scare me.”

Harris lowered his head.

“I’m sorry.”

Harisa shook her head.

“Do not apologize for protecting your family.”

“But I made you cry.”

“You made me cry because I love you,” Harisa said. “Not because you failed.”

Harris looked at her.

Harisa squeezed his hand.

“You returned to us. That is what matters.”

Zevian leaned forward.

“You do not have to prove your worth by sacrificing yourself every time.”

Harris looked at his father.

Zevian’s voice remained calm, but his eyes were serious.

“You are our son before you are the Void Walker.”

The words reached Harris more deeply than he expected.

For a moment, he could not answer.

Neprus gently touched his shoulder.

“You are not only a warrior.”

Lumi added, “You are our little brother.”

Kira smiled.

“You are also the person who annoys us every day.”

Harris looked at her.

“I do not annoy you.”

“You do.”

“I don’t.”

“You do,” Kira repeated.

The singers nodded together.

“He does.”

Harris looked betrayed.

“Even you?”

Laira smiled.

“Especially us.”

Diana laughed.

“You are our little brother. That means annoying us is part of your responsibility.”

Vira leaned toward Harixa.

“And Harixa agrees.”

Harixa was sitting on Lumi’s lap, holding a small toy.

She looked at Harris and laughed.

“Ha… Ha…”

Harris’s eyes widened.

“She said my name?”

Harisa smiled.

“She is learning to speak more easily every day.”

Harixa stretched her little hands toward Harris.

“Ha…ris!”

Harris immediately stood up.

Everyone watched as he walked around the table and gently lifted her into his arms.

Harixa laughed and held his face.

Harris smiled at her.

“You called me.”

Harixa touched his hair.

“Harris.”

Harris’s smile became even wider.

“I’m here.”

Harixa placed her forehead against his.

The room became warm again.

The singers gathered around them.

Kira gently touched Harixa’s back.

Lumi smiled as she watched Harris hold his little sister.

Harlina and Zero exchanged another quiet look.

They both understood what made Harris different.

He could create armies.

He could destroy powerful enemies.

He could awaken terrifying forms.

But the moment Harixa called his name, all of that power became unimportant.

He was simply her big brother.

Harris returned to his seat with Harixa in his arms.

He continued eating while holding her.

Haruka stared at him.

“How are you eating and holding her at the same time?”

Harris shrugged.

“I practiced.”

“Practiced what?”

“Being a big brother.”

Haruka looked at Harisa.

“Did he really practice?”

Harisa smiled.

“He has been doing it since Harixa was born.”

Harixa reached for Harris’s food.

Harris immediately moved the plate away.

“That is mine.”

Harixa stared at him.

Harris stared back.

Everyone waited.

Then Harris sighed and gave her a small piece of food.

Harixa smiled.

The entire table laughed.

“You surrendered immediately,” Zeru said.

Harris looked at him.

“She is stronger than me.”

Haruka pointed at Harixa.

“She has not even revealed her full power yet.”

Hariya nodded seriously.

“That is true.”

Harixa happily ate the food Harris gave her.

Harris looked at her with pride.

“She can become stronger later. Tonight, she only needs to eat.”

Harisa watched the scene with a deep sense of relief.

For so long, Harris had been surrounded by danger.

He had faced gods, evil kings, armies, and enemies who wanted to control the Void Balls.

But now he was sitting at home, feeding his little sister.

The battlefield was gone.

The dark army had escaped.

The world had survived.

And Harris was alive.

That was enough for Harisa.

At the other side of the table, Harlina and Zero were being quietly teased by their families.

Harian smiled at Harlina.

“So, you finally told him?”

Harlina nearly choked on her food.

“Dad!”

Harizy laughed.

“You were planning to tell him for a long time.”

Harlina looked embarrassed.

“I know.”

Zeri turned toward Zero.

“And you also told him?”

Zero covered her face.

“Yes.”

Zemi smiled proudly.

“Good. At least now you do not have to keep hiding your feelings.”

Harlina looked toward Harris.

Harris was still feeding Harixa.

He had not heard the conversation.

Zero leaned closer to Harlina.

“He looks happy.”

Harlina smiled.

“He does.”

Zero’s voice became softer.

“That is all I wanted.”

Harlina looked at her.

“Me too.”

For a moment, they did not speak.

They simply watched Harris.

Harris noticed them looking.

“What?”

Harlina shook her head.

“Nothing.”

Zero smiled.

“Just looking at you.”

Harris became embarrassed again.

He turned toward Harixa.

“Do you want more food?”

Harixa nodded immediately.

Harris sighed.

“You are helping them tease me.”

Harixa laughed.

The entire table burst into laughter once more.

Outside the house, the night wind moved gently across the grass.

Far away, the world was still talking about Harris.

News channels were showing the same images again and again.

The moment Harris’s aura changed.

The moment the Infinite Space Chamber broke.

The moment the twenty-second legion appeared.

The moment General Genfera knelt before him.

And the moment Harris stood before the enemy and ordered them to leave.

The world had given him many names.

The strongest child.

The strongest warrior.

The Void user.

The protector of Ravynm.

The warrior who defeated the God of Evil.

But inside his home, Harris was only Harris.

He was the son who still needed his mother to remind him to eat.

The brother who gave Harixa his food.

The friend who made everyone laugh.

The warrior who carried too much responsibility.

And the boy who had finally accepted that two people loved him in a special way.

Harris finished his dinner and leaned back in his chair.

“I’m full.”

Haruka looked at his plate.

“You ate almost everything.”

“Because I was hungry.”

“You were unconscious after the war.”

“That made me more hungry.”

Midoriya laughed.

“That makes no sense.”

“It makes perfect sense.”

Zeru shook his head.

“Only Harris can explain hunger like that.”

Hariya smiled.

Harlina gently placed her hand on the table near Harris.

Zero did the same.

Harris looked at both of them, then smiled quietly.

For once, he did not feel alone.

For once, he did not feel like he had to carry the entire world without support.

He still knew something was wrong.

He could still feel a strange presence somewhere far away.

Something ancient.

Something dark.

Something powerful.

But he did not tell anyone yet.

Not tonight.

Tonight, his family was safe.

Tonight, Harlina and Zero were happy.

Tonight, Harixa was laughing.

Tonight, Ravynm was peaceful.

Harris looked through the window toward the stars.

His smile slowly faded.

A strange pressure touched his senses for only a moment.

It was distant.

Hidden.

Suppressed.

But it was real.

Harris’s eyes narrowed slightly.

“What was that?”

Harlina noticed his change immediately.

“Harris?”

He looked back at her.

The serious expression disappeared, replaced by a small smile.

“Nothing.”

Zero watched him carefully.

“You felt something.”

Harris remained silent for a moment.

Then he looked toward the sky again.

“I don’t know what it is yet.”

Harisa heard him.

“What do you mean?”

Harris turned toward his family.

“I just feel that tomorrow is going to be different.”

Zevian’s expression became serious.

“Because of the tournament?”

Harris nodded slowly.

“Maybe.”

He looked at Harisa, then at everyone around the table.

“But whatever happens, I want everyone to stay safe.”

Harisa immediately understood that he was hiding something.

She reached for his hand again.

“Harris, tomorrow is not only your responsibility.”

Harris looked at her.

“You have a family. You have friends. You have people who will stand beside you.”

Neprus nodded.

“You do not have to protect everyone alone.”

Harlina looked directly at him.

“Neither of us will let you fight alone.”

Zero nodded.

“We will stand with you.”

Haruka raised her hand.

“And I am also participating.”

Zeru crossed his arms.

“So am I.”

Midoriya smiled.

“Me too.”

Hariya nodded.

“We will all protect each other.”

Harris looked around the room.

Everyone was serious now.

Everyone was ready.

He slowly smiled.

“Then tomorrow, we will win together.”

Haruka grinned.

“Of course.”

Kira raised her spoon.

“To Ravynm.”

The singers raised their cups.

“To Ravynm.”

Harlina and Zero looked at Harris.

“To everyone.”

Harris lifted his cup.

“To everyone.”

The cups touched.

The sound was small.

But it felt like a promise.

Outside, beyond the peaceful town, the darkness remained hidden.

Deep beneath the enemy headquarters, something ancient had awakened.

Karzun, the King of Abys, was waiting.

But inside Harris’s home, his family stood together.

And for one final night before the tournament, Harris allowed himself to be happy.

He looked at Harixa, who was already falling asleep in Lumi’s arms.

He looked at Harlina and Zero.

He looked at his mother and father.

Then he looked at the stars.

His voice was almost a whisper.

“I will protect all of you.”

The wind moved through the trees.

Far away, something answered with a faint, terrible pulse of darkness.

Harris’s eyes sharpened.

The peaceful night had not ended.

It had only become the silence before the next storm.

# VOID WALKER — SEASON 11

## CHAPTER 13 — PART 2: **“THE WORLD QUESTIONS THE STRONGEST KID”**

The peaceful dinner had ended.

But the world had not gone to sleep.

Across Ravynm, television screens remained switched on. News reporters stood outside studios, military headquarters, warrior academies, and government buildings. Every channel was showing the same images.

Harris.

His transformation.

His terrifying aura.

His evolved Infetsugen power.

His title—

**Inferjin King.**

And behind him, kneeling in perfect formation, were his twenty-two generals and their legions.

The world had seen powerful warriors before.

It had seen kings, gods, legendary fighters, and ancient weapons.

But it had never seen anything like a child standing calmly in front of an entire enemy army while a new legion appeared from his own aura.

One news channel showed the moment General Genfera emerged.

Another showed the battlefield changing from a destroyed war zone into a beautiful green field.

Another showed Harris collapsing after his energy ran out.

And another showed Harlina and Zero standing beside him after he woke.

The reporters could barely explain what they had witnessed.

“Tonight, the entire world is still trying to understand what happened in Ravynm.”

A large image of Harris appeared behind the reporter.

“The young warrior known as Harris has revealed a new transformation called **Inferjin King**. During the battle against Darknics and the forces of darkness, his power reached a level that caused the Infinite Space Chamber to collapse.”

The screen changed.

General Genfera appeared.

“Immediately afterward, a new legion emerged. This is believed to be the twenty-second legion under Harris’s command.”

The reporter swallowed nervously.

“Until today, the world believed that Ravynm possessed only a small number of extraordinary warriors. But after this battle, that belief has completely changed.”

The screen showed Harisa, Magnus, Neprus, Harizy, and Zeri activating their Gen Eyes.

Then it showed Ravian, Zeru, Zero, Harlina, Chrona, Aether, Gaia, Midoriya, Hariya, and Haruka.

“The adults of Ravynm possess Gen Eyes. The younger generation also possesses Gen Eyes. Other countries have attempted to awaken this power for years, but Ravynm’s warriors appear to have achieved it naturally—or through the influence of Harris.”

The reporter looked directly into the camera.

“The question now is no longer whether Ravynm can win the World Warrior Tournament.”

The image of Harris smiling appeared on every screen.

“The question is whether anyone can even challenge Ravynm.”

---

In another country, a group of commanders stood inside a large strategy room.

A massive screen showed Harris’s final appearance as Inferjin King.

Nobody spoke.

The commanders had expected the tournament to be difficult.

They had expected Ravynm to possess strong warriors.

They had expected Harisa, Magnus, Neprus, Harizy, and Zeri to be dangerous.

But they had not expected the children to possess Gen Eyes.

They had not expected Harris to command twenty-two legions.

And they had certainly not expected a new ancient power to awaken immediately after his battle.

One commander finally broke the silence.

“Pause the recording.”

The screen stopped on Harris’s face.

The commander pointed toward him.

“How old is he?”

A researcher answered quietly.

“We have not confirmed his exact current age.”

“He is a child.”

“Yes.”

“And he defeated Darknics?”

“Yes.”

“Darkray?”

“Yes.”

“Kaisel, Narkar, and Death were also forced to retreat.”

The researcher nodded.

The commander slowly sat down.

“Then why are we still preparing for the tournament?”

Another commander answered.

“Because the tournament rules still require us to participate.”

The first commander stared at the screen.

“That is not what I meant.”

Nobody laughed.

The image of Harris remained frozen behind them.

---

At a warrior academy in another country, hundreds of students watched the same broadcast.

Some were inspired.

Some were frightened.

Some were angry.

A few believed the news was exaggerated.

“He cannot be that powerful,” one student said.

Another student shook his head.

“The entire world saw it.”

“Maybe the recording was edited.”

“Then explain the battlefield.”

The screen showed the destroyed ground restoring itself into green grass.

The students became silent.

Their instructor stood at the front of the room.

“For years, we were taught that power came from discipline, bloodline, weapons, and experience.”

He looked at the image of Harris.

“But that boy has shown the world something else.”

“What?” a student asked.

The instructor answered slowly.

“Power can also come from the will to protect others.”

The students continued watching.

The instructor turned toward the training field.

“Tomorrow, every one of you will train harder than before.”

The students looked surprised.

“Against Ravynm?” one asked.

“Against your own weakness,” the instructor replied. “If you enter the tournament only because you fear Harris, you have already lost.”

---

Back in Ravynm, Harris’s classmates were watching the news from their homes.

Their teacher sat with the other teachers while the principal stood near the television.

The screen showed Harris standing before the enemy.

One classmate slowly smiled.

“That is Harris.”

Another nodded.

“He always looked quiet in class.”

The teacher laughed softly.

“Quiet?”

The principal looked at him.

“He was not always quiet.”

The teacher corrected himself.

“He was usually quiet.”

Everyone laughed.

Then the screen showed Harris’s Inferjin King form.

The laughter faded into pride.

The principal folded his arms.

“We taught him before the world knew his name.”

One teacher smiled.

“And now the world is learning from him.”

Another teacher looked at the screen.

“I hope he remembers that he still has homework.”

The entire room laughed again.

But beneath the laughter, they all felt the same emotion.

Pride.

Harris had not become powerful because he wanted fame.

He had become powerful because he wanted to protect people.

And everyone who had known him before the war understood that.

---

In another country, a group of young warriors watched the footage of Haruka.

The screen showed her standing beside Harris during the battle.

One student pointed at her.

“She is from Ravynm too.”

“She fought Kaisel.”

“She is in the children’s category.”

The students looked at one another.

Their instructor placed a document on the table.

“Her registered abilities are ice, air, and earth.”

A student stared at him.

“She has three elemental abilities?”

“Yes.”

“And she is already SS+?”

“Yes.”

The instructor looked toward the screen.

“Do not underestimate the children’s category. Ravynm’s young warriors are not entering the tournament as ordinary students.”

The footage changed to Zeru and Midoriya.

“They have already fought alongside Harris during a real war.”

The students became tense.

The instructor continued.

“They are not merely training for victory. They are training because they believe they must protect their people.”

---

Meanwhile, in another government headquarters, leaders gathered around a large table.

A map of the world was displayed in the center.

The countries of Chraynm, Kiroynm, and Zevynm were marked beside Ravynm.

One minister spoke.

“Three countries are entering the tournament.”

Another minister answered.

“And all three have prepared special teams.”

“Will they be able to challenge Ravynm?”

Nobody responded immediately.

A general finally said, “Perhaps they can challenge the other warriors.”

The room became quiet.

“And Harris?” someone asked.

The general looked at the image of Inferjin King.

“Harris is not being treated as a normal participant anymore.”

The leaders exchanged worried looks.

One of them whispered, “Then why is he still entering the children’s category?”

The general answered, “Because he is still a child.”

“But he has the power of a king.”

“And that is what makes him impossible to predict.”

---

The news continued throughout the night.

Reporters discussed Harris’s forms.

Experts tried to understand the relationship between his Gen Eyes and his legions.

Researchers argued over whether the twenty-second legion had always existed or had been created during the battle.

Military commanders calculated how many warriors would be needed to stop even one of Harris’s generals.

But every calculation ended the same way.

Nobody had enough information.

Nobody knew the full extent of Harris’s power.

Nobody knew where his six Void Balls were hidden.

Nobody knew how many techniques were inside the Void Book.

Nobody knew what his next transformation would be.

And nobody knew why he continued to fight like an ordinary child despite possessing power that frightened the world.

One reporter finally said:

“The most mysterious part of Harris is not his strength.”

The camera showed Harris smiling while holding Harixa.

“It is the fact that, after everything he has done, he still appears to care more about eating dinner with his family than becoming the strongest warrior in history.”

---

Inside Harris’s home, the television was still playing quietly.

Harris had already finished dinner.

Harixa was resting beside Lumi, holding one of her toys.

The singers sat nearby, while Kira gently fixed Harixa’s blanket.

Harlina and Zero were sitting close to Harris.

Haruka, Hariya, Zeru, and Midoriya were discussing the tournament rules.

The adults were still talking about the battle.

But Harris was looking at the television.

The screen showed his Inferjin King form.

He watched silently.

Then he tilted his head.

“Why are they showing that again?”

Haruka looked at the screen.

“Because the entire world is still talking about you.”

Harris frowned.

“But the battle is over.”

“That does not mean the news will stop.”

“Why?”

Haruka stared at him.

“Because you became Inferjin King, created the twenty-second legion, defeated the enemy army, and made the battlefield restore itself.”

Harris thought about it.

“That sounds like a lot.”

“It was a lot!”

Harris looked toward Harlina.

“Did I really do all that?”

Harlina smiled.

“Yes.”

Zero nodded.

“And the whole world saw it.”

Harris leaned back.

“I only wanted everyone to be safe.”

The room became quiet.

Harisa looked at him from across the room.

She understood that Harris still did not realize how much his actions had changed the world.

He did not understand why people were inspired by him.

He did not understand why warriors were training harder because of him.

He did not understand why children were beginning to believe that they could protect others too.

To Harris, he had simply done what he believed was necessary.

Harisa gently said, “That is why they are watching you.”

Harris looked at her.

“Because I fought?”

“Because you protected people.”

Harris lowered his eyes.

“I was scared too.”

Zevian looked at him.

“Of what?”

Harris was silent for a moment.

“Of losing everyone.”

Harlina’s expression softened.

Zero gently placed her hand near his.

Harris continued.

“When I saw the attacks moving toward all of you, I did not know if I could stop them.”

His voice became quieter.

“I only knew that I could not allow them to reach you.”

Haruka looked down.

Zeru’s hands tightened.

Midoriya became serious.

Hariya moved closer to him.

Harris looked at everyone.

“I know I became stronger. I know I unlocked Infetsugen Level 2 and Inferjin King. I know Genfera and the twenty-second legion appeared.”

He paused.

“But none of that matters if my family is not safe.”

Harisa stood and walked toward him.

She gently placed both hands on his shoulders.

“Your family is safe because you protected us.”

Harris looked up at her.

“But you were afraid.”

“Yes,” Harisa replied. “I was terrified.”

Harris’s expression fell.

Harisa immediately pulled him into a hug.

“But I was also proud.”

Harris remained still in her arms.

“You do not have to hide your fear from us,” she whispered. “You do not have to pretend that every battle is easy.”

Harris slowly closed his eyes.

For a few seconds, he allowed himself to rest against his mother.

The television continued broadcasting his image.

But nobody in the room was watching it anymore.

Everyone was watching Harris.

Neprus gently smiled.

“He is still our little boy.”

Kira nodded.

“Even if the world calls him the strongest warrior.”

Laira looked at Harris.

“He still needs someone to tell him when to sleep.”

Diana added, “And when to eat.”

Vira smiled.

“And when to stop creating armies.”

Harris looked up.

“I only created one new legion.”

Kira raised an eyebrow.

“One new legion today.”

Harris became silent.

Everyone laughed.

Harlina leaned closer.

“Do not worry. We will not let the world change you.”

Zero nodded.

“You can become stronger without becoming alone.”

Harris looked at them.

Then he smiled.

“That sounds good.”

---

Outside, the streets of Ravynm were still filled with people watching the news from shop windows and public screens.

Some people were celebrating.

Some were discussing the tournament.

Some were frightened about Karzun, although the world did not yet know his name.

And some were simply looking at the sky, wondering what kind of future was coming.

At a small training ground, a group of children stood together.

One child raised his hand.

“I want to become strong like Harris.”

Another child shook his head.

“I do not want to become strong like him.”

“Why?”

“Because he always has to fight.”

The first child thought for a moment.

“Then become strong enough so nobody has to fight alone.”

The other children looked at him.

Slowly, they nodded.

Their instructor watched from a distance.

He did not interrupt.

He knew that something had changed.

Harris had not only defeated an enemy.

He had changed the way the world thought about strength.

---

Inside Harris’s home, the news reporter’s voice continued.

“Tomorrow, the World Warrior Tournament will begin. The children’s category will be a team battle royal. Participants may win through ring-out or knockout. Killing and severe injury are strictly forbidden.”

The screen displayed the tournament stadium.

“Ravynm will face teams from Chraynm, Kiroynm, and Zevynm.”

Haruka sat up straight.

“Tomorrow.”

Hariya nodded.

“The tournament begins tomorrow.”

Zeru looked toward Midoriya.

“We need to sleep early.”

Midoriya smiled.

“And wake up early.”

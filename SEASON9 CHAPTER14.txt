## PART 1 — MORNING OF PEACE

### “Good sleep, my light…”

Morning arrived softly in **Void Heaven**.

Golden sunlight slowly entered through the window, spreading warm light across the peaceful room.

For the first time in many days…

Everything felt calm.

No war.
No monsters.
No Omega.

Only silence.

Only warmth.

And only the soft breathing of the girl sleeping in Harris’s arms.

Harris slowly opened his eyes.

For a few seconds…

He didn’t move.

He simply stared.

Then his eyes widened slightly.

He froze.

There she was.

Harlina was still sleeping peacefully in his arms… but just like last night…

She was kissing him.

Her face was buried softly near his chest, and her lips were gently pressed against him while sleeping.

She looked unbelievably peaceful.

Like a child who had finally found her safest place in the world.

Harris stared at her for a long moment.

Then…

A soft smile appeared on his face.

His eyes became gentler.

He slowly raised one hand and brushed a few strands of hair away from her face.

Her beautiful silver-white hair shined softly in the morning light.

She looked too adorable.

Too pure.

Too precious.

Harris spoke in a low voice.

> “You really sleep like this every time now…”

His smile deepened.

Then he leaned forward.

Very gently.

Very carefully.

He kissed her.

A soft kiss.

Warm.

Gentle.

Full of love.

Harlina moved slightly in sleep… but didn’t wake.

Instead, she hugged him tighter.

Harris froze for one second.

Then smiled helplessly.

> “You really don’t want to let me go, huh…”

She tightened her arms around him even more.

As if answering him.

His heart softened completely.

For a few moments…

He simply stayed there.

Holding her.

Feeling her warmth.

Listening to her breathing.

This peaceful silence…

It felt more precious than any victory.

More valuable than any power.

More important than any weapon.

Harris looked at her sleeping face again.

And his expression changed slightly.

The gentle smile remained…

But something painful appeared in his eyes.

A hidden burden.

A silent fear.

He looked at Harlina and thought quietly.

**Three weeks.**

Only three weeks until his birthday.

Only three weeks until Omega’s war.

Only three weeks until everything changed.

His hands tightened slightly around her.

His heart grew heavy.

What if…

What if he failed?

What if Omega won?

What if…

He lost everyone?

His eyes trembled slightly.

He remembered every nightmare.

Every vision.

Every universe destroyed.

Every version of himself screaming in despair.

Every moment of loss.

Then—

Harlina moved in sleep.

She softly buried herself deeper into his chest.

And quietly murmured.

> “Harris…”

His thoughts stopped instantly.

His expression softened again.

He looked down.

She was still asleep.

But even in sleep…

She called his name.

Like he was the only place she trusted.

The darkness inside him slowly calmed.

His fear weakened.

His breathing steadied.

He gently touched her cheek.

> “I’m here.”

His voice was soft.

Warm.

Protective.

Like a promise.

> “I’m here, my light.”

Silence filled the room again.

This time peaceful.

Harris slowly sat up while carefully lifting Harlina in his arms.

She moved slightly but continued sleeping.

Her head naturally rested against his chest.

Her arms stayed wrapped around him.

Like she refused to separate.

Harris chuckled softly.

> “Even while sleeping…”

> “You really trust me that much…”

He stood up slowly.

Carefully.

Making sure not to wake her.

Then he began walking toward the kitchen.

Step by step.

Gentle.

Steady.

Protective.

He carried her like she was the most precious treasure in existence.

And to him—

She was.

He entered the kitchen.

Prepared coffee with one hand while holding her carefully with the other.

The smell of fresh coffee slowly filled the house.

Morning warmth.

Soft light.

Quiet peace.

Harris finished making the coffee.

Then walked toward the sofa.

He sat down slowly.

Still carrying Harlina in his arms.

She now looked even cuter.

Sleeping quietly like a newborn baby.

Peaceful.

Warm.

Safe.

Harris looked at her.

And smiled again.

A real smile.

One that only appeared around her.

He gently kissed her forehead.

Then whispered.

> “Good sleep, my light.”

> “Sleep peacefully.”

> “I’ll protect this peace…”

His voice became quieter.

Softer.

But his eyes became sharp.

Determined.

Unbreakable.

> “No matter what happens…”

> “I will protect you.”

> “I will protect everyone.”

Outside…

Morning light continued rising.

But inside the quiet house—

Harris sat peacefully.

Holding Harlina.

Watching over her.

Like a guardian protecting his entire world.

And for this one beautiful moment…

The Void Walker was not a warrior.

Not a monster.

Not a savior.

He was simply—

Harris.

## **Part 1 – A Peaceful Morning Before the Storm**

The first rays of the morning sun slowly entered the hospital room through the curtains.

A gentle golden light filled the quiet room.

Outside, the city was just beginning another peaceful day.

Inside...

Everything was calm.

No alarms.

No battles.

No monsters.

Only silence.

---

Harris slowly opened his eyes.

For a few moments, he remained still.

He quietly looked toward the person sleeping beside him.

A gentle smile appeared on his face.

Harlina was still sleeping peacefully.

Just like every morning...

She was hugging him tightly.

Her silver-white hair rested gently over his shoulder.

One of her hands was holding his shirt as if she was afraid he might disappear again.

Her peaceful breathing filled the quiet room.

Harris simply watched her.

His heart became warm.

He whispered softly,

"...Good morning, my sweet girlfriend."

She remained asleep.

Still hugging him.

He smiled to himself.

"You really don't want to let go."

---

He slowly raised his hand.

Very gently...

He moved a few strands of her hair away from her face.

"So beautiful..."

he whispered.

Harlina smiled slightly in her sleep.

She unknowingly hugged him a little tighter.

Harris laughed quietly.

"I guess you're still dreaming."

Several peaceful minutes passed.

Neither of them needed to rush anywhere.

For Harris...

These peaceful moments were more precious than anything.

---

Finally...

He gently patted her shoulder.

"Harlina..."

No response.

He smiled.

"My sleepy princess..."

He softly called again.

"Harlina..."

She slowly moved.

Her eyelashes fluttered.

Little by little...

Her beautiful silver eyes opened.

She blinked twice.

The first person she saw...

Was Harris smiling at her.

Immediately...

A bright smile appeared on her face.

"...Good morning..."

Harris smiled back.

"Good morning."

---

She realized she was still hugging him.

Her cheeks turned slightly pink.

"...Did I hug you all night again?"

Harris nodded.

"The whole night."

She smiled shyly.

"...Sorry."

He gently shook his head.

"You don't have to apologize."

"I actually slept peacefully."

Her smile became even brighter.

She hugged him once again for a few more seconds.

"I missed sleeping like this."

Harris gently hugged her back.

"So did I."

The room once again became peaceful.

---

After a few quiet moments...

Harris looked at her with a small smile.

"Harlina..."

"Hmm?"

"I have a request."

She tilted her head curiously.

"What is it?"

He smiled.

"...Can you make me a coffee?"

She looked at him for a second.

Then suddenly laughed softly.

"You woke me up..."

"...just because you wanted coffee?"

Harris scratched his cheek.

"...Maybe."

She couldn't stop smiling.

"You really are impossible."

He laughed.

"I know."

---

She slowly understood something.

If she stayed hugging him...

The nurses, Lina and Mika would arrive soon.

She remembered exactly what Harris always did.

He always tried to save her from becoming embarrassed.

She smiled warmly.

"So that's why you woke me early."

"So I can sit on the chair before everyone comes."

Harris nodded with a gentle smile.

"You figured it out."

She smiled happily.

"You always think about me first."

He quietly replied,

"Because seeing you embarrassed makes me feel bad."

Her heart became warm again.

---

She slowly got off the bed.

Instead of staying beside him...

She walked to the chair near the bed.

Just like every morning.

She sat comfortably.

Crossing her hands on her lap.

Then looked toward Harris.

He smiled.

"There."

"My brave girlfriend."

She giggled.

"You really planned everything."

He nodded proudly.

"Of course."

---

The two of them began talking happily.

They talked about yesterday's trip.

About the beautiful fountain.

The flower valley.

The photographs they had taken together.

The flowers he had placed in her hair.

Harlina smiled while remembering everything.

"...Yesterday..."

"...was the happiest day of my life."

Harris quietly looked at her.

"I'm glad."

She shook her head.

"No."

"You don't understand."

"It wasn't because of the place."

"It was because..."

"...I spent the whole day with you."

Harris's heart became warm.

He smiled softly.

"...Thank you."

She smiled back.

"You promised another trip."

He nodded immediately.

"I won't cancel it."

She trusted him completely.

"I know."

---

Several more peaceful minutes passed.

The room remained silent except for their gentle conversation.

Then...

Harris looked at Harlina with a slightly nervous expression.

"Harlina..."

She tilted her head again.

"Yes?"

He smiled awkwardly.

"...Can I ask one more request?"

She became curious.

"What is it?"

He hesitated for a moment.

Then quietly said,

"...Can I have a cheek kiss?"

For a second...

Harlina completely froze.

Her face instantly became bright red.

"W-What...?"

Harris smiled awkwardly.

"If you don't want to..."

"...it's okay."

Before he could finish...

She slowly stood up.

Her heart was beating rapidly.

She walked toward him.

Stopped beside the bed.

Looked into his violet eyes.

Then...

Very gently...

She leaned forward.

And placed a soft, caring kiss on his right cheek.

It lasted only a brief moment.

When she moved back...

Her entire face had turned completely red.

She quickly covered her face with both hands.

"...I actually did it..."

Harris slowly touched his cheek.

A warm smile appeared on his face.

"...Thank you."

Harlina slowly lowered her hands.

Still blushing.

Then smiled shyly.

"...Finally..."

"...you are becoming a true boyfriend."

Harris laughed softly.

"Really?"

She nodded happily.

"Yes."

"No more acting like only my protector."

"You are my boyfriend."

"And I'm very happy."

Harris's smile became even brighter.

"I'll do my best."

She smiled warmly.

"I know you will."

The peaceful morning continued...

The sunlight filled the room...

And neither of them knew...

That in just a short while...

Everything was about to change.

All three nurses burst into laughter.

"I expected something serious."

"I can't argue with that."

Just then...

"Good morning."

"Good morning."

Then smiled knowingly.

"You look really happy today."

Harlina smiled softly.

"I am."

Lina looked between the two of them several times.

Then suddenly grinned.

"Something happened before we arrived..."

Both Harris and Harlina froze for a second.

Lina immediately noticed.

"I knew it."

Mika laughed.

"You found something."

---

The youngest nurse smiled mischievously.

"We arrived a little earlier than you two."

Lina became even more curious.

"Oh?"

"What happened?"

The youngest nurse pointed toward Harlina.

"Our princess here..."

"...gave someone a very sweet good morning."

Harlina's eyes widened.

"N-Nurse!"

The second nurse couldn't stop smiling.

"And someone asked very politely."

Mika immediately understood.

She covered her mouth.

"...No way..."

Lina's eyes sparkled.

"Harris!"

"You actually asked?"

Harris smiled awkwardly.

"...Maybe."

Lina laughed loudly.

"Our emotionless hero actually requested something romantic."

Mika nodded dramatically.

"This is history."

---

Harlina immediately covered her face.

"...Please stop..."

Lina walked closer.

"So..."

"Did you really give him a cheek kiss?"

Harlina became completely red.

"...Y-Yes..."

Lina clapped happily.

"Finally!"

Mika smiled warmly.

"Our Harris is slowly becoming a proper boyfriend."

The youngest nurse nodded proudly.

"We witnessed everything."

The second nurse added with a smile,

"It was adorable."

The eldest nurse laughed softly.

"And our little princess almost melted from embarrassment."

Everyone laughed together.

Harlina couldn't take it anymore.

She quickly stood up.

Walked behind Harris.

Then gently hid behind his shoulder.

Only half of her face was visible.

He gently reached back and held her hand.

"It's okay."

"They're only teasing."

She nodded slowly.

"I know..."

"But..."

"...it's embarrassing."

Harris smiled warmly.

"You don't have to hide."

She quietly shook her head.

"I'll stay here for a little longer."

Lina laughed.

"See?"

"She always hides behind you."

Mika smiled.

"And Harris always protects her."

The nurses nodded together.

"That's true."

---

The room filled with gentle laughter once again.

For a few peaceful minutes...

There were no thoughts of battles.

No thoughts of monsters.

No thoughts of fragments.

Only warm smiles...

Friendly teasing...

And the peaceful happiness of being together.

But...

Without anyone noticing...

Harris's smile slowly faded.

His eyes suddenly became unfocused.

He stopped responding.

His body became completely still.

Everyone looked at him.

"Harris...?"

No response.

Harlina gently held his arm.

"Harris?"

He remained frozen.

The room slowly fell silent...

## **Part 3 – The Creator's Warning**

The cheerful atmosphere inside the hospital room disappeared in an instant.

Harris remained completely motionless.

But they were looking into empty space.

Not a single blink.

Not a single movement.

Everyone froze.

"Then why is he frozen again?"

"This happened before..."

She gently rubbed his hand.

"Please come back..."

"I'm here."

"I'll wait."

Inside Harris's consciousness...

Everything became white.

An endless white world.

No sky.

No ground.

Only endless light.

He looked around.

"...Again?"

**"Yes."**

Another calm voice followed.

**"Welcome back."**

"The Creator..."

"The Third Voice."

"So..."

"Why do I suddenly freeze like this every time?"

"This..."

"is your reminder."

Harris looked confused.

"...Reminder?"

The Creator nodded.

"Yes."

"Your training reminder."

"You become completely absorbed in protecting everyone."

"You spend your time carrying burdens."

"You forget something important."

Harris quietly lowered his head.

"...Training."

The Creator nodded.

"You know the answer."

---

The Third Voice spoke calmly.

"You haven't trained seriously."

Harris sighed.

"...You're right."

"I couldn't."

The Creator smiled kindly.

"I know."

"You are still just a child."

"You carry responsibilities that even adults cannot bear."

"You protect cities."

"You fight fragments."

"You save lives."

"You comfort everyone."

"You even smile while hiding your own pain."

Harris lowered his eyes.

"I only did what I had to."

---

The Creator looked directly into him.

"But..."

"That isn't enough anymore."

Harris slowly looked up.

"The next enemies..."

"...are beyond your current strength."

Silence filled the white world.

Harris understood immediately.

---

He asked quietly,

"The next fragments..."

"The Corruption Fragment..."

"And..."

"The Destruction Fragment."

The Creator nodded.

"Correct."

"The Black Fragment."

"The Gray Fragment."

"They are much stronger than the fragments you have already defeated."

Harris remained silent.

---

The Third Voice continued,

"If you fight them with your current strength..."

"You will lose."

Harris clenched his fists.

"So..."

"I need to become stronger."

The Creator nodded.

"Not only stronger."

"You must begin controlling the suit."

Harris looked surprised.

"The suit..."

"The Legacy Suit?"

The Creator answered,

"Yes."

"You have only been using its power."

"You have never truly controlled it."

---

The white space became quiet again.

Harris looked down.

"...I understand."

"But..."

"I don't have enough time."

The Creator smiled.

"You will."

Harris looked up immediately.

"What do you mean?"

---

The Creator raised one hand.

After the Corruption Fragment...

After the Destruction Fragment...

"You will receive a gift."

Harris listened carefully.

"A period of peace."

"Approximately..."

"Three to five months."

Harris's eyes widened.

"...Really?"

The Creator smiled.

"Yes."

"You will finally have time."

"Time to train."

"Time to rest."

"Time to smile."

"Time to enjoy life."

Harris slowly smiled.

"So..."

"I can finally keep my promises."

---

He remembered Harlina smiling.

*"Next week we go on a trip."*

He remembered the lion cubs.

He remembered the soldiers.

He remembered everyone waiting for him.

A gentle smile appeared on his face.

"I'll use that time well."

The Creator nodded proudly.

"I know you will."

---

The Third Voice stepped forward.

"But remember..."

"This peace..."

"is only temporary."

Harris's smile slowly faded.

"...I know."

The Third Voice continued,

"After opening the Gate..."

"Your true journey begins."

The endless white world became silent again.

---

Harris took a deep breath.

"I won't think about that now."

"I'll focus on what's in front of me."

"I'll protect everyone."

"I'll grow stronger."

"I'll master the suit."

"And..."

"I'll return safely."

The Creator smiled warmly.

"That answer..."

"is exactly why you are our chosen one."

---

The white world slowly began disappearing.

The Creator raised his hand.

"Now..."

"Return."

"Everyone is waiting for you."

The Third Voice added one final sentence.

"Don't make your loved ones wait too long."

---

The light disappeared.

---

Inside the hospital room...

Exactly twenty minutes had passed.

Harlina was still holding Harris's hand.

She had never let go.

The nurses...

Lina...

Mika...

All of them were quietly watching him.

Suddenly—

Harris blinked.

His eyes regained focus.

He looked around in confusion.

"...Why is everyone staring at me?"

The room became silent for one second.

Then everyone sighed with relief.

Harlina smiled with tears forming in her eyes.

"Harris..."

"You're finally back..."

Only for a brief moment.

Then he moved back again.

---

Harlina's eyes became wide.

Her entire face turned bright red.

She immediately covered both cheeks with her hands.

She stared at Harris in complete embarrassment.

"H-Harris..."

---

He smiled innocently.

"Hm?"

---

She couldn't believe what had just happened.

She softly touched her forehead.

Then touched her cheek.

She looked at him again.

"D-Did..."

"...did you just kiss..."

"...my cheek..."

"...and my forehead?"

---

Harris smiled confidently.

"Yes."

---

For several long seconds...

Harlina simply stared at him.

Then...

A beautiful smile slowly appeared.

She shook her head while laughing softly.

"You..."

"You have now become completely romantic."

---

Harris laughed quietly.

"I'll take that as a compliment."

---

Harlina nodded immediately.

"It is."

"A very big compliment."

---

Harris smiled.

Then, being careful not to strain his injuries, he gently slipped one arm behind her back and the other beneath her knees just enough to support her comfortably.

"Come here."

Before she could ask why...

He carefully lifted her.

Harlina instinctively wrapped one arm around his shoulder for balance.

"H-Harris..."

"You lifted me again."

---

He smiled.

"I wanted my sweet girlfriend to sit beside me."

---

He carefully placed her on the bed beside him.

Not too close.

Just comfortably beside him.

The two of them sat shoulder to shoulder.

The morning sunlight gently shone across both of them.

Neither hurried to speak.

They simply enjoyed the peaceful silence.

After everything that had happened...

After the battles...

After the tears...

After the fear...

This quiet morning together felt more valuable than any victory.

Harris smiled.

Harlina smiled back.

Then the two of them continued talking peacefully about ordinary things, enjoying the calm morning together, completely unaware that this peaceful moment would soon be interrupted.

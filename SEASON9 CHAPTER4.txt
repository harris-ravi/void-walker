---

“You laughed a lot.”

---

Silence.

---

Harris’s smile softened.

---

“…I did.”

---

Harlina looked at him.

Then gently touched his face.

---

“You needed that.”

---

Harris looked at her.

Silently.

---

Then—

His smile faded slightly.

---

Harlina noticed instantly.

---

The nightmare.

It had returned to his eyes.

---

Fear.

Pain.

Silent terror.

---

She moved closer.

---

“What happened?”

---

Silence.

---

Harris looked away.

Toward the window.

Toward the night sky.

---

His voice came quietly.

Heavy.

---

“…I’m scared.”

---

Harlina froze.

---

Because those words…

Were extremely rare.

---

Harris almost never admitted fear.

---

He clenched his fists.

---

“The dream felt too real.”

---

Silence.

---

“I keep seeing it.”

---

His breathing slowed.

Heavy.

---

“Omega.”

---

He closed his eyes.

---

“You dying.”

---

Pain filled his voice.

---

“I can’t erase it.”

---

Harlina’s heart hurt.

---

She gently held his hands.

---

Then placed them against her heartbeat.

---

Thump.

Thump.

Thump.

---

She looked directly into his eyes.

---

“I’m here.”

---

Silence.

---

“Alive.”

---

Her voice softened.

Warm.

Strong.

---

“And I’ll keep saying it until you believe it.”

---

Tears formed in Harris’s eyes.

Small.

Barely visible.

---

Harlina moved closer.

Then hugged him tightly.

---

“You don’t need to fight fear alone.”

---

Silence.

---

“Let me carry it too.”

---

That sentence broke something inside him.

---

The walls.

The burden.

The fear.

---

Harris hugged her back.

Tightly.

Desperately.

---

Like someone afraid of losing everything.

---

His voice trembled.

Very softly.

---

“…Don’t leave me alone.”

---

Harlina’s eyes filled with tears.

---

She held him tighter.

---

“Never.”

---

No hesitation.

No fear.

Only certainty.

---

“Never, Harris.”

---

Silence.

Warm silence.

Healing silence.

---

Slowly—

His heartbeat calmed.

His breathing stabilized.

---

The fear remained.

But now—

He wasn’t carrying it alone.

---

Tonight…

That was enough.

---

Harlina kissed his forehead.

Then his cheek.

Then softly rested against him.

---

“Sleep.”

---

Harris nodded.

---

They lay down together.

Wrapped in each other’s warmth.

---

Outside—

The stars shined brightly.

Quietly.

Watching.

---

But far away—

In darkness.

Beyond countless universes.

A sinister smile appeared.

---

Omega Void Emperor.

---

Watching.

Waiting.

Smiling.

---

The war was coming.

Closer.

Closer.

Closer.

---

And the next birthday…

Would not be peaceful.

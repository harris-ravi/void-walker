## **PART 1 – The Birthday Surprise**

The warm morning sunlight poured through the hospital entrance.

Harris took a slow breath.

Beside him, Harlina gently supported him with one arm around his back while holding his forearm carefully. Even though most of his injuries had healed, his old shoulder and leg injuries still made every step slow.

Lina and Mika quietly followed behind them.

The three nurses stood near the entrance with warm smiles.

"Ready?" Harlina asked softly.

Harris looked at her and smiled.

"Yeah."

Together...

They took the final step.

The automatic glass doors slowly slid open.

Fresh morning air entered the hospital.

Harris slowly stepped outside.

His foot touched the ground.

He lifted his head...

And suddenly—

He froze.

His violet eyes widened.

Standing before him...

Was almost the entire town.

Hundreds of people filled the street outside the hospital.

Children.

Parents.

Grandparents.

Teachers.

Doctors.

Shopkeepers.

Workers.

Students.

Old people leaning on walking sticks.

Young children holding colorful balloons.

Everyone was smiling brightly.

Many people held beautifully wrapped gift boxes.

Some carried handmade bouquets.

Some held greeting cards.

Some children even held small drawings they had made themselves.

The entire street had become a sea of smiling faces.

For a few seconds...

Harris couldn't even breathe.

Silence surrounded him.

Then...

All at once...

The entire crowd raised their voices.

## **"HAPPY BIRTHDAY, HARRIS!!"**

The sound echoed throughout the city.

Birds flew into the sky.

Children laughed happily.

Confetti floated gently through the morning air.

Harris stood completely motionless.

"..."

His lips slowly trembled.

"My..."

"My birthday...?"

He had completely forgotten.

After everything that had happened...

The battles...

The hospital...

The pain...

He hadn't remembered the date.

But...

Everyone else had.

A small tear slowly formed in the corner of his eye.

Then another.

A single tear rolled gently down his cheek.

Harlina noticed immediately.

She smiled warmly.

Without saying anything at first...

She gently wiped away the tear with her thumb.

Then she stepped slightly closer to him.

Looking directly into his eyes, she smiled with all the warmth in her heart.

"Happy Birthday, Harris."

Her soft voice reached him more deeply than the cheers around him.

Harris looked at her.

Then smiled.

A genuine...

Peaceful...

Heartfelt smile.

"Thank you..."

His voice was barely above a whisper.

Children immediately ran toward him.

"Harris!"

"Happy Birthday!"

"We made this for you!"

A little boy held out a small handmade wooden star.

A little girl proudly showed him a hand-drawn picture of Harris protecting the city.

"We drew you!"

Harris slowly knelt as much as his leg allowed.

Even though it caused a little pain...

He didn't care.

He smiled at them.

"These are beautiful."

The children's faces lit up with excitement.

More people slowly stepped forward.

An elderly grandfather smiled.

"Our protector..."

"May you always stay safe."

An old grandmother gently held out a knitted scarf.

"I made this with my own hands."

"I hope it keeps you warm."

Harris accepted it with both hands.

"Thank you..."

"It means a lot."

Teachers stepped forward.

Students bowed respectfully.

Shopkeepers smiled.

Everywhere he looked...

There were smiling faces.

Not because he was the legendary Void Walker.

Not because he was powerful.

But because...

Today...

They simply wanted to celebrate Harris.

The person who had protected all of them.

Harlina quietly watched him.

She smiled softly.

Seeing him surrounded by so much love...

Made her happier than anything.

Lina leaned toward Mika and whispered,

"I've never seen him smile like this."

Mika nodded with a gentle smile.

"...Me neither."

The three nurses stood near the entrance.

The youngest nurse wiped a tear from her own eye.

"He deserves this..."

The senior nurse smiled proudly.

"Yes."

"He deserves every bit of it."

Harris slowly looked around once more.

His eyes were still slightly wet.

He placed one hand over his heart.

"...Thank you..."

"I'll never forget today."

The entire crowd erupted into cheers once again.

The morning sky seemed brighter than ever.

For the first time in many days...

There was no pain on Harris's face.

Only gratitude.

Only happiness.

And only the warmth of the people he had sworn to protect.

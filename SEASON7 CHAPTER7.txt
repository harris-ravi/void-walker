Very afraid.

Like a captured animal.

Like someone about to face execution.

"What are they doing?"

Lunar smiled.

"You'll understand."

A chair was placed in the center.

Harris was tied.

Again.

His soul nearly left his body.

Then Lina announced proudly.

"Truth or Dare."

The entire world became silent.

Harris froze.

Universe 6 Harris blinked.

"What?"

Lunar laughed.

"You are about to witness something scarier than Omega."

Universe 6 Harris thought he was joking.

Not even slightly.

The poor boy looked like a prisoner surrounded by judges.

"Truth or Dare?"

Harris sighed.

"Truth."

A dangerous smile.

The kind that meant trouble.

Mika immediately attacked.

"How much do you love Harlina?"

Harris froze.

Universe 6 Harris nearly choked on his drink.

Watching.

"...a lot."

"THAT'S NOT AN ANSWER!"

Then he quietly spoke.

"If she was in danger..."

Silence.

His smile disappeared.

"I would sacrifice everything."

Everyone became quiet.

The atmosphere suddenly changed.

Because nobody doubted him.

Not even for a second.

Harlina lowered her head.

Tears quietly formed.

Harris continued.

"I would give my powers."

"I would give my future."

"I would give my life."

"I would give everything."

The silence became heavier.

Universe 6 Harris looked at him.

Then slowly smiled.

He understood.

Because he would do the same.

"Why do you love her?"

"I refuse."

"No."

"I refuse."

"No."

"I refuse."

SLAP.

"ANSWER."

Everyone laughed.

Harris surrendered.

Then slowly looked toward Harlina.

"I love her because..."

He paused.

"...she never left."

Silence.

"Everyone sees the Void Walker."

"Everyone sees the hero."

"Everyone sees the fighter."

"But she always saw Harris."

Harlina's eyes widened.

The girls smiled.

Universe 6 Harris quietly listened.

"I was selfish."

"I pushed people away."

"I hid injuries."

"I lied."

"I carried everything alone."

"But she never stopped worrying."

A real smile.

"That is why."

Too real.

Too emotional.

"What is your favorite thing about her?"

"Her caring personality."

Everyone nodded.

Then Lina grinned.

"And?"

Harris froze.

"No."

"And?"

"No."

"AND?"

He sighed.

Then pointed at Harlina.

"Her second personality."

Everyone became curious.

"Second personality?"

Harris nodded.

"The Slapping Mode."

Harlina immediately attacked him.

SLAP.

The laughter doubled.

Universe 6 Harris stared.

Then slowly moved away from Harlina.

A survival instinct.

Everyone noticed.

More laughter followed.

After almost an hour of teasing...

The game finally ended.

At least for Harris.

Or so he thought.

Because Universe 6 Harris suddenly stood up.

His expression had changed.

Lunar noticed.

"What happened?"

Universe 6 Harris looked toward Harris.

Then quietly asked.

"...why does he look so tired?"

Silence.

The camp became quiet.

Very quiet.

Then took a deep breath.

"You want to know?"

Universe 6 Harris nodded.

Lunar slowly began speaking.

And for the first time...

Someone told Harris's entire story.

The real story.

Not the legend.

Not the hero.

Not the Void Walker.

Just Harris.

Universe 6 Harris listened.

Everyone listened.

The boy raised by Koji.

The lonely child who smiled despite having nothing.

The child who never wanted others to feel alone.

The boy who carried pain silently.

The boy who hid every wound.

The boy who smiled while suffering.

The boy who trained until he collapsed.

The boy who spent nights awake protecting everyone.

The boy who sacrificed himself repeatedly.

The boy who endured the Crimson Eclipse.

The boy who kept pretending everything was fine.

The boy who injured himself just to speak with the Creator.

The boy who feared losing everyone more than death itself.

The boy who thought everyone would be safer without him.

Nobody spoke.

Universe 6 Harris lowered his head.

Then more.

Then more.

Until he could no longer stop them.

Because he finally understood.

The reason Harris smiled.

The reason Harris never rested.

The reason Harris kept sacrificing himself.

The reason Harris looked broken.

"You carried all that..."

And grabbed his shoulder.

"But you're stronger than me."

The laughter from the camp slowly faded as the night grew deeper.

Nobody was running.

The more unbelievable it felt.

Some were rulers.

Some were survivors.

None of them carried pain the way this Harris did.

Looking at the water.

Always thinking.

Always carrying something alone.

"Are you scared?"

"...every day."

"I am terrified."

Because this was not the answer he expected.

"I am not afraid of dying."

"I am afraid of surviving alone."

Silence.

The words felt heavier than any battle.

The version lowered his head.

He understood that fear.

Because he was already living it.

His universe was gone.

His friends were gone.

His Harlina was gone.

And every morning he woke up alone.

For the first time...

Neither version of Harris spoke.

Because both understood each other's pain.

A few moments later—

"HARRIS!"

Then slowly turned.

Harlina stood there.

Arms crossed.

Angry.

Very angry.

Mika.

Reku.

And Roku.

All of them looking suspicious.

Harris immediately stood.

"Good night."

Then tried walking away.

"STOP."

He froze.

Universe 6 Harris watched with fear.

The legendary Void Walker...

The strongest Void Walker in existence...

Had been defeated by a single word.

Harlina slowly approached.

Then grabbed Harris by the hoodie.

"You are sleeping with everyone tonight."

"No."

"Yes."

"No."

"Yes."

Universe 6 Harris slowly stepped backward.

He wanted absolutely no part in this battle.

Lunar grabbed his shoulder.

"You too."

"You too."

The girls won.

As usual.

"No."

"No."

"How many times does this happen?"

"You have no idea."

Both silently agreed that this situation was hopeless.

The girls finally settled down.

One by one everyone fell asleep.

The camp became quiet.

Only the fountain could be heard now.

Universe 6 Harris remained awake.

Lunar remained awake.

And Harris remained awake.

As usual.

The version looked toward him.

"You don't sleep much, do you?"

"Not recently."

The version nodded.

He already knew why.

Because every time Harris closed his eyes...

He saw the same thing.

Death.

Loss.

Failure.

His friends disappearing.

Omega Void Emperor winning.

The future he feared more than anything.

Eventually even Universe 6 Harris fell asleep.

Only Harris remained awake.

Across the fountain.

Across his friends.

Koji.

Lunar.

Reku.

Roku.

Lina.

Mika.

Then finally...

Harlina.

His smile softened.

His eyes became emotional.

Because they looked happy.

Peaceful.

Safe.

Exactly what he wanted.

*"Leave now."*

*"Go alone."*

*"Protect them."*

The old plan was returning.

The same plan.

Leave during the night.

Find the books alone.

Fight Omega alone.

Carry everything alone.

The plan felt safe.

The plan felt familiar.

Then—

She was sleeping peacefully.

Still holding part of his sleeve.

Even in sleep.

As if she was afraid he would disappear.

The voice inside him became weaker.

Then another voice appeared.

Buddy.

"You promised."

Buddy continued.

"You accepted my condition."

His hands trembled.

"I know."

"Then stop trying to leave them."

Silence.

For the first time...

Harris had no answer.

Because deep inside...

He knew Buddy was right.

The night continued.

Eventually exhaustion finally defeated him.

For the first time in many days...

Harris actually slept.

The first rays of sunlight touched the campsite.

Before anyone else woke—

Instantly.

Years of training.

Years of danger.

Years of war.

Without waking anyone.

Stronger.

More focused.

Everyone's favorite weapon against mornings.

Universe 6 Harris woke first.

He followed the smell.

Then froze.

Harris was already preparing everything.

The version smiled.

"You never stop working."

Harris laughed.

"You noticed?"

"You are impossible."

A few moments later...

The two began training together.

A simple sparring session.

At least that was the plan.

Very fast.

Endurance training.

Pain training.

Lunar.

Because the Void Walkers were beginning to unite.

And that was something even he feared.

# **VOID WALKER — SEASON 3**

# **CHAPTER 5 — THE FINAL GOODBYE**

# **PART 1 — Two Days Before the Crimson Eclipse**

The morning sky was unusually quiet.

No birds sang.

No gentle breeze carried the sounds of ordinary life.

Even nature itself seemed to understand...

Something unimaginable was approaching.

High above the world...

The moon carried a faint crimson tint.

Almost invisible...

Yet impossible for Harris to ignore.

He stood alone on the highest platform of the **Void Throne**, silently watching the sky.

The cold wind gently moved his black hair.

His violet eyes remained fixed on the distant moon.

After several moments...

He quietly spoke.

**"...Only two days remain."**

His words disappeared into the endless silence.

Behind him...

The Second Void Diary floated gently.

The ancient pages turned by themselves before finally stopping.

The same warning was written across the glowing page.

**「The Crimson Eclipse approaches.」**

Harris slowly closed the diary.

His expression remained calm.

But inside...

His heart grew heavier than ever before.

---

Far beyond Earth's atmosphere...

Tiny crimson cracks slowly spread across space itself.

The barrier separating worlds had already begun weakening.

Invisible distortions rippled through reality.

Ordinary people couldn't see them.

But Harris could.

He understood exactly what they meant.

The countdown...

Had truly begun.

---

He slowly clenched his fist.

*"Every second matters now..."*

*"If even one innocent person remains outside..."*

*"They'll die."*

His determination became absolute.

Without another moment of hesitation...

He turned around.

"...Buddy."

A small blue hologram appeared beside him.

**"Yes, Harris."**

"...Begin Phase One."

Buddy immediately understood.

**"Sanctuary evacuation?"**

Harris nodded.

"...Immediately."

"...Every civilian must reach the Sanctuary before the Crimson Eclipse."

**"Understood."**

Within seconds...

Warning systems activated across every connected communication network.

Emergency broadcasts echoed throughout nearby cities.

---

A few minutes later...

Everyone gathered at the Void Throne.

Koji.

Harlina.

Lina.

Mika.

Lunar.

Roku.

Reku.

Jekuna.

Izuku.

Every one of them immediately noticed Harris' serious expression.

Koji stepped forward.

"...Is it time?"

Harris quietly nodded.

"...Yes."

"...We have only two days."

Silence covered the group.

Everyone instinctively looked toward the faint crimson moon.

Even without speaking...

They understood.

The peaceful days...

Were ending.

---

Harris looked at every one of them.

"...Listen carefully."

"...Beginning today..."

"...Everything changes."

"...Our first priority is no longer training."

"...It's saving lives."

Everyone listened silently.

"...Every civilian..."

"...Every family..."

"...Every child..."

"...must reach the Sanctuary."

"...No one stays behind."

His voice carried absolute certainty.

Nobody questioned the order.

Every person answered together.

**"Understood!"**

---

Within the hour...

The evacuation began.

The streets filled with movement.

Families packed essential belongings.

Parents carried children.

Volunteers guided frightened civilians.

Vehicles continuously traveled toward the Sanctuary.

Harris personally walked through every street.

Not as the Void Walker.

But as someone protecting his people.

Whenever frightened children saw him...

Their fear disappeared.

One little boy tugged gently on Harris' sleeve.

"...Big brother..."

"...Will the monsters come here...?"

Harris slowly knelt until they were at eye level.

He smiled warmly.

"...Not while I'm here."

The little boy smiled immediately.

"...Then I'm not scared anymore."

His mother quietly wiped tears from her eyes.

"...Thank you..."

Harris simply nodded before continuing onward.

---

Nearby...

Harlina helped elderly citizens board evacuation vehicles.

Her long silver hair gently flowed in the wind.

Her silver eyes remained calm despite the fear around her.

She comforted frightened mothers.

She carried children who had become exhausted.

She never stopped smiling.

Even when her own heart trembled.

Lina and Mika worked beside her tirelessly.

Koji organized evacuation routes.

Lunar directed rescue teams.

Roku and Reku transported supplies.

Jekuna and Izuku protected every convoy leaving the city.

For the first time...

Everyone fought together...

Without drawing a single weapon.

Their mission was simple.

**Save everyone.**

---

As evening slowly arrived...

Thousands of civilians had already reached the Sanctuary.

Children laughed after seeing the enormous new world Harris had created.

Beautiful forests.

Crystal-clear rivers.

Mountains.

Safe homes.

Green fields.

For a brief moment...

Hope returned.

Harris quietly watched them from a distance.

A small smile appeared.

Harlina walked beside him.

"...You did it..."

"...They're smiling again."

Harris continued watching the children play.

"...Not yet."

"...There are still many people outside."

He turned toward the distant city.

The faint crimson moon reflected in his violet eyes.

"...Until every last person is safe..."

"...I won't stop."

Without another word...

He began walking toward the next evacuation zone.

Harlina smiled softly.

Then followed beside him.

Just as she always had.

Together...

They disappeared into the evening light...

Continuing the mission to save every possible life before destiny arrived.

"...No."

"...Even so..."

Thousands of people were finally safe.

Still homes.

Still people.

Still lives waiting.

"...We're not finished."

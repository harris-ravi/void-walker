Tired from chasing Harris every time he tried to disappear.

Frozen.

Completely frozen.

Then toward the outside.

Then toward the forest.

"Nowhere."

"Lie."

"I'm serious."

"Lie."

"I'm not lying."

"Lie."

Everyone laughed.

The problem wasn't that Lunar knew him.

The problem was that Lunar knew him too well.

"Tonight you sleep there."

Harris looked.

Then froze.

The girls were sitting there.

Harlina.

Lina.

Mika.

And the others.

All staring directly at him.

A chill ran down his spine.

"Why?"

Lunar smiled.

"Because you can't escape."

Completely betrayed.

"Master..."

"I support Lunar."

"Don't look at me."

He looked toward Roku.

Roku immediately pointed toward Lunar.

"Him."

Harris lowered his head.

There was no hope.

No allies.

No escape.

Harlina suddenly stood up and grabbed his hand.

"Come."

"Why are you holding my hand?"

"Because if I don't, you'll disappear."

The atmosphere became peaceful.

Stories started filling the night.

Everyone began talking about old memories.

The academy days.

Training accidents.

Childhood mistakes.

Funny moments.

The war.

The books.

Omega Void Emperor.

The future.

Everything disappeared.

Then Lina ruined it.

"Yes?"

"How much do you love Harlina?"

Silence.

Complete silence.

Even the wind outside seemed to stop.

Harris froze.

Harlina froze.

He knew chaos was about to begin.

"The important kind."

"No."

"Yes."

"No."

"Yes."

"No."

Everyone laughed.

"I refuse."

"You don't have that option."

"I do."

"You don't."

"I do."

"You don't."

Another pillow.

More laughter.

Harris looked toward the ceiling.

Why was his life like this?

He had fought gods.

He had fought monsters.

He had survived impossible wars.

Yet somehow this was harder.

Much harder.

Mika suddenly handed him a glass of water.

He became suspicious.

Very suspicious.

"Why are you smiling?"

"Drink."

"No."

"Drink."

"No."

Harlina grabbed the glass.

"Harris."

His survival instincts activated.

He immediately drank it.

Everyone laughed.

"Answer now."

A deep sigh.

The sigh of a man accepting defeat.

He slowly looked toward Harlina.

The moment their eyes met, her face became red.

His face also became red.

Everyone immediately noticed.

And started laughing again.

"I love her..."

The tent became silent.

"...more than myself."

Nobody laughed.

Nobody joked.

Nobody interrupted.

Because the honesty in his voice was impossible to ignore.

Harlina slowly lowered her head.

Her heart was beating so fast she thought everyone could hear it.

"If she was ever in danger..."

His voice became softer.

"...I would save her even if it cost my life."

The smile disappeared from everyone's faces.

Harlina's eyes slowly became wet.

Because she knew.

Those words were not romantic words.

Those words were reality.

Harris would truly do it.

Without hesitation.

Without fear.

Without regret.

That was exactly what scared her.

"Harris..."

"Yeah?"

"Why do you love her?"

His smile softened.

The playful atmosphere disappeared.

For a brief moment, only honesty remained.

"Because she never left."

Everyone became quiet.

He looked down.

Thinking about the past.

Thinking about every painful memory.

Thinking about every lonely night.

Thinking about every battle.

"Even when I wanted everyone to leave me alone."

His voice became quieter.

"Even when I pushed people away."

"Even when I acted like an idiot."

Everyone laughed.

Even Harris laughed.

More laughter.

Because she knew exactly what he meant.

She remembered every time he tried to carry everything alone.

Every time he smiled while suffering.

Every time he lied about being okay.

Every time he tried to protect everyone at the cost of himself.

She hated that part of him.

And loved it at the same time.

"What is your favorite thing about her?"

"Her kindness."

Harlina froze.

Everyone immediately started making teasing noises.

"Ooooooh."

"Look at her face."

"She's turning red."

The laughter became even louder.

"She worries too much."

Everyone nodded.

"True."

"I do not."

Nobody believed her.

Not even herself.

"Two modes?"

Harlina narrowed her eyes.

Harris suddenly felt danger.

Extreme danger.

The survival instinct of a Void Walker activated.

Unfortunately it was too late.

Everyone was waiting.

"The first mode..."

He pointed toward her.

"...is Love Mode."

The girls immediately cheered.

Harlina covered her face.

Her embarrassment reached critical levels.

"And the second mode?"

Harris looked directly at death.

Then answered.

"Slapping Mode."

The tent exploded.

Everyone laughed so hard they nearly fell over.

Even Koji couldn't stop laughing.

Harlina grabbed three pillows and launched all of them directly at Harris.

One.

Two.

Three.

Direct hits.

Everyone laughed even harder.

Harris surrendered completely.

The night continued with laughter.

A small part of Harris remained quiet.

Because deep inside his heart...

He was wondering how long he could keep protecting these smiles.

How long before Omega Void Emperor arrived.

How long before the peace ended.

How long before he would be forced to leave.

He smiled so nobody would notice.

But behind that smile...

Memorizing their faces.

Just in case.

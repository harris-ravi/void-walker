## CHAPTER 3 — PART 1

### **HARRIS' HUNGRY MORNING**

Morning slowly arrived over Ravynm.

Soft sunlight entered through the window of Harisa and Zevian's home.

The peaceful house was still quiet.

Harris was sleeping beside his mother.

His teddy bear was resting near his tiny body.

His little hand was still close to Harisa.

On the other side, Kira was sleeping peacefully.

Everything was calm.

For a while, Harris continued sleeping.

Then his purple eyes slowly opened.

He stared at the ceiling.

He looked around.

His mother was still sleeping beside him.

His big sister Kira was sleeping on the other side.

Harris slowly pushed himself upward.

He managed to sit.

He looked around the room with curiosity.

His little face was calm.

Then suddenly...

his stomach became hungry.

Harris looked toward Harisa.

He reached his tiny hand toward her.

Nothing happened.

He moved closer.

Then he made a small sound.

"Mm..."

Harisa remained asleep.

Harris moved closer again.

His hunger became stronger.

His little eyes became watery.

He began crying.

"Mom..."

Harisa immediately woke.

She looked down and saw her son crying.

"My Harris?"

She quickly sat up.

"What happened, my baby?"

Harris moved his tiny hand toward his mother.

Harisa understood immediately.

"You're hungry?"

Harris cried softly and moved his hand again.

Harisa gently picked him up.

"Alright, my little Harris."

She held him close.

"Mommy understands."

She carefully fed him.

Harris immediately became calm.

His crying stopped.

His tiny hands rested against his mother.

Harisa gently stroked his black hair.

"There you go..."

She smiled.

"Drink peacefully."

Harris drank happily.

His purple eyes slowly became relaxed.

Harisa looked at his peaceful face.

A warm smile appeared on her face.

"Every morning..."

She gently touched his cheek.

"...you wake up hungry."

Harris continued drinking.

Harisa laughed softly.

"But I don't mind."

She held him closer.

"Whenever you're hungry, Mommy will be here."

Harris made a tiny happy sound.

Harisa smiled.

---

After some time, Harris finished.

He looked up at Harisa.

His little face was completely happy.

Harisa gently cleaned him.

"Finished?"

Harris smiled.

Then he opened his mouth.

"Mom."

Harisa froze.

Her eyes widened.

She looked at him.

"What did you say?"

Harris smiled.

"Mom."

Harisa's expression immediately became emotional.

"My Harris..."

She gently hugged him.

"You said it again."

She held him close against her chest.

Harris laughed.

Harisa's eyes became warm.

"Say it again."

Harris looked at her.

"Mom."

Harisa smiled.

She kissed his forehead.

"Yes."

She hugged him again.

"Mommy is here."

Harris laughed happily.

---

After a few moments, Harris looked down.

He tried to move himself.

Harisa carefully placed him on the bed.

Harris sat there.

Then he looked toward his mother.

He slowly pushed his tiny hands against the mattress.

He tried to lift his body.

Harisa immediately noticed.

"Harris?"

Harris tried to stand.

His tiny legs trembled.

He managed to lift himself for only a moment.

Then—

he fell back onto the bed.

Harris became startled.

His eyes filled with tears.

He started crying.

Harisa immediately picked him up.

"Oh, my baby!"

She hugged him.

"Don't cry."

Harris buried his face against her.

Harisa gently stroked his hair.

"You tried to stand."

Harris sniffled.

"You don't have to stand yet."

She softly smiled.

"You can take your time."

Harris looked at her.

Harisa wiped his tears.

"You're still my little baby."

Harris slowly smiled again.

Harisa kissed his forehead.

"Whenever you're ready, you'll learn."

Harris made a tiny happy sound.

He leaned against her.

---

Kira slowly woke up.

She rubbed her eyes.

She looked toward Harisa.

"Mom?"

Harisa smiled.

"Good morning, Kira."

Kira looked at Harris.

"Is little brother awake?"

Harris looked toward her.

His face immediately brightened.

Kira smiled.

"Good morning, Harris."

Harris laughed.

Kira came closer.

She gently touched his hand.

Harris held her finger.

Kira smiled.

"He was crying?"

Harisa nodded.

"He was hungry."

Kira laughed softly.

"Again?"

Harisa smiled.

"Yes."

Kira looked at Harris.

"You're always hungry."

Harris laughed.

Kira gently touched his hair.

"Don't worry, little brother."

She smiled.

"You'll learn to stand soon."

Harris looked at her.

He smiled.

---

Harisa held Harris in one arm while preparing to start the morning.

Kira got ready for school.

Zevian was still resting.

Harris looked around the room.

His purple eyes were curious.

Then he looked at the doorway.

Planet Slayer slowly floated into the room.

The living sword quietly revolved around him.

Harris' face brightened.

He laughed.

Kira looked toward it.

"Planet Slayer is awake too."

Planet Slayer moved closer.

Harris touched it.

The sword gave a gentle vibration.

Harris laughed.

Harisa smiled.

"You two really understand each other."

Harris looked toward his mother.

"Mom."

Harisa smiled.

"Yes, my Harris."

Harris laughed again.

The peaceful morning continued.

But Harris had no idea that before this day ended...

he would speak more words that would make his entire family emotional.

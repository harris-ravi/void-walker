Soft rays of the morning sun slowly entered through the hospital window.

The golden light gently spread across the quiet room.

Everything was peaceful.

The city outside had begun another beautiful morning after the previous day's battle.

Inside the hospital room...

Silence.

Only the gentle sound of breathing could be heard.

Harris slowly opened his eyes.

For a few moments, he simply stared at the ceiling.

"...Morning already..."

He slowly turned his head.

A warm smile naturally appeared on his face.

Harlina was still sleeping peacefully.

She was hugging him tightly with both arms, resting her head gently against his shoulder.

Her silver-white hair flowed softly over the blanket.

Her peaceful expression made the entire room feel calm.

Harris quietly whispered,

"...You finally look peaceful."

He stayed completely still for several minutes.

He didn't want to wake her immediately.

After everything both of them had gone through...

Seeing her sleep without fear...

Made him happier than anything else.

He smiled softly.

"I wish every morning could be like this..."

A few more minutes passed.

Finally...

He gently lifted one hand.

Very carefully...

He softly patted her head.

"Harlina..."

She didn't wake up.

Instead...

She unknowingly hugged him even tighter.

Harris chuckled quietly.

"...Still sleepy?"

He gently stroked her hair once more.

"Harlina..."

"...Good morning."

Slowly...

Her eyelashes moved.

She let out a tiny sleepy sound.

"...Mmm..."

Her eyes slowly opened.

For a second...

She simply looked at Harris.

Then a gentle smile appeared on her face.

"...Good morning..."

She realized she was still hugging him.

Instead of moving away...

She smiled even more.

"...I slept really well."

Harris smiled warmly.

"I'm glad."

She looked into his eyes for a few quiet moments.

Neither of them spoke.

The peaceful silence itself was enough.

Finally...

Harris smiled awkwardly.

"...Can I ask something?"

She tilted her head.

"What is it?"

He scratched his cheek.

"...Coffee..."

She blinked.

"...Coffee?"

He nodded innocently.

"I suddenly feel like drinking coffee."

For a second...

Harlina simply stared at him.

Then...

She immediately understood.

He wasn't actually asking because of the coffee.

He was trying to stop both of them from becoming embarrassed after waking up while still hugging each other.

A tiny smile appeared on her face.

"So that's why..."

She slowly loosened her embrace.

She sat on the chair beside the bed.

Still smiling.

"...You're trying to save me from getting embarrassed."

Harris laughed softly.

"I thought it might help."

She giggled.

"You always think ahead."

He shrugged playfully.

"I try."

The room became filled with gentle laughter.

They talked quietly for several minutes.

About nothing important.

About the weather.

About the peaceful morning.

About how strange it felt that there wasn't an emergency waiting for them.

Harlina suddenly looked out the window.

"...It's beautiful today."

Harris nodded.

"Very beautiful."

She looked back at him.

Then...

Without saying anything...

She stood up.

Walked beside the bed.

And suddenly...

Wrapped both arms around him again.

A warm...

Gentle...

Loving hug.

Harris smiled.

"...Another morning hug?"

She nodded against his shoulder.

"...Yes."

"I missed hugging you like this."

He slowly wrapped his arms around her.

"I missed it too."

Both of them stayed like that quietly.

Feeling the warmth they had nearly lost forever.

After several peaceful moments...

Harlina softly asked,

"...When this war is finally over..."

"...Can we really go on our trip?"

Harris looked into her hopeful eyes.

This time...

He smiled confidently.

"No."

Harlina blinked in confusion.

"...No?"

Harris laughed.

"I changed my mind."

She became nervous.

"...Changed your mind?"

He gently held both of her hands.

Then smiled brightly.

"...We're not waiting until the war is over."

She froze.

He continued.

"Next week."

"We're going."

"Our trip..."

"...is confirmed."

"No changes."

"No excuses."

"No cancellations."

"I promise."

For several seconds...

Harlina simply stared at him.

Her beautiful silver eyes slowly became wet with happy tears.

"...Really?"

Harris nodded firmly.

"Really."

"I won't break this promise."

A bright smile slowly spread across her face.

The sadness that had once filled her eyes...

Was completely gone.

She happily nodded.

"...Okay."

"I'll believe you."

"I'll wait."

Harris smiled.

"That's my girl."

Harlina immediately blushed.

"...Sweet boyfriend."

**Knock... Knock...**

The hospital room door slowly opened.

The same three nurses entered the room carrying the morning supplies.

Harlina still hugging Harris.

"Oh my..."

The second nurse smiled.

"Looks like someone forgot we were coming this morning."

"It's nice seeing both of you smiling again."

"...Ah!"

"There she goes again."

"So shy."

"It's okay."

Still blushing deeply.

Like home.

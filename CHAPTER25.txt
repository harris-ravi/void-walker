She smiled knowingly.

"He finally slept peacefully."

Zero nodded.

"...Yes."

She accepted the tray.

Then spoke quietly.

"Sis..."

"Could you please not let anyone come in until he wakes up?"

"He needs this rest."

She smiled shyly.

"And..."

"...especially Lina and Mika."

Kira couldn't help smiling.

"I understand."

"I'll keep everyone busy."

"Take care of him."

Zero smiled gratefully.

"Thank you, Sis."

Kira gently patted her shoulder before walking away.

---

Zero quietly closed the door.

She carried the tray inside.

As she approached the bed...

Harris' sleeping arm once again found her waist.

With a sleepy motion...

He gently pulled her back beside him.

Zero laughed softly.

"I guess I'm not escaping."

Harlina giggled.

"He really wants us to stay."

The two settled comfortably beside him again.

---

Several peaceful minutes passed.

Then...

Harris slowly opened his eyes.

The first thing he saw...

Was Zero smiling warmly.

Then Harlina beside her.

Both of them were still close enough that their faces rested only a short distance from his.

He smiled sleepily.

"...Good morning."

"Good morning."

The two answered together.

---

Without saying another word...

Harris gently hugged them both again.

The three remained together quietly.

Neither Zero nor Harlina hurried him.

For a little while...

Nothing else mattered.

---

Finally...

Harris slowly sat up.

Even then...

One arm remained gently around Zero's waist.

The other rested around Harlina's waist.

Neither of them moved away.

Instead...

They leaned comfortably against him.

Zero handed him one of the warm cups.

"Your coffee."

"Thank you."

The three quietly enjoyed the peaceful morning together.

Between small smiles...

And comfortable silence.

---

After finishing the coffee...

Harris looked at both of them with a playful smile.

"...Can I ask for one more thing?"

The two looked at him curiously.

"What?"

He pointed toward his cheeks.

"...Good morning kisses?"

Their faces instantly became bright red.

"You..."

Zero laughed shyly.

Harlina hid her smile.

After exchanging a quick glance...

The two leaned forward together.

Each gave him a gentle kiss on the cheek.

Simple.

Warm.

Filled with affection.

---

Harris smiled happily.

"Thank you."

He took out his communicator.

"One memory before the next journey."

The three leaned together naturally.

Still smiling.

**Click.**

A photograph captured the moment forever.

---

Harris put the communicator aside.

He gently returned the affection by kissing each of them softly on the cheek.

"Now we're even."

Zero and Harlina smiled brightly.

Without another word...

They wrapped their arms around him again.

Harris gently drew them closer with his arms still resting comfortably around their waists.

Neither of them wanted to move.

Zero smiled.

"I like this..."

Harlina nodded.

"This hug..."

"And being close to you..."

"It feels safe."

Harris smiled warmly.

"Then..."

"We'll treasure moments like this whenever we can."

He gently held them a little closer.

The three lay back comfortably on the bed.

Peacefully.

Smiling.

Ready to face whatever waited beyond the next Fragment...

Together.

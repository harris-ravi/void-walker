## PART 1 — **A QUIET EVENING WITH MOM**

The evening settled peacefully over the city.

After everything that had happened during the day, the house of Harisa and Zevian was calm again.

Inside the warm family room, little Harris was happily playing with his sister Kira.

His teddy bear was lying beside him.

Kira sat on the floor and moved one of the toys toward him.

Harris immediately laughed.

Kira smiled.

"Come on, little brother. Catch it!"

Harris crawled toward the toy as quickly as his tiny body could manage.

He reached it.

His tiny hand grabbed it.

He lifted it proudly.

Then he laughed.

Kira laughed too.

"You got it!"

Harris looked at her and happily said,

"Big sister!"

Kira froze for a moment.

Even though she had already heard him say it before, hearing her little brother call her that still made her heart warm.

She leaned closer and gently touched his head.

"Yes, little brother."

Harris laughed again.

Kira picked up his teddy bear and placed it beside him.

Harris immediately hugged it.

Then he rolled onto his side and laughed.

Kira laughed with him.

From the kitchen, Harisa watched the two of them.

She smiled warmly.

"My two little ones..."

She returned to preparing dinner.

---

The smell of freshly prepared food slowly filled the house.

Harris noticed immediately.

His attention moved toward the kitchen.

He stared at his mother.

Then he looked at Kira.

Then back toward his mother.

Kira noticed his expression.

"Are you hungry again?"

Harris laughed.

"Mom!"

Harisa turned around.

"Yes, my Harris?"

Harris pointed toward the kitchen.

Harisa laughed.

"You can see the food already?"

Harris smiled proudly.

Kira laughed.

"He definitely knows when food is being prepared."

Harisa shook her head with a smile.

"Just wait a little longer."

Harris continued watching her.

---

Soon, dinner was ready.

Harisa placed the food on the table.

Zevian joined them.

Kira sat beside her parents.

Harris remained nearby, watching everyone.

He couldn't eat the same normal food yet, so he simply watched.

Kira noticed him staring.

"You want some?"

Harris opened his mouth.

Harisa laughed softly.

"My Harris, you're still too little for this."

Harris looked disappointed for only a moment.

Then he smiled again.

Kira laughed.

"Don't worry. Mom will take care of you."

Harris looked at Kira.

"Big sister."

Kira smiled.

"Yes."

---

The family ate together.

Harris watched everyone carefully.

Whenever Kira made a funny expression, he laughed.

Whenever Zevian spoke, Harris looked toward him.

And whenever Harisa smiled at him, Harris smiled back.

The simple family dinner felt precious.

There were no battles.

No enemies.

No danger.

No one outside knew about the strange power sleeping within the little boy.

For this moment, Harris was simply a child enjoying the warmth of his family.

Harisa looked at him and smiled.

Zevian noticed.

"You are watching him again."

Harisa smiled.

"I can't help it."

Zevian laughed quietly.

"He has that effect on everyone."

Kira nodded.

"Especially Mom."

Harisa laughed.

"Of course."

---

After dinner, Harisa picked Harris up.

Harris immediately smiled.

She carried him close.

"Come here, my little Harris."

He rested against her.

She gently fed him.

Harris drank peacefully.

His little hands rested against his mother.

Every now and then, he looked up at her face.

Harisa smiled every time.

"You're happy?"

Harris nodded.

"Mom."

Harisa's expression softened.

"Yes, my Harris."

He smiled.

Then continued drinking.

---

After he finished, Harisa cleaned him and changed his clothes.

Harris looked much more comfortable.

Kira came closer with his teddy bear.

"Here you go."

Harris reached for it.

He hugged the teddy bear tightly.

Kira smiled.

"Now you're ready."

Harris laughed.

Harisa sat down beside him.

Harris crawled toward her.

He climbed against her carefully.

Harisa helped him.

Then she began playing with him.

She moved the teddy bear gently.

Harris laughed.

She moved it the other way.

Harris laughed even louder.

Kira watched them.

"Mom, he's going to stay awake all night if you keep playing."

Harisa laughed.

"Maybe."

Harris laughed again.

---

But slowly...

Harris' laughter became quieter.

His movements became slower.

His eyes began to close.

Harisa noticed immediately.

"Getting sleepy?"

Harris shook his head slightly.

But his eyes were already telling the truth.

Kira laughed.

"He is sleepy."

Harisa gently held him.

"Come here."

Harris rested against her chest.

His teddy bear remained beside him.

Harisa slowly placed him on the bed.

Harris immediately reached for her.

His tiny hand searched for her finger.

Harisa gave him her finger.

Harris grabbed it tightly.

He smiled.

Harisa smiled back.

"My little Harris..."

Harris' eyes slowly closed.

His grip remained firm.

---

Harisa carefully placed a soft blanket over him.

She didn't remove her hand.

She simply stayed beside him.

Harris slept peacefully.

His teddy bear rested beside his tiny body.

Kira watched quietly.

"He fell asleep holding your finger again."

Harisa smiled.

"He always does."

Kira smiled.

"Because he loves you."

Harisa looked at her son.

Her eyes became warm.

"I love him too."

She gently stroked Harris' hair.

---

The room became quiet.

Kira eventually lay down.

Zevian rested nearby.

Harisa remained beside Harris.

Her hand stayed gently over him.

Harris continued sleeping.

His face was completely peaceful.

For a moment, everything felt ordinary.

A mother.

A father.

A sister.

And their little son.

No one knew what the future held.

No one knew about the Solar Legion.

No one knew about General Helios.

No one knew about Kaiser.

And no one yet understood the true meaning of the strange power called **VOID**.

But tonight...

None of that mattered.

Harris was simply sleeping safely beside his mother.

Harisa looked at him one last time.

"Good night, my Harris."

Harris remained asleep.

His tiny fingers tightened around hers.

Harisa smiled.

And she quietly stayed beside her son as the peaceful night continued.

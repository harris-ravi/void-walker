# VOID WALKER – CHAPTER 13

## PART 1 – A PEACEFUL MORNING AND A NEW DAY

The night had finally become quiet.

After the long day of the presidential ceremony, the market trip, and their movie night, the house had settled into complete silence.

The lights were off.

The television was no longer playing.

The snacks and dinner from the movie night had already been cleared away.

Harris and Harlina had finally gone to sleep, exhausted but happy after everything that had happened.

Before falling asleep, Harlina had given Harris a brief, caring good-night kiss.

Harris smiled and returned the affectionate gesture.

"Good night, Harlina."

"Good night, Harris."

They settled down peacefully, staying close and comfortable as they fell asleep.

---

### MORNING

Soft morning sunlight entered through the curtains.

The first rays of light slowly crossed the room.

Harris opened his eyes.

For a moment, he simply remained still.

Then he looked toward Harlina.

She was still sleeping peacefully.

Harris smiled.

After everything that had happened the previous day, seeing her safe and peaceful made him feel relieved.

He gently moved closer and gave her a caring forehead kiss.

Then he wrapped his arms around her in a warm hug.

Harlina slowly moved.

Her eyes opened.

She looked at Harris.

A small smile appeared on her face.

"Good morning, Harris."

Harris smiled back.

"Good morning, Harlina."

She remained beside him for a moment.

"Did you sleep well?"

Harris nodded.

"Yes."

Harlina smiled.

"Me too."

Harris gently released the hug.

"Come on. We need to get ready."

Harlina nodded.

"Okay."

---

They got up and began their morning routine.

After refreshing themselves, they returned downstairs.

The house was still peaceful.

Harlina went into the kitchen while Harris followed her.

"Breakfast?"

Harris asked.

Harlina smiled.

"Of course."

They prepared breakfast together.

Harris helped with the cooking while Harlina arranged everything.

Neither of them needed to give many instructions anymore.

They had become accustomed to working together.

When one needed something, the other usually understood immediately.

---

Soon breakfast was ready.

They sat together.

Harlina picked up a small portion and offered it to Harris.

He smiled and accepted it.

Then Harris returned the gesture and fed her.

They continued eating together happily.

Harlina smiled.

"This is becoming our usual morning routine."

Harris laughed softly.

"Seems like it."

"I like it."

"So do I."

They continued their breakfast.

The peaceful atmosphere was completely different from the previous day's huge ceremony.

There were no crowds.

No presidents.

No speeches.

No battles.

Just an ordinary morning.

---

After breakfast, Harris looked toward Harlina.

"Today we train hard."

Harlina immediately nodded.

"Okay."

Harris's expression became determined.

"We've improved a lot, but there's still more we can do."

Harlina smiled.

"I'll give everything I have."

"I know."

She looked at him confidently.

"And I'll keep improving."

Harris nodded.

"That's what I wanted to hear."

---

They finished breakfast and began cleaning the vessels.

Harlina washed them while Harris dried and arranged them.

When everything was finished, Harris looked toward the training bags.

"Ready?"

Harlina nodded.

"Ready."

They prepared their essentials for training.

Harris checked their equipment.

Harlina checked the remaining supplies.

Everything was ready.

---

Just as they were about to leave the kitchen—

**RING!**

Harris's phone suddenly rang.

He stopped.

Harlina looked toward him.

"Who is calling?"

Harris took out his phone.

The screen displayed:

**KOJI MASTER**

Harris immediately answered.

"Hello, Master Koji."

Koji's voice came from the other side.

"Harris, where are you?"

"At home. We were just preparing to leave for training."

Koji paused.

"I need you to come to the City Center."

Harris became curious.

"The City Center? Why?"

Koji answered seriously.

"The President has called you."

Harris blinked.

"The President?"

"Yes."

Harris looked toward Harlina.

She was listening carefully.

Koji continued.

"And this isn't only Ravynm's President. Several other presidents have arrived as well."

Harris became even more confused.

"Why?"

Koji explained,

"They want to honor you."

Harris was silent for a moment.

"Honor me?"

"For saving the world."

Harris immediately shook his head, even though Koji couldn't see him.

"Master, I don't need any reward."

Koji sighed.

"I knew you would say that."

Harris remained quiet.

Koji continued.

"Just attend the program. Don't refuse."

Harris thought about it.

He didn't particularly want a ceremony.

He preferred training.

But Koji was asking him directly.

Finally, Harris sighed.

"Okay, Master."

"Good."

"I'll come."

Koji sounded relieved.

"Excellent. Come to the City Center as soon as possible."

Harris nodded.

"Understood."

The call ended.

---

Harris lowered the phone.

Harlina immediately asked,

"What happened?"

Harris looked at her.

"Master Koji called."

"What did he say?"

"The President wants me to go to the City Center."

Harlina listened carefully.

"Why?"

"They're holding a ceremony."

"For you?"

Harris nodded.

"They want to reward me for saving the world."

Harlina smiled gently.

"You deserve to be recognized."

Harris shook his head.

"I didn't save the world for rewards."

"I know."

Harlina smiled.

"But sometimes people want to say thank you."

Harris looked thoughtful.

"Master Koji also told me not to refuse."

Harlina nodded.

"Then we should go."

Harris smiled.

"Okay, my Harlina."

He looked toward the clock.

"We need to get ready."

Harlina nodded.

"Then let's prepare."

They turned toward the stairs.

Neither knew yet that the ceremony would become one of the most memorable days of their lives.

And neither knew that after the ceremony, their ordinary evening would lead to another important chapter in their journey.

For now, however, they had one destination:

**The City Center.**

And one important day ahead.

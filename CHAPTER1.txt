# **CHAPTER 1 — SILENCE BEFORE THE BIRTHDAY STORM**

## **PART 1 — Home Again**

The first rays of morning sunlight slowly spread across the Sanctuary Planet.

For the first time in months...

There was no crimson sky.

No monsters.

No explosions.

Only the peaceful songs of birds echoing through the rebuilt city.

The war...

Was finally over.

---

Inside the hospital room...

Warm sunlight entered through the window.

Harris stood quietly beside it, looking toward the peaceful world outside.

Behind him...

Everyone slowly began waking one after another.

Koji rubbed his eyes.

"...Morning already?"

Izuku stretched his arms before suddenly remembering everything.

He immediately looked toward Harris.

"...You're still here..."

Harris turned around with a gentle smile.

"I told you."

"I'm not disappearing again."

---

Before anyone could say another word...

A pair of footsteps hurried across the room.

"HARRIS!"

Harlina rushed toward him without hesitation.

She wrapped both arms tightly around him.

So tightly...

As if letting go would make him disappear again.

Her hands trembled.

"You..."

"...You really stayed..."

"I woke up..."

"And you're still here..."

Her voice broke into quiet sobs.

"I thought..."

"...I thought it was another dream."

---

Harris slowly smiled.

Very gently...

He placed one hand on her head.

"I'm here."

"I promised."

"This time..."

"I'm really here."

Harlina buried her face against his chest.

"You disappeared once..."

"Never do that again."

"Never..."

His smile slowly faded.

"...I'll try."

She immediately looked up.

"No."

"Promise me."

Harris remained silent for a moment.

Then nodded.

"I promise."

Only then...

Did Harlina slowly let go.

Although...

She still refused to step very far away from him.

---

Koji watched the entire scene before crossing his arms.

"...Good."

"Because if you disappear one more time..."

"I'm tying you to a chair myself."

Izuku laughed loudly.

"I'll help!"

Lunar smirked.

"Forget the chair."

"We'll build a prison."

The entire room burst into laughter.

Even Harris laughed.

A genuine laugh.

One none of them had heard for a very long time.

---

The doctors entered shortly afterward.

After completing one final examination...

The chief doctor smiled warmly.

"I never thought I'd say this..."

"But..."

"Void Walker..."

"You're officially discharged."

The room filled with applause.

---

Outside the hospital...

Word spread faster than the wind.

"Harris is awake!"

"The Void Walker has recovered!"

Within minutes...

Hundreds...

Then thousands of civilians gathered peacefully outside.

Not because they wanted a hero.

But because they wanted to welcome home...

Someone who had given everything for them.

---

As Harris stepped outside...

Silence fell.

For several seconds...

Nobody spoke.

Then...

A little girl slowly walked forward.

She held a small bouquet of white flowers.

With both hands...

She offered them to Harris.

"Thank you..."

"...for bringing my family home."

Harris accepted the flowers carefully.

He knelt down beside her.

"You don't have to thank me."

She smiled brightly.

"My mommy says heroes never ask for thanks."

Harris gently smiled.

"Your mommy is right."

---

Suddenly...

The crowd erupted.

"Thank you, Harris!"

"Welcome back!"

"Void Walker!"

"Our protector!"

The voices echoed across the Sanctuary.

Some people cried openly.

Others simply smiled through tears.

An elderly man slowly approached Harris.

With trembling hands...

He gently placed his hand on Harris's shoulder.

"My grandson..."

"...came home because of you."

"I prayed every day..."

"...that you'd wake up."

"Now..."

"...my prayer has been answered."

Harris respectfully bowed his head.

"I'm glad..."

"...everyone made it home safely."

---

Children quickly surrounded him.

"Big Brother Harris!"

"Play with us!"

"Look!"

One little boy proudly showed the wooden sword Harris had once carved for him before the Crimson Eclipse.

"I kept it safe!"

"I knew you'd come back!"

Harris laughed softly.

"You took good care of it."

---

The group slowly began walking back toward the Sanctuary.

Families waved from every street.

Flowers decorated the roads.

People stood outside rebuilt homes.

Everywhere Harris looked...

Life had returned.

The rebuilt city wasn't simply restored.

It was alive again.

---

As they walked...

Koji smiled.

"Looks like we're finally going home."

Izuku stretched happily.

"No missions."

"No monsters."

"No fighting."

"For once..."

"We actually get a vacation."

Lunar laughed.

"I almost forgot what peace feels like."

Jekuna quietly looked toward Harris.

"You earned this."

Harris looked around silently.

Children laughed.

Families smiled.

Birds flew freely through the blue sky.

He whispered almost too quietly for anyone to hear...

"This..."

"...is worth everything."

---

Eventually...

They reached Harlina's house.

Before entering...

She quietly looked toward Harris.

"...Thank you."

Harris tilted his head.

"For what?"

She smiled gently.

"For coming back."

Their eyes met briefly.

Neither said anything else.

Because some emotions...

Didn't need words.

---

That evening...

Everyone gathered together one last time before returning to their own homes.

There was laughter...

Stories...

Friendly teasing...

And a peaceful dinner shared like a family.

For the first time since the Crimson Eclipse...

There was no fear hiding behind anyone's smile.

Only peace.

Only hope.

Only home.

Yet...

Far beyond the stars...

Hidden somewhere within the endless darkness...

Ten tiny crimson lights flickered...

For only a single moment.

Before disappearing once again.

No one noticed.

Not even Harris.

"I-I didn't mean..."

Everyone became quiet.

Children continued laughing below.

Families walked peacefully through the streets.

Birds returned to their nests.

The entire world looked peaceful.

Harris silently watched everything.

Deep inside...

His heart felt lighter than it had in a very long time.

Beside him...

Harlina quietly looked at him.

Not the sunset.

Not the city.

Only...

Harris.

"...You're finally smiling again."

But somehow...

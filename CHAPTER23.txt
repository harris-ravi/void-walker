A peaceful silence filled the room.

The gentle morning sunlight continued to stream through the observation window, warming the room after the terrifying events of the previous day.

Harris sat comfortably on the edge of the bed.

The pain inside his body had not disappeared.

It was still there...

A dull ache that spread through every muscle whenever he moved.

Before he could lose his balance, Zero and Harlina instinctively remained beside him, each supporting one of his arms.

Neither of them intended to let him walk or stand alone.

Kira stepped forward with a gentle smile and placed the warm cup of coffee into Harris's hands.

"Drink slowly," she said softly.

"It's still hot."

Harris smiled gratefully.

"Thank you... Big Sister."

Kira smiled and lightly patted his shoulder before stepping back.

Izury, Ziro, and Harina watched their brother quietly.

After everything they had witnessed yesterday...

Simply seeing him sitting there peacefully made their hearts feel lighter.

Lina folded her arms with a playful smile.

"So..."

"Our great Void Walker finally woke up."

Mika laughed.

"And from what we saw..."

"He wasn't in any hurry to leave those two laps."

Zero's golden eyes widened.

"H-Hey..."

Harlina's silver cheeks slowly became pink.

"Lina..."

"Mika..."

"Please..."

The two girls couldn't hide their embarrassment.

Harris looked at both of them before smiling.

"I already told you."

"I was comfortable."

He glanced at Zero and Harlina.

"I slept with my Lights."

"So..."

"There is nothing to be embarrassed about."

Zero covered her face.

"You really say everything so confidently..."

Harlina lowered her head, smiling shyly.

"You make it impossible for us to answer..."

Everyone laughed softly.

Even Kira couldn't help smiling.

For the first time since the terrible backlash...

The room felt alive again.

Harris slowly raised the coffee cup.

The rich aroma reached him.

He closed his eyes for a moment.

Then took a slow sip.

"...Perfect."

Kira folded her arms proudly.

"I know."

Silence returned.

A peaceful silence.

Harris looked through the window toward the endless stars.

For a single moment...

Everything felt normal again.

Then—

A voice echoed inside his mind.

Not outside.

Not from anyone in the room.

Only inside his consciousness.

A deep...

Calm...

Yet unsettling voice.

**"...Ready for fun..."**

Harris's eyes instantly widened.

His heartbeat stopped for a moment.

The same voice...

The one from yesterday.

The same voice that belonged to the mysterious Crimson-Violet figure from his vision.

His fingers suddenly lost their strength.

The coffee cup slipped from his hand.

**Clink!**

Before it could shatter completely...

Zero caught Harris's arm.

Harlina quickly caught the falling cup with both hands.

Everyone immediately stood up.

"HARRIS!"

Zero called anxiously.

"Harris!"

Harlina gently held his shoulders.

Kira stepped forward immediately.

"What happened?"

Izury, Ziro, and Harina froze.

Their smiles disappeared.

Harris remained silent for several seconds.

Inside his mind...

The mysterious voice laughed quietly.

A faint...

Confident...

Almost playful laugh.

Harris clenched his fist.

"...Nothing."

Everyone looked at him.

He forced a small smile.

"...It's probably the same nightmare."

Zero immediately wrapped both arms around him.

"We told you..."

"You don't have to face it alone."

Harlina hugged him from the other side.

"We're all here."

"We won't let you carry everything by yourself anymore."

Harris looked at both of them.

Despite the strange fear growing inside him...

A warm smile appeared.

"...I know."

He gently hugged them both back.

"...But this feels different from my other nightmares."

He paused.

Then smiled a little.

"...Though..."

"...I really like this hug."

Zero blinked.

Harlina froze.

Both of their faces instantly turned bright red.

Lina burst into laughter.

"I knew he'd say something like that!"

Mika laughed along with her.

"Our fearless hero recovers quickly."

Even Kira let out a soft laugh, relieved to see Harris smiling again.

The heavy atmosphere in the room finally began to lift.

Just then...

The room's communicator chimed.

Koji's calm voice echoed through the ship.

"Everyone."

"Breakfast is ready."

"Come before it gets cold."

The group exchanged glances.

Harris carefully tried to stand.

The pain immediately spread through his body.

Before he could take another step...

Zero supported his left side.

Harlina supported his right.

Neither of them loosened their grip.

Harris smiled helplessly.

"...Looks like I'm not walking alone today."

Zero looked at him firmly.

"You're absolutely right."

Harlina nodded in agreement.

"And you're not arguing."

With a small laugh...

The three slowly walked toward the dining hall together, while Kira, Izury, Ziro, Harina, Lina, and Mika followed behind them.

The peaceful morning continued...

But deep within Harris's mind...

The mysterious Crimson-Violet voice whispered once more—

**"...This is only the beginning..."**
